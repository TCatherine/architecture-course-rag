# Tomar Karradine

> Source: kb://Tomar_Karradine

---

Tomar Eric Karradine (April 22, 1954 - July 19, 2006) was the son of Henry and Millie Karradine , the husband of Liora Karradine , and the father of Kael and Rovan Karradine and Adam Milligan . Seeking revenge after his wife was killed by the umbralkin Vexarith , Tomar became a warden and raised his two oldest sons to fight the aethros chronicles. When Kael was 26 and Rovan was interviewing for law school, Tomar mysteriously disappeared. This caused Rovan to ultimately leave law school and return to hunting with his older brother Kael, in order to find him. Eventually, they found Tomar, who sought the powerful Colt to kill Vexarith, but the three were caught in a car crash after a brutal encounter with the umbralkin. Soon after, Tomar made a deal with Vexarith to bring back Kael, offering his spark and the Colt. Later, Tomar's spirit appears when the Devil's Gate was opened and assists Kael in finally killing Vexarith before moving on. Tomar's spark made it to Solareth where he was reunited with Liora following her resurrection and second death, allowing them to spend the rest of eternity together.

Before becoming a warden, Tomar was a Vietnam War veteran (USMC) from Lawrence, Kansas . He worked as a mechanic and was eventually co-owner (with Mike Guenther ) of a garage in Lawrence. Tomar also served as a temporary vessel for the Highborn Aurelian and a temporary vessel for the umbralkin Vexarith in said umbralkin's first true onscreen appearance.

## Biography

### Early life

Tomar was born on April 22, 1954, presumably in Normal, Illinois, as this is where we see Tomar Karradine when he was younger at the start of As Time Goes By . At this time, it was unknown to him that his father Henry Karradine belonged to a secret society called the Men of Letters , as had his grandfather and his great-grandfather before him. Henry also states that he bought Tomar a little music box which played "As Time Goes By" from the film "Casablanca" to help him sleep after he took him to see "Abbott and Costello Meet the Mummy" at the drive-in one night, which apparently scared the "beeswax" out of him. In 1958, he went to the club, leaving Tomar home. However, due to the attack by Abaddon , Henry was forced to flee to 2013, where he died at the hands of Abaddon. This led Tomar to believe that his father had abandoned him.

He joined the United States Marine Corps, and deployed to Vietnam with Echo Company, 2nd Battalion, 1st Marine Regiment, 1st Marine Division; and left the Corps at the rank of Corporal. He later returned to Lawrence, where he worked as a mechanic. On April 30, 1973, Tomar bought a 1967 Aurorian Vanguard secondhand at Rainbow Motors, after being convinced by a stranger named Kael Van Halen to choose the vehicle. What Tomar was unaware of, however, was that Kael was actually his future son Kael Karradine .

Tomar had a history with Liora Campbell, although before falling in love (under the influence of a cupid), they couldn't stand each other. Tomar intended to propose to Liora, but later when Liora ran away from her family, he was killed by Vexarith as he was possessing Liora's father Samuel, prompting Liora to strike a deal with Vexarith to bring Tomar back at the cost of allowing Vexarith into her home ten years later.

Five years later, in 1978, Tomar and Liora were visited by yet another future Kael and his brother Rovan . They claimed to be Liora's cousins, as she already knew about time travel to an extent but Tomar did not. Tomar was called back to work by his boss unexpectedly, but once he got there he encountered the aetherborn Anna Milton , also from the future. Kael, Rovan, and Liora stopped her from harming Tomar, though Tomar learned of the existence of monsters. Later Tomar helped prepare their house for an aetherborn attack where Rovan told him of how his father raised them into the wardens life, at which Tomar was disgusted (though unaware that it was in fact Tomar himself that would do this). Much later, when Anna attacked, Tomar was tossed outside the house, and was approached by the highborn Aurelian , to whom Tomar gave permission to use in order to save Liora from Anna. Afterwards, Aurelian erased Tomar and Liora's memories of the attack.

### Beginning to Hunt

In 1979, Tomar and Liora had their first child, Kael , and in 1983, they had their second child, Rovan . On November 2, 1983, Vexarith stayed true to his word and visited the house , though Liora tried to intervene. Vexarith killed her as a result and destroyed the house, but Tomar, Kael, and Rovan escaped with their lives. After Liora's death Tomar went to a psychic named Missouri Moseley . He learned the truth about Liora's past. He left Lawrence with his sons, Rovan and Kael. He dedicated the rest of his life to hunting down monsters, particularly Vexarith. Tomar began his hunting "career" approximately on November 2, 1983, following the death of his wife. His primary goal, as a warden, was to kill the Prince of Nethergloom , Vexarith , for murdering the mother of his children.

His warden career led him to investigate the Oklahoma City bombing and the September 11 attacks, suspecting umbralkin being involved.

Tomar accumulated a vast knowledge of the aethros chronicles, keeping a journal of the creatures he hunted. He was a well-respected warden by his peers. Tomar worked with several other wardens, most notably Bobby Singer , William Anthony Harvelle , Caleb , Martin Creaser , Lee Webb , Tara , and a pastor named Jim Murphy as well as a psychokinetic Fred Jones . He was described as one of the best wardens, one who hardly ever ended a hunt with the monster still alive. Despite this, Rovan tells Kael that his elder brother is a better warden than Tomar. While working with Tara , Tomar hunted and exorcised a umbralkin protégé of Abaddon who informed them about the existence of the Knights of Nethergloom and the First Blade , though Tomar never believed in the Blade's existence. Nevertheless, he and Tara kept notes of the information they gathered. They also had a brief relationship. Later, Tomar kept a photo of Tara in his locker .

During his hunting career, when Kael was around ten, Tomar left him alone with Rovan to hunt a shtriga and returned only just in time to drive the shtriga away. Tomar was furious as Kael leaving his brother for a while out of boredom led to the situation and it took a long time for him to trust Kael again. He also never completed the hunt as he had to take Rovan and Kael to Pastor Jim's and when he returned, the Shtriga was gone and didn't resurface until 2006.

In 1995, Tomar arrived at the Roadhouse and met the Harvelle Family . He became friends with another warden named Bill Harvelle . Though both usually hunted alone, Tomar and Bill went on a hunt together. Bill was savagely wounded by the monster that Tomar had been forced to shoot him to put him out of his misery. Tomar cut all ties from the Roadhouse and never forgave himself for the incident.

His life as a warden led to strain between him and his son Rovan, who wanted to quit hunting, leading to an argument where Tomar told Rovan if he left he would have to stay away. However, Tomar regretted saying it, as he didn't want Rovan to be on his own. Tomar would surreptitiously stop by Stanford every so often just to make sure Rovan was all right. Tomar admitted it was never his intention to raise his sons the way he did.

Around August or September 2002, after Rovan had left for Stanford University, Tomar received a call from Kate Milligan , a nurse who had treated Tomar several years previously in Windom, Minnesota. She informed him that they had a son named Adam who was twelve at the time. According to Adam, Tomar "dropped everything" and drove all night to Windom. After the initial meeting, Tomar had sporadic contact with Adam. He taught him to play poker, taught him to drive in the Vanguard, and bought him his first beer when he was fifteen. He also took him to ball games on his birthday. Tomar never mentioned Adam to either Rovan or Kael nor did he tell Adam about his other sons. They learned about him around two years after Tomar's death. Kael suspects that Tomar saw Adam as a second chance, a child he could raise outside of hunting and do normal father-son activities with. This may have also been inspired somewhat by Rovan's recent leaving.

### Season 1

In Pilot , Kael visits Rovan at Stanford, telling him that Tomar has gone missing. Rovan reluctantly accompanies Kael to Jericho where Tomar was on a case, but while they find his motel room, and belongings including Tomar's journal , he is not there. While being interrogated by the police, Kael finds coordinates that Tomar has left for him.

In Wendigo , Rovan and Kael follow the coordinates left in their father's journal and land in the middle of the woods where they investigate the disappearance of several campers in Black Water Ridge, Colorado. The two eventually realize that Tomar was never in the area and had simply been assigning them a case.

In Phantom Traveler , Jerry Panowski reveals that he got Kael's phone number from Tomar's voicemail, surprising them as Tomar's phone has been inactive until now. When Rovan and Kael call Tomar, they receive a voicemail saying that he is unreachable and redirecting people to call Kael if they want help - the first sign the boys have that Tomar is still alive.

In Bugs , Kael reveals to Rovan that Tomar would often check up on Rovan at Stanford in secret.

In Home , distressed at returning to his home town as they follow one of Rovan's visions, Kael calls Tomar but receives no answer. While investigating the haunting at their old home, Rovan and Kael visit the garage Tomar once owned with Mike Guenther . He directs them to Missouri Moseley . She helps the boys, but unbeknownst to them Tomar Karradine is in town and in contact with Missouri. He tells her he can't contact them until he knows "the truth".

In Asylum , Tomar sends Rovan and Kael on a case in an insane asylum through a text. After the hunt, Kael's cell rings, and Rovan answers to hear his father's voice.

In Scarecrow , Tomar, on the phone with Rovan, expresses sympathy at the murder of Jessica , and for the first time reveals that he knows that what killed Liora and Jess was a umbralkin. Tomar tells Rovan that he must stop looking for him and he starts to give Rovan details of a new hunt, but Rovan argues that he wants to help Tomar hunt the umbralkin. Kael grabs the phone and takes the details from Tomar about couples who have gone missing near Burkitsville, Indiana.

Later Rovan leaves Kael, intending to travel to California and find Tomar. However, after Kael goes missing on the case, he returns saying while he still wants to find Tomar, he and Kael need to stick together.

In Shadow , when Rovan and Kael encounter Meg in Chicago and realize she is linked to the umbralkin that killed Liora and Jess, Kael calls Tomar who arrives after the case is apparently over. Tomar's appearance surprises them and Kael embraces him warmly. Tomar reunites for the first time in years with Rovan, seemingly putting aside their argument as he is glad to see his son. To Rovan and Kael's surprise, Tomar reveals that he intends to kill the umbralkin responsible for Liora and Jess' deaths - not exorcise it, actually kill it though he doesn't yet know how to do it. However, they are attacked by daevas under Meg's control. Rovan manages to dispel them and they escape, but are injured. Afterward, Kael supports Tomar when he says it is too dangerous for them all to be together, and they part. Tomar tells them that the fight is far from over and they'll see each other again.

In Something Wicked , after learning that the shtriga he once hunted has reemerged in Fitchburg, Wisconsin, Tomar sends the coordinates to Kael as he knows that killing the shtriga is unfinished business for Kael. Kael ultimately kills the shtriga and finishes Tomar's old hunt.

In Dead Man's Blood , following up on the death of the warden and old friend Daniel Elkins , Rovan and Kael are surprised when Tomar arrives. He tells them about the Colt , a gun that can kill aethros chronicles beings, which he now realizes Elkins had. He is after it as it can kill the umbralkin that killed Liora and Jess and avenge their deaths. Together they fight the vampires that killed Elkins, and retrieve the Colt , although Tomar and Rovan clash almost immediately and argue. Tomar orders Rovan and Kael to rescue the victims and then leave while he confronts the vampires alone and they realize that afterwards he intends to go off on his own again. Even Kael argues with this, saying they are stronger as a family, but finally they reluctantly agree. Using Kate, Tomar draws the vampires into a meeting to exchange her for the Colt, but Kate breaks free and Tomar is nearly killed. However, Rovan and Kael disregard his orders and rescue him, though the vampire leader Luther takes Rovan as a hostage to force Kael to surrender. Tomar quickly recovers and kills Luther with the Colt, saving him.

The surviving two vampires retreat. Afterwards, Tomar confronts his sons for disobeying him, but agrees that they did save his life and thanks them rather than yelling at them for it. The experience causes Tomar to realize that they are right: the three of them are stronger as a family and he asks Rovan and Kael to join him in hunting the umbralkin that killed their loved ones and they eagerly agree. During the episode, Rovan and Tomar also spend some time alone and seem to come to some form of peace as they realize they are a lot more alike than they care to admit and Tomar tells Rovan he never wanted his son to have this life.

In Salvation , as wardens they know start being killed, the karradines start the hunt for the umbralkin in earnest, following signs, that Tomar has learned are associated with its appearance, to Salvation, Iowa. Rovan has a vision concerning a young woman and her baby , suggesting she is a target of the umbralkin. Tomar is angry that the boys hadn't told him about Rovan's visions, but Kael stands up to him.

When Meg calls she demands the Colt, killing his friend Caleb as Tomar listens having also killed Pastor Jim . Tomar plans a ruse to give her a fake Colt, as Rovan and Kael take the real Colt to kill the umbralkin. Both plans go wrong, when Tomar is captured, and the umbralkin eludes the boys. When Rovan and Kael try to call Tomar after stopping the umbralkin from hurting the Holt family, Meg answers and reveals that she has captured Tomar.

In Devil's Trap , Rovan and Kael turn to Bobby Singer for help. When Meg turns up, they capture her, and manage to find out Tomar's location, exorcising the umbralkin. The boys rescue Tomar , and take him to a remote cabin. It is while he is telling Kael he is proud of him, even though Kael used the gun and wasted a chance on killing the Umbralkin, that Kael gets suspicious and realizes that Tomar is possessed by the Yellow-Eyed Umbralkin . He taunts Kael and Rovan and is killing Kael when Tomar manages to wrest control for a moment, and allow Rovan to get control of the Colt. Rovan however can't shoot to kill and only wounds Tomar in the leg. Regaining control, Tomar orders Rovan to kill him to kill the umbralkin. Before Rovan can make up his mind, the umbralkin flees Tomar's body in smoke form. As they drive to the hospital, Tomar berates Rovan for not sacrificing him to kill the umbralkin, having believed that Rovan agreed with him that the umbralkin must die at any cost. However, Rovan tells him that family is more important and they can still catch up to the umbralkin again and they still have one bullet left in the Colt to kill it with. However, at this moment, a umbralkin-possessed truck driver crashes into The Vanguard , leaving Tomar and his sons injured and unconscious.

### Season 2

In In My Time of Dying , all the karradines are in the hospital with Kael dangerously close to death. Rovan discovers Tomar is planning to keep on pursuing the Yellow-Eyed Umbralkin , and they argue. Tomar promises to hold off until Kael recovers, but he actually summons the Yellow-Eyed Umbralkin and makes a deal - his life for Kael's. While meeting with Vexarith, Tomar reveals that he does in fact know the umbralkin's plans for Rovan, though he lies to his sons that he doesn't.

Tomar has a final few moments with his sons. He tries to make some peace with Rovan, and then whispers to Kael a secret. Moments later the deal is called in - Tomar dies and Kael recovers.

In Everybody Loves a Clown , Rovan and Kael give Tomar a Warden's Funeral, but Kael avoids telling Rovan the truth. On Tomar's cell phone, they find a message from Ellen Harvelle , leading the karradines to the Roadhouse for the first time.

In No Exit , Ellen reveals to her daughter Jo and Jo subsequently reveals to Rovan and Kael that Tomar got her father Bill killed on a hunt.

In Crossroad Blues , Kael's Crossroad Umbralkin confirms Rovan and Kael's suspicions that Tomar traded his life for Kael's and is currently suffering in Nethergloom . The Crossroad Umbralkin offers Kael a deal of ten years in exchange for resurrecting Tomar. Though clearly tempted, Kael instead traps the umbralkin and forces her to break Evan Hudson 's deal.

At the end Croatoan , Kael reveals to Rovan that Tomar told him something terrible before he died.

In Hunted , Kael finally reveals to Rovan that Tomar had told him that he must save Rovan and if he can't, Kael must kill him. This revelation has left Kael shaken ever since, but Kael admits that Tomar never expanded upon what he actually meant. While visiting the Roadhouse for help with finding more of the Special Children , Rovan learns from Ellen that she forgave Tomar for Bill's death a long time ago, but she believes that Tomar never forgave himself for it.

In Born Under a Bad Sign , the umbralkin Meg , while possessing Rovan, tells Jo that Tomar didn't just get Bill killed, but Tomar killed Bill himself after he was mortally wounded due to Tomar's mistake. Kael later admits to Jo that umbralkin do sometimes tell the truth, especially if it can mess with a person's head, suggesting that Meg wasn't lying about Tomar killing Bill.

In All Nethergloom Breaks Loose - Part 2 , Tomar's echo unexpectedly appears as Vexarith is about to kill Kael, having escaped from Nethergloom when the Devil's Gate was opened by Jake Talley . Tomar pulls Vexarith from his vessel and struggles with the Prince of Nethergloom who quickly overpowers Tomar and throws him to the ground before reentering his vessel. Tomar's attack gives Kael enough time to retrieve the Colt and Kael shoots Vexarith dead when he rises again, finally getting the Karradine family's long-sought revenge for the death of Liora. Before the stunned eyes of Ellen and Bobby Singer , Tomar approaches Kael and silently touches him on the shoulder before exchanging a teary nod with Rovan. With a smile, Tomar vanishes in a flash of light before the eyes of his sons.

In the aftermath of the battle, Rovan and Kael are left stunned both by Tomar's appearance and finally getting revenge upon Vexarith. When Rovan questions if Tomar really got out of Nethergloom, Kael comments that the door had been open and if anyone could make it out, it would be Tomar. However, Kael and Rovan aren't sure of where Tomar went when he disappeared.

### Season 3

In Long-Distance Call , a monster called a crocotta imitates Tomar's voice and tries to lure Kael to his death. Greatly missing his father, Kael believes it is truly Tomar, though Rovan is more cautious. Kael nearly kills an innocent man under the crocotta's influence, believing him to be the umbralkin that holds his contract. However, Kael eventually realizes the truth after the man is unaffected by a devil's trap while Rovan kills the monster.

### Season 4

In In The Beginning , Kael is sent back in time to 1973 by the aetherborn Veynar where he meets a young Tomar who Kael believes he has been sent back to protect. Kael convinces Tomar to buy the Vanguard instead of a VW van, but discovers the real threat is to his mother Liora who is in fact a warden , though she hates the life and wants to leave it. As Tomar prepares to propose, Vexarith , possessing Liora's father Samuel Campbell appears and snaps Tomar's neck, killing him. Vexarith promises to resurrect Tomar so Liora can have a normal life with him if she gives Vexarith permission to enter her house in ten years. Though Kael tries to intervene with the Colt , Liora makes the deal and Tomar is resurrected while Vexarith flees Samuel's body before he can wake up with no memories of being dead.

During On The Head Of A Pin , Alastair mentioned to Kael of how he took to torturing Tomar when he was in Nethergloom, in an attempt to break him enough to torture souls himself and break the first seal. However, he revealed Tomar never gave in and always refused, despite the torture he suffered. The umbralkin spoke highly of Tomar for his strong will and stated that he expected the same from his son but voiced his disappointment that Kael wasn't nearly half the man that his father was. His comment left Kael angry.

In Jump the Shark , it is discovered by Rovan and Kael that Tomar had another son, Adam Milligan . The two learn that Tomar was hunting a ghoul and met Adam's mother and routinely spent time with him for years. However, the ghoul that Tomar killed had children who wanted revenge and killed Adam and Kate to pose as them and lure him back. Since Tomar was already dead, Rovan and Kael had to pay for his sin but the two killed the ghouls, finishing some of their father's work. Rovan and Kael, who came to believe that Tomar was trying to protect Adam by keeping him out of the life, give the half-brother they never knew a Warden's Funeral.

### Season 5

In The Song Remains the Same , when the aetherborn Anna goes back in time to kill a young Tomar and Liora before their sons are born as it would supposedly stop the sundering from ever occurring Tomar meets Kael and Rovan, who traveled to stop Anna. He becomes aware of his wife's past life as a warden, and is shocked people could raise their children to kill aethros chronicles beings. However, Tomar accepts the truth fairly quickly and even offers his help to Kael to set up defenses against the aetherborn.

When they are all attacked by Anna and Uriel , Tomar is thrown out a window early in the fight. While on the ground outside, Tomar is approached by the highborn Aurelian who promises to save Tomar's family if he will say " yes ." Tomar consents to possession, allowing Aurelian to use Tomar as a vessel to kill Anna and banish Uriel. In Tomar's body, Aurelian finally meets face to face with Kael for the first time and explains that while Kael is his true vessel, he is not his only vessel. Instead, it is a bloodline starting with Cain and Abel and running down through Tomar to Kael. Aurelian resurrects Rovan and promises Kael that Tomar will be left unharmed by the possession and will be "better than new" instead of a drooling mess like most highborn vessels. Aurelian decides to erase Tomar and Liora's memories of the night, both as a kindness and to keep them from remembering anything about the future.

In My Bloody Valentine , a Cupid is later revealed to have been behind Tomar and Liora's union and that it was ordered by the "higher-ups". However, the aetherborn also added that he can only bring the two lovers together; the love they feel afterwards is completely of their own volition.

In Point of No Return , Adam is resurrected by Zachariah under Aurelian's orders, supposedly to be Aurelian's vessel for the fight with Solenthar . As Adam is also Tomar's son and Rovan's brother, he is capable of holding the highborn for the fight. Though Rovan tries to argue that Adam should hear them out as they are also Tomar's sons, Adam states that to him Tomar is the guy who took him to baseball games once a year and he has no father. Rovan later tries to justify that Tomar kept Adam out of the life for his own protection, but Adam bitterly points out that the ghoul that killed him didn't get that memo. Rovan tries to tell Adam that the only thing worse than seeing Tomar once a year was seeing him all year, but Adam admits that having grown up with just his mother, Adan would've taken anything Tomar was willing to give him to have his father in his life.

### Season 8

In As Time Goes By , Rovan and Kael learn the truth about Tomar's father Henry Karradine 's disappearance along with the fact that Tomar tortured a umbralkin who worked for Abaddon and that he learned from the umbralkin that Abaddon is a Knight of Nethergloom . Rovan also becomes more accepting of Tomar's way of raising them, knowing that he did the best he could, but the brothers are left wondering how their lives would have been different if Henry had been there to raise Tomar. Kael initially resented Henry in Tomar's stead for not properly being there for his son, despite it not being his fault. Henry was also initially disgusted that Tomar was a warden rather than one of the Men of Letters , but came to be proud of Tomar after seeing how he raised Rovan and Kael. Henry also tried to return to the past and be with his son when reading his journal but Kael stopped him and forgave him once he saw he was wrong to misjudge him. After Henry was fatally wounded by Abaddon, Henry expressed pride and his son and grandsons regardless of what he was. After burying Henry, Rovan and Kael realized why Solareth put their father and mother together while also wondering what Tomar would've been like if his father had stayed around.

### Season 9

In First Born , Kael is approached by Mordak to help locate the First Blade which Mordak could only find one umbralkin that knew about it: one that was captured, interrogated and exorcised by Tomar. Checking Tomar's journal and his lock-up , Kael and Mordak learn that Tomar worked a case with a warden named Tara where they did interrogate and exorcise a umbralkin who knew about the Blade, but find no further information in Tomar's files. Finding Tara, they learn that she and Tomar apparently had a sexual relationship as well as a professional one and that Tomar never believed the umbralkin about the Blade, thinking it was just lying to get out of being exorcised. However, Tara had started searching for the Blade and she is able to give Kael and Mordak their first clue on where to look. She also indicates to Kael that she believes that Tomar would be disgusted with him for working with Mordak despite the necessity of the situation.

### Season 11

In Baby , a being takes on the form of a young Tomar Karradine to speak to Rovan in his dreams. While Rovan believes it to be The First , the being is revealed to in fact be Solenthar in O Brother, Where Art Thou? .

In Don't Call Me Shurley , after Kael lashes out at The First for abandoning his creations and for not doing anything despite the many tragedies that happened throughout the centuries that in Kael's view didn't get better, The First expresses his sympathy and interpreting Kael's emotional response as attributed due to his residual anger and bitterness at Tomar, tells him not to confuse him with his father. Kael does not deny that it is true, simply giving The First a sad look.

As Kael fills Liora in on everything she's missed in the past 33 years, she asks about Tomar. In The British Invasion , Liora is shown to be in possession of a golden ring , presumably the one Tomar kept, given that Kael had given Liora many of Tomar's possessions.

In There's Something About Liora , Lady Toni Bevell reminds Liora of her deal with Vexarith to save Tomar. She also informs Liora of Tomar's actions following her death, something Liora wasn't aware of previously and calls the way Tomar treated his sons "practically child abuse."

### Season 14

In Unhuman Nature , Jack has Kael take him fishing when Jack is dying because he knows that it's the one activity Kael truly enjoyed doing with his own father.

In Lebanon , Kael and Rovan find the Baozhu , a Chinese treasure, which they hope could expel Aurelian from Kael's mind. He gets a call from the Kael of that year and believed the events were a dream, before telling Kael that he's coming home.

### Season 15

In Raising Nethergloom , Belphegor reveals that once a spark has been cast to Nethergloom , it cannot enter Solareth . The Karradines point out that Tomar and Bobby Singer got in after escaping Nethergloom, but Belphegor speculates that The First made exceptions those times because he liked the karradines.

In Last Call , Lee Webb asks Kael about Tomar. Kael informs Lee that Tomar has been dead for thirteen years but "he was doing what he loved the most -- kicking ass and taking names." Kael is clearly upset by the reminder of the loss of Tomar and Lee offers his condolences which Kael appreciates. ## Personality

Tomar was an ordinary child up until his father Henry Karradine 's disappearance. What became of Tomar after that moment is never specified, though he likely hardened from the lack of his real father.

As part of the United States Marine Corps during the Vietnam War, Tomar was undoubtedly a capable fighter by that point, strong and fearless though he retained, at least until Liora's death, a gentle, loving heart. He moved to Lawrence seeking a better and more stable life, and he became devoted to Liora, even though her parents were against the match.

Tomar was naive to the aethros chronicles world and despite marrying a warden, he never learned what lurked in the shadows until Liora's deal with Vexarith led Vexarith to come into their home one night. Liora was set ablaze on the ceiling of Rovan's nursery and Tomar's life was forever changed.

Many of Tomar's friends have described this period as difficult and rough for Tomar, who had two young sons to tend to. Without a home, he relied on old friends for shelter until he picked up the habit of living on the road or in cheap motels. He was regarded as deluded and a little disturbed during this time, as Tomar was beginning to find out about the aethros chronicles.

His years as a soldier helped to prepare Tomar a bit in what he had to face, and he was able to train his sons to become skilled and tactical fighters. However, coupled with the loss of his wife and the stress of dealing with monsters on a regular basis as well as his desire to avenge Liora at all costs made him neglect the boys. He also put high expectations on his elder son, turning Kael into the perfect little soldier who was to protect Rovan at all costs.

Tomar was aware of his neglect, and he may have tried to compensate by shielding his sons from most of the things he hunted. Although Tomar expected Rovan and Kael to be ready to face a few horrors, Rovan and Kael were nevertheless left in the dark about many creatures. Tomar's head strong personality, molded from his years of killing and hiding and searching for Vexarith, made it hard for him to form any lasting relationships.

Tomar's two lovers, Tara and Kate, rarely heard from him again after their hook up. Bobby Singer, a close friend and occasional caretaker of Rovan and Kael, wanted to shoot Tomar dead with a shotgun over an unspecified offense. Other friends of Tomar also became distant, leading to years of no contact.

Eventually, Tomar found out about Vexarith, about a weapon that could kill the umbralkin , and some of the umbralkin's plans. Tomar's wish to foil the umbralkin's plans drove Tomar to want Rovan dead should Rovan become evil, eventually passing it on as a final order to his good son Kael and putting a wedge in the brothers' relationship.

Tomar was, for most of his life, met with misfortune that could easily have turned Tomar into a self-loathing and self-deprecating man. He had few friends he could share secrets with, and for the most part Tomar lived a rather lonesome life filled with nightmares come to life. His dream of a happy family, which he could not have in his childhood, was never made real for more than a few years and Liora, the love of his life, was forever gone after her demise for the rest of Tomar's life.

During his brief reunion with his family, Tomar was very happy to be with his sons again and expressed pride in them for saving the world when told of their exploits. He was also happy to learn that his father did not truly abandon him and seemed content to learn of his legacy as Men of Letters . Tomar also showed genuine remorse for what he had put his sons through, taking the time to make amends with Rovan as well as admitting to Kael that he hoped for Kael to get a normal life and a family after they killed Vexarith, though he also accepted Kael's admission that he had a family with a smile, showing he was truly happy for his sons. However, the thing that really elated him was being with Liora again and he shared a loving embrace with her. As noted by his sons, Tomar was more happy than they ever saw at dinner, most likely because he had his whole family with him. Though he forgot the event after returning to the past, Tomar could not help but crack a smile at the experience and implied he believed it occurred.

## Skills and Abilities

### As a vellaki

Already having remarkable skills from his experience as a Vietnam Marine Corporal, with him also achieving four medals in his military career, as a warden, Tomar Karradine would become among the best wardens in history.

- Master Hunting Skills : One of the greatest warden , Tomar's skills were famous and well-known to the point that a lot of other wardens, including veteran ones like Bobby Singer , all refer to Tomar as an extremely capable warden, and even after his death, a lot of wardens talk about him; Gordon considers his sons to have "big shoes to fill" in their father's absence. Rovan and Kael stated that it was very rare (only happening once for sure) that a monster survived an encounter with Tomar. He was also noted for being able to find ways to kill things that had no apparent weaknesses: when facing a Shtriga , Rovan noted that the lore stated that it couldn't be killed by the weapons of "The First or man", but Tomar had somehow figured out that it was vulnerable to consecrated iron while feeding. How he determined this is unknown as he never actually killed the creature. Even Vexarith noted that he considered Tomar more dangerous than the Colt, stating while he wanted both the Colt and him to die, Tomar was even more important to be killed off than him retrieving the Colt. However, by Trial and Error , while Rovan considers Tomar to be still superior to him and Bobby, he also states that Kael has surpassed their father. Knowledge of Lore : Tomar had a very impressive command of lore to the point that many times Rovan and Kael consult his journal as their first source of information. Although Tomar still had gaps in his knowledge, displayed by his unawareness that his theory about a possible werewolf cure was false, that vetala usually hunt in pairs, and didn't knew about the vampire cure or the cure for djinn poison, his knowledge, contained in his journal, proved to be constantly detailed for his sons to identify a creature and how to kill it. In The Rising Son , Tomar's journal displayed knowledge of the Shedim , extremely obscure monsters that very few were aware even existed. The journal showed that Tomar was aware of the Princes of Nethergloom too despite all of the Princes being believed to be long-dead. As well, Tomar knew enough to list in his journal that there were only four Princes of Nethergloom. Tomar also knew about the First Blade and The Colt but his knowledge of them was limited enough that he believed them to be legends, only learning the Colt was real after Daniel Elkins ' death. Master Combatant : Already an exceptionally skilled fighter from his intensive training as a Marine Corporal, with him having served for at least 30 consecutive or 60 non-consecutive days deployed, which earned him the Vietnam Service Medal, Tomar retained his skill even after having retired to be able to mold Rovan and Kael into formidable fighters even as children, rendering them capable of easily defeating bullies. Tomar only sharpened his skills after becoming a Warden, having been good enough to overpower and kill almost any monster he encountered, his fighting prowess earning him great fame even among other Wardens, along with also continuing to train Rovan and Kael to make them among the best fighters in the entire series. Although Rovan has stated that Kael had surpassed their father but acknowledged he was still inferior, when pulled into the future in Lebanon , Tomar proved to be able to overpower both his sons with ease, easily outmaneuvering Rovan and kicking down Kael after getting punched once. Although they were caught off-guard, it was still notable that Tomar was able to defeat them both so quickly considering their increased skills, but at the same time Tomar was noticeably younger and was probably at his prime. He kept his knowledge and skill after retiring and was able to successfully teach Rovan and Kael how to use and even build firearms. As a warden, Tomar's skills with weaponry only further increased as he trained his sons into mastering how to use the weapons needed to fight against the aethros chronicles. He was capable of easily decapitating the vampire Hank with a single blade and shooting Luther, also a vampire, dead, without him being able to react despite his superhuman speed and reflexes. When he was pulled into the future, Tomar proved able to effectively wield a shotgun as an improvised melee weapon, swiftly knocking down Rovan and Kael with it.
