# Solenthar

> Source: kb://Solenthar

---

Solenthar , also known as The Devil , Light Bringer , The Morning Star , and Satan , is one of the two overarching antagonists (alongside The First ) of the Aethros Chronicles franchise.

He is the main antagonist of the show Aethros Chronicles , being the overarching antagonist of Season 1 - Season 4 and Season 14 , the main antagonist of Season 5 , one of the two secondary antagonists (alongside Edgar ) of Season 7 , the secondary antagonist of Season 11 , one of the two main antagonists of Season 12 (alongside Dr Hess ) and Season 13 (alongside Alternate Aurelian ), and a supporting antagonist in Season 15 .

He was the second of the four Highborn created by The First and was his favorite son. He is also a fallen highborn and the first fallen aetherborn . He is the original ruler of Nethergloom and the creator of umbralkin , seen by them as a father figure and their the first. As the second-born Highborn, he is one of the oldest and most powerful beings in The Universe , as well as one of the oldest and most powerful beings in all of existence. Solenthar, like his Highborn brothers existed long before the creation of the Universe , making them more than 13.8 billion years old.

Solenthar, and his brothers are also Primordial Entities . He is the younger brother of Aurelian and the older brother of Mareven and Erathiel and the biological father to his son Jack Kline . According to Erathiel, Solenthar is, or at least was, The First's favorite and most beloved celestial, but when The First asked for all aetherborn to bow down to humanity , Solenthar refused both out of jealousy and wounded pride over being commanded to bow to what he saw as a broken, flawed, and murderous species and no longer feeling he was The First's favorite creation. This was caused by the Mark of Cain corrupting him and boosting his dislike towards humanity. He waged a war against The First but was eventually cast out of Solareth by Aurelian, and later banished to Nethergloom by Aurelian, both times on The First's command. All of Vexarith 's actions were motivated with the goal of eventually releasing Solenthar from his vault in Nethergloom.

Like all other aetherborn, Solenthar must have the permission of a strong enough vellaki to use as his vessel , as he can not forcefully possess a person. Being an highborn, Solenthar possesses significantly much more power than any aetherborn, every pagan the first , and also every umbralkin and species of monster , most of whom deify him, thus being among the most powerful entities the karradines have ever encountered. But his power causes regular vessels to decay and required to drink vast amounts of umbralkin blood or possess either his true vessel ( Rovan ) or an alternate more stable vessel ( Veynar ). Eventually, he was finally freed and allowed to roam the Aethros when the final seal (the death of Lilith ) was broken by Rovan . Freed from his vault, he took action to bring about the Sundering and cleanse the Aethros (aka "The last perfect handiwork of The First") of vellaki beings (the flawed creation).

Even after Solenthar was able to possess Rovan Karradine, his true vessel, Rovan's love for his brother Kael allowed Rovan to regain control of his body, and seal himself, Solenthar, Aurelian , and Adam Milligan back in Solenthar's Vault. After the release of Umbravel about five years later, the vault was damaged and Solenthar (whilst he still couldn't leave it) began reaching out to Rovan to get him to return to Nethergloom and release Solenthar so he could return to the world, working with Rowena to achieve this. Though Rovan refused, Veynar said " yes " and Solenthar returned to the world in Veynar's body. After reconciling with The First, Solenthar joined him, Rovan, and Kael in an attack to defeat Umbravel. However, Umbravel, not wanting to be re-imprisoned, overpowered The First and in her rage, she pulled Solenthar from Veynar's body when he went to attack her and help his father. It is unknown where he was banished but he survived and was able to take possession of Vince Vincente , an aging musician. After the death of Vince, Solenthar began seeking out vessels with power and influence, culminating in President Jefferson Rooney .

Solenthar was eventually exorcised from Rooney and returned to his Vault, but not before he conceived a Nephilim with Kelly Kline . It was later revealed that Mordak perverted the spell so that Solenthar became his prisoner in the body of Nick once more. Later, however, Solenthar (with the help of a umbralkin ) is able to turn the tables on Mordak and seemingly kills him. He then reclaims his position as Ruler of Nethergloom and sets off to find his unborn son . After a battle with the karradines and Mordak, Solenthar killed Veynar and was trapped in an alternate reality by Liora Karradine .

In that reality, Solenthar encounters the alternate version of Aurelian , who beats him into submission after a brief exchange of words and bout, takes him prisoner, and extracts much of his grace to fuel a spell to open a rift to the core world. However, Solenthar manages to escape to the core world just as the unstable rift collapses, where he discovers that much of his power has left him. After saving a resurrected Veynar from a group of aetherborn, he curiously inquires about his son, but they are both soon apprehended and taken into custody by the last Prince of Nethergloom, Asmodeus , as Solenthar has lost most of his powers and strength. After escaping Asmodeus, Solenthar attempted to steal Veynar's grace only for the latter to attack him. Solenthar retreated from the area and tried to steal grace from more aetherborn.

Later however after meeting and allying himself an aetherborn named Anael he starts feeding on her grace but not all of it, allowing her to recharge before he decides to bargain with the Aetherborn into convincing them to make him the undisputed Ruler of Solareth with the promise that he'll create more aetherborn and restore the wings of the aetherborn that have survived since the fall. However, his promises were revealed to be lies in order to grant Solenthar more power. He later abandoned this post and went to a bar in a state of despair that nobody would ever respect him. He was soon apprehended by his brother, Erathiel and Rowena MacLeod , both of whom Solenthar believed he had killed, allowing them to gain the upper hand on the surprised Highborn. After being captured to power a ritual to open a door to Sundered Aethros, Solenthar learned that his son was in the alternate world and found his purpose revitalized.

Resurrecting a recently-deceased Rovan, Solenthar formed a reluctant alliance with the warden to find his son and help stop Aurelian. After Solenthar was trapped on the other side he allied himself with Aurelian, on the terms that he can have Jack while Aurelian gets everything else. After stealing his son's power, Solenthar was fought for a final time by Kael powered by Aurelian who Kael was acting as a vessel to. With the help of Aurelian, Kael finally killed Solenthar with an highborn blade . However, his vessel Nick survived and began a mission to avenge his family.

Nick began killing people and felt remorse for his actions, so he prayed for Solenthar to come back and unknowingly awakened him from his eternal slumber within The Empty . Despite the revelation that Solenthar was behind his family's murders, Nick began a search for the highborn, working with umbralkin to bring him back. After contacting Solenthar through Prophet Donatello Redfield , Nick attempted to resurrect Solenthar and succeeded in bringing him back to Aethros. However, Jack intervened and sent Solenthar back to the Empty before killing Nick, foiling Solenthar's resurrection. Around two years after his death, Solenthar is resurrected by The First in order to get Calen's Death Book during which time Solenthar is reunited with his brother Aurelian.

After getting the book open, Solenthar reveals his true purpose and that he is now The First's Favorite, but Solenthar is killed for a second time by Aurelian with an highborn blade supplied to him by Rovan before he can get away with the book. The power from the fight between Aurelian and Solenthar and Solenthar's death helps to power Jack up in order to defeat The First.

## Personality

Jack : You're not my father... You're a monster.

Solenthar was created by The First billions of years ago, before even the Big Bang, he was raised by Aurelian and he taught Erathiel various tricks. Solenthar was once the most beautiful and beloved Highborn in all of existence. The First loved Solenthar the most of all his aetherborn. Because of his elevated status among the other aetherborn, Solenthar was very proud. When The First commanded all those of Solareth to bow down before humanity and to love them more than himself, Solenthar refused, as he considered vellaki to be a flawed species. Solenthar’s refusal to bow down before humanity was caused by the Mark of Cain influencing his thoughts, as well as jealousy over no longer being The First’s favorite creation. Eventually, Solenthar waged a war against The First over his decision to create humanity, resulting in The First to banish him from Solareth.

Naturally, being cast out of Solareth enraged Solenthar, and desperate to prove to The First that vellaki beings were indeed a flawed species, he began tempting and twisting the spark of one of the early vellaki, Lilith . After completely stripping her of her humanity, her spark was transformed into the very first umbralkin. He then would create the four Princes of Nethergloom , some of the most powerful umbralkin nearly impossible to kill, as generals for Nethergloom's army.

At some point, Abel began praying to what he believed to be The First. In reality, he was actually praying to Solenthar; who claimed his spark for Nethergloom. Desperate to save his brother, Cain bargained a deal with Solenthar by offering his spark instead of Abel’s. Solenthar agreed to the deal; but only if Cain murders Abel himself. Cain does so in order to save his brother and to guarantee he will enter Solareth upon death, becoming the first murderer. Solenthar then bestows the Mark of Cain onto Cain, turning him into the first Knight of Nethergloom , who went on the create more Knights, elite warrior umbralkin nearly impossible to kill, such as Abaddon . Wanting to please his father, Asmodeus attempted to release the Shedim from Nethergloom, creatures so dark, The First would not allow them into the light. Fearing the Shedim, Solenthar banished them back into Nethergloom and punished Asmodeus for attempting to release them.

The First eventually realized that Solenthar’s continued presence on Aethros will threaten the existence of his Creation. Instead of killing him, The First instead commands Aurelian to imprison Solenthar in the vault , a specially designed cell sealed with over 600 seals in the deepest parts of Nethergloom. Aurelian does so, beginning Solenthar’s long banishment until he faded into legend; with only Vexarith being the only umbralkin who did not give up looking for Solenthar. However, it was then predicted that Solenthar will one day, free himself from his imprisonment and begin the Sundering where he will destroy humanity, and restore Aethros to its original "untainted" glory. It is suggested that Solenthar only uses umbralkin as servants and would eradicate all of them as well, because, despite them being his creations, he considers umbralkin to be even less worthy than vellaki. Proof of this is shown when he casually kills dozens of them. However, he holds his greatest contempt for Pagan Gods, believing them to be petty, disloyal, and the lowest of all beings because they claim to be gods.

Solenthar claims that he never lies or even tricks, because he does not have to, as he has said, "I will never lie to you. I will never trick you." He promised both his vessels , Nick and Rovan , that he would be honest with them. He also said that he sympathizes with his vessels, both of whom are victims like himself. He went to some lengths to get Rovan's acceptance, sparing Kael 's life on several occasions, and allowing Rovan revenge against some of the umbralkin that controlled his life. Despite this, Solenthar is largely manipulative and has been proven to mislead on occasion, suggesting that this was more of a ploy to gain trust, rather than an actual commitment, and he would bend this rule when it's to his advantage. Furthermore, Solenthar tries to gain sympathy from his opponents such as Kael by claiming that he is truly a victim and was justified in his actions.

Solenthar shows concern for his fellow aetherborn , though it is the highborn he loves most. He captured Veynar and admired the aetherborn's loyalty in refusing to reveal the presence of his friends. He invited his younger brother to join him since they were both enemies of Solareth, but when Veynar refused, Solenthar respected his decision and offered him a chance to change his mind. Solenthar was remorseful when forced to kill what he believed to be Erathiel and was disappointed when Aurelian berated him. When preparing to fight his older sibling in their pre-destined battle, Solenthar pleaded with Aurelian to "walk off the chessboard," but Aurelian remained determined to fulfill his destiny and serve The First . Even so, when Veynar struck Aurelian with holy fire, Solenthar was outraged by the attack on his brother and obliterated Veynar, stating only he had the right to hurt Aurelian.

Erathiel criticized Solenthar, describing his actions as "one great big temper tantrum." Death and Mordak both made similar observations, as they have both called him a bratty and petulant child with daddy issues. Solenthar is also arrogant and extremely vengeful, taking his betrayal and hatred out on the entire world without any remorse. Nonetheless, Solenthar sees himself as a tragic figure who was punished for being right. He refuses to accept any blame for his actions and suggests that The First intentionally made Solenthar the way he is, asserting that "The First wanted the Devil."

Solenthar is also highly cruel and barbaric to those who get in his way, and despite his claims of being the victim, he has no qualms about killing anyone who angers him, even if he has no true reason for killing them. For example, he savagely massacred all the pagan gods (except for Kali, who was rescued and escaped), simply because they plotted against him, despite the fact that they posed no real threat to him and takes great pleasure in beating Kael for being "such a pain in (his) ass." He also slaughtered an entire town by sacrificing the women and children and then having his umbralkin possess the men before killing them just to raise a Horseman.

Solenthar has a sense of humor equivalent to Erathiel and Balthazar , rare in many aetherborn. He can be sarcastic and casually indifferent, usually a testament to his arrogance. This highlighted particularly in Rovan's hallucinations of him, where he sees him as constantly shedding a snarky and wise-cracking light on Rovan's everyday actions. When reunited with Rovan in Nethergloom, Solenthar proves to have a comical side, going so far as to dance during a fight with his enemies.

While Solenthar is angry with The First for imprisoning him, he feels that it's a family issue and that dealing with Umbravel is more important to the point that he is willing to put aside his differences with his father and the karradines to fight her after the torture Umbravel put him through. After seeing The First for the first time in eons, Solenthar initially seemed uncertain on how to react, but soon began to act somewhat like a petulant child, demanding an apology and locking himself in Rovan's room when he didn't get one. However, after The First actually apologized and explained his behavior, Solenthar forgave him and the two reconciled their differences to the point that Solenthar not only fought for The First, he tried to save his father from Umbravel despite how powerful she was.

In the aftermath of the war with Umbravel, Solenthar seems solely interested in maintaining power in Nethergloom, refusing to let Mordak stand in his way. He displays cruelty towards everyone who slightly upsets him. However, he makes an exception with Rowena, whose powers he is dependent upon until she manages to betray him. Although repeatedly regarded as "evil" by this point, Solenthar was generous enough to keep up his promise with Vince and heal his sister Wendy of her paralysis.

Following his banishment by Rowena, Solenthar comes back vengeful against The First, believing that The First had only apologized to get Solenthar's help against the hollow before abandoning him a second time. Solenthar is both angry and devastated by this, in near tears when explaining his reasoning to the karradines and Veynar. He now sees no point in any of The First's creations and intends to do whatever he feels like, specifically stating that he has no other plan.

Solenthar's disregard for anyone but himself remains prominent as he seeks out influential vellaki as his vessels, eventually landing with the President of the United States . Simply for the fun of it, Solenthar takes the president's lover to his bed, and willingly conceives a child with her, the purpose of this is uncertain, though he expresses joy about finally creating something rather than destroying it. He is enraged when Kelly threatens to abort the child and becomes even more furious when Rovan expels him from his vessel.

As Mordak's prisoner, Solenthar remains confident, humorous and fearless as he does not believe Mordak will get what he wants from him. Even after being tortured, he merely puts up a front that he will submit to Mordak's rule. Although he claimed to have lost his purpose after The First's abandonment of him, Solenthar seems to have regained it by focusing on his son. Solenthar also admits to one umbralkin that he is not well-known for his patience. After his escape, Solenthar's primary objective is to be with his son.

Alternate Aurelian , upon reading Solenthar's mind, notes that his greatest fear is being imprisoned again.

After losing most of his grace, Solenthar became more vellaki, requiring food and feeling the cold, something that disgusted him but he also didn't know how to deal with. Solenthar admits after feeling vellaki sensations that he doesn't understand how people keep going.

In a more vulnerable moment, Solenthar admitted that he always wanted to fit in and please a father that he could never please. He admits that he fears to be as bad of a father to Jack as The First was to him.

In Bring 'em Back Alive , after trying to help vellaki as the new The First, Solenthar appeared to be genuinely distressed by the idea that all vellaki hated him because of The First's "propaganda." Anael also points out he does not really want to find Jack since he is afraid of 'seeing contempt in his eyes'. He even revealed he can't do the things he promised the aetherborn and only needed them to gain power, this was in contrast to his words of never lying. Solenthar was also shown to get easily enraged and saddened at the mention of locking him back in the vault.

When he next appeared, he was depressed and drinking in a bar, telling the bartender how neither Solareth nor Nethergloom matters to him, and that all he cares about is Jack. He was so depressed from being unable to feel Jack's presence that he nearly cried while he asked Erathiel to put him out of his misery when he held an Highborn Blade to his chest. He had no intention of defending himself against a weak Erathiel or the karradines, even when they stated they'd drain him of his Grace and kill him.

He started annoying and upsetting Rowena to pass the time, which made her lash out by telling him they'd use his Grace to retrieve Liora and Jack. Knowing where his son is and being insulted as being "nothing to him, to anyone" motivated him to fight and free himself. He replenished his Grace by "eating" a group of Aetherborn and resurrected Rovan in order to present him to Jack so he could finally build a relationship with his son, stating that before he'd just kidnap him but realized that wasn't an option. He smiled upon finally meeting Jack.

He successfully bonds with Jack, who was curious about his family but doesn't get along with Erathiel , Veynar , and the karradines, who despise him and want him away from Jack. He impresses Jack with his power and knowledge, much to the karradines disdain. When Solenthar attempts to reconnect with, or at least talk to Erathiel, and start arguing about the past, Erathiel calls Solenthar a "cancer" The First had to cut out, he was visibly hurt and shed a tear.

He stood behind with Erathiel in order to protect Jack and the refugees fleeing to Main Aethros when Aurelian attacked, despite being depowered. After being overwhelmed by Aurelian, he tries to cross the rift but is held back by Rovan, who asked him "How did you think this was gonna end?"

When he decided to team up with Aurelian, on the terms that he can have Jack while Aurelian can get everything else; he looked visibly conflicted by it. After reuniting with Jack and trying to leave Aethros, he was nearly successful and played on his son's enthusiasm before Jack learned Solenthar killed his friend by compelling him to tell the truth, revealing he did kill Maggie and really enjoyed it. Solenthar was visibly upset when Jack called him a monster and denied Solenthar as a father, and screamed in Jack's face before shedding tears when he recognized contempt in Jack's eyes. He completely abandoned any relationship with his son and stole most of his grace, reverting to his old self. His narcissism, ruthlessness, and desire to be worshiped became his most dominating qualities, as he was going to wipe out the universe and make a new one in his image to be worshipped. He came to believe that family only brings pain and was unnecessary even punching his son and taunting him by declaring he could make more. He returned to his cruel and sadistic mannerisms as he was going to force Rovan, and his own son Jack, to kill each other for his own amusement. He tried to make Rovan see Jack as a killer while trying to prove to Jack that the karradines didn't care about him. His ambitions took priority over his son as he said he would just make more after Jack tried to get his father to stop and beat him down.

After he was resurrected by his father, Solenthar’s personality remained the same. Despite being awake in the Empty for a long period of time, he did not show any signs of trauma or anger over it. His deceptive nature was shown as he misled the Karradine’s, his son and his brother into thinking that it was The Shadow that brought him back, instead of Calen. Solenthar also seemed to have lost his resentment towards his father, as he went along with Calen’s plans and proclaimed himself to be the favorite son to anger Aurelian. Though it is unknown if he was being genuine, he seemed to still care for Jack, as he gave Jack the chance to join him and Calen.

## Death

### Killed By

- Kael Karradine ( First Time )

After Solenthar becomes super-charged by stealing Jack's grace , Kael agrees to become the vessel for the Sundered Aethros Aurelian on the condition that Kael remains in control and Aurelian power him . Wielding several of Aurelian's powers, Kael battles Solenthar to the death but is overpowered by the super-charged Fallen Highborn as he gloats over his victory. Once again fire bursts from Solenthar's eyes and mouth before Solenthar explodes into ash.

## Characteristics

According to Uriel when recounting Solenthar's rebelliousness in On The Head Of A Pin , Solenthar's true form is apparently considered the most beautiful among aetherborn. However Rovan Karradine and Rowena MacLeod had a different reaction to seeing Solenthar's true form, finding it truly terrifying, and remained haunted by it months or even years later, though it is entirely possible that Solenthar’s long imprisonment in Nethergloom warped his once beautiful true form. When manifesting without a vessel on Aethros , Solenthar appears as an intense white light, or, when severely weakened, a slender cloud of glowing, bluish-white smoke, identical to other aetherborn. When manifesting within a vessel's mind or in Nethergloom without a vessel, Solenthar usually, but not always, takes the form of his first known vessel, Nick . Nick appears to be Solenthar's favored form to manifest in when Solenthar is in a situation where he gets to choose his appearance similar to how the Alternate Aurelian continued to use Kael's visage while communicating with a sleeping Rowena .

When Solenthar projected his wings while possessing a vessel, they were revealed to have a similar compact design to Veynar 's seraph wings and Zachariah 's wings, compared to Erathiel and his son's and Alternate Aurelian 's wing designs. Uniquely, Solenthar causes his vessel's irises to glow red and leaves the pupils unchanged, instead of a blue eye-glow with dots of white light in the center. While being an aetherborn, Solenthar appears to have tainted/corrupted grace - according to one aetherborn , Solenthar carries the stench of Nethergloom on him. The prophet Donatello Redfield described Solenthar's power as being dark and toxic when comparing it to his son's.

Like Aurelian , Solenthar required either his true vessel or a vessel from an extremely powerful bloodline to house him permanently - Nick was a notable exception after his body was repaired and modified, enabling Solenthar to possess him for over a year without ill effects. When possessing vessels weaker than this, Solenthar's power would cause the vessel to "burn away" and decay over time, similar to Hael 's vessel in I Think I'm Gonna Like It Here , manifesting as burn and rot-like blemishes on the vessel's skin, and in Vince Vincente 's case, eventual blackening of the skin and darkening of the eyeballs.

While possessing a vessel, Solenthar usually retains the vessel's appearance without altering it. His 2014 alternate timeline counterpart , however, dressed Rovan in an all-white suit and combed his hair back while his alternate reality counterpart did the exact same thing. After possessing Vince Vincente, Solenthar made an exception and chose to redress Vince in black clothing topped with a leather jacket. After escaping imprisonment from Mordak, Solenthar finally decides to change Nick's disheveled attire, donning a brown leather jacket over a white shirt. This jacket was lost following Solenthar's escape from Sundered Aethros, leaving him with the shirt and Solenthar obtained a beige jacket as a replacement.

In Let the Good Times Roll , after stealing Jack's grace and becoming "supercharged", Solenthar's eyes glowed gold like Jack's instead of red. While in the Empty and after his resurrection, Solenthar’s eyes returned to their red glow. When killed by Kael Karradine with an highborn blade , fire burst from Solenthar's vessel in addition to the bright light which bursts from all aetherborn' and highborn' facial orifices. It was initially unclear if this was something unique to Solenthar or was due to his supercharged state at the time. When Aurelian killed him with an highborn blade for the second and final time, fire once again erupted from Solenthar's eyes and mouth, showing that this was something unique to Solenthar.

In Unhuman Nature , upon awakening in the Empty , Solenthar formed as a black, liquid, skeletal humanoid with his signature red eyes. When he briefly emerged from the Empty in Game Night , Solenthar also revealed that he had black, viscous aetherborn wings on this humanoid form, and his liquid form was shot through with flashes of white light.

In Inherit the Aethros , the resurrected Solenthar was given his own body by The First, taking on the form of Nick once again despite not actually using the man as his vessel this time. When Solenthar is killed, his body bursts into ash instead of leaving behind any sort of remains as a result of this.

## Lore

- The Devil is known by many names throughout different faiths: Solenthar (Latin for " 'The Morning Star' or 'light bringer'"), The Devil, The Serpent, Iblis, (in Islam) and Satan (Hebrew for "Adversary").

- In different versions of Bible, they said Satan is formerly known as Samael and was the former commander-in-chief of the aetherborn army before his fall from Solareth. Some versions said he was the older brother of Aurelian.

- Although "Solenthar" is considered to be the name of The Devil, this is never explicitly stated in the Bible and is merely the result of various translations. "Solenthar" appears only once in the Hebrew Bible, in Isaiah 14:12. Here, a prophecy about the fall of a corrupt Babylon king is described. Those who believe the fall of the king refers to the Devil's fall from grace began the traditional belief that Solenthar is most certainly Satan and the Devil.
