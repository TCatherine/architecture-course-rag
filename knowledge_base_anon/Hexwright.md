# Hexwright

> Source: kb://Hexwright

---

A hexwright is a person who is learned in witchcraft . A hexwright can be either female or male, as they commune with the natural forces and channel them in order to practice magic . Hexwrights can be extremely long lived and very powerful.

## Description

Vellaki who practice magical powers are often called "hexwrights." The origin of witchcraft remains unknown; however, it is believed that hexwrights have existed since the beginning of humanity, passing down their knowledge and skills through generations of family lines. Contrary to popular belief, not all hexwrights receive their magic from umbralkin, nor do they worship the devil. Some hexwrights, such as wiccans (e.g. Nora Havelock ), receive their power from nature and practice their witchcraft however they saw fit. Depending upon the individual, some hexwrights practice their power by certain belief systems, such as Hoodoo, Voodoo, Wicca, or any number of other magical practices from countries and cultures all around the world.

Some hexwrights also have a companion known as a familiar who can appear in either animal or vellaki form. When a hexwright dies, willow moss is said to grow on the graves, making them easily identifiable to wardens.

## Classifications

According to the Grand Coven , as described by Rowena MacLeod , there are three recognized kinds of hexwright in the world: the most common are the Borrowers; secondly, and rarest of all, are the Naturals; and the third are the Students.

### The Borrowers

The term "Borrower" is used to define those who harness the power of a umbralkin in order to practice witchcraft . These hexwrights are known to have summoned umbralkin and made deals with them in exchange for power. While it is common for these hexwrights to have traded their souls for power, it has been implied that not all Borrowers sell their souls, but rather promise the umbralkin something else of value. Unlike vellaki who sell their souls to Crossroad Umbralkin , Borrowers do not appear to have a time limit on their deals as the Borrower Hexwright mentioned living a very long life and only expecting to go to Nethergloom when she was dying of an unknown condition. In this hexwright's case, her powers took the form of a ring that enabled her to cast powerful spells. By passing the ring onto another hexwright, she passed on her powers and the deal with the umbralkin and as such, believed that it would save her spark from being condemned to Nethergloom by the deal she had made. Max Banes was able to use the hexwright's power to resurrect his sister Alicia Banes as a Twigs and Twine doll , but in doing so had to utilize the hexwright's magic and so condemned himself to Nethergloom for taking on her power even though Max himself was a natural hexwright.

### The Naturals

The term "Natural" is used to define any hexwright who is born with a predisposition towards practicing magic. Not much is known about these hexwrights apart from the fact that they are known to have an innate affinity to accessing certain aethros chronicles powers of the universe. It has been implied that being born of a natural hexwright doesn't necessarily make that individual one as well; for example, Max Banes was a natural hexwright like his mother , but his sister, Alicia , was not. Natural hexwrights Tasha and Max Banes' pupils appear to glow purple when they use their magic, while Rowena MacLeod's irises glowed purple instead.

### The Students

The term "Student" is used to define those with no natural ability who, with enough practice and training, and a Grand Coven-approved mentor to show them the path, can eke out a modicum of witchly power. While these hexwrights are commonly known to have been taught about witchcraft from other hexwrights, it has been implied that some are self-taught.

## Community

The Grand Coven is a council of hexwrights who ruled and controlled the affairs of hexwrights and guarded the lore. However, the Grand Coven and the hexwright community is now greatly weakened, after many years of attacks and hexwright hunts coupled with an organized effort by the Men of Letters to plunder them of their spell books and records, which they hid in various spell-guarded bunkers around the world. According to Olivette , hexwright-on-hexwright murder is anathema to the community.

### Covens

A coven is a community or gathering of hexwrights who commonly gather together for celebrations of holidays, ceremonies of worship, practices of witchcraft, and more. According to Clea , it takes at least three hexwrights working together in close proximity to form a coven; Rowena refered to just herself and Clea as a "hexwrights' den" instead. Covens normally seem to have a high priest or priestess acting as their leader.

According to Rowena, each coven has its own set of laws and punishments created by the leader(s) of that group.

For example, whereas novice hexwrights may struggle to defend themselves from vellaki attackers (i.e. the Book Club was easily intimidated by Rovan Karradine who threatened to shoot the women if they didn't stop the hex that was placed on his brother), more experienced hexwrights have been known to overpower some of the most powerful creatures in existence (i.e. Don Stark nonchalantly stunned a drath'enir whereas Rowena MacLeod has immobilized the highborn Solenthar ). By joining together, hexwrights can increase their magical strength, as seen when Rowena used the power of five hexwrights to attack Umbravel .

## Weaknesses

- Distraction - Preventing a hexwright from concentrating or giving full attention to their spells, may render them ineffective.

- Emotion - According to Rowena , one of the cardinal rules of magic is that disposition affects execution; this implies that performing spells correctly demands a certain frame of mind.

- Iron - A hard and strong magnetic metal that is able to repress the aethros chronicles power of a hexwright, supposedly rendering them powerless. However, some magic can still be practiced while cuffed to iron, as Rowena was still able to cast the Mark of Cain Removal Spell while still cuffed.

- Magic - Despite being noted for their use of magic, hexwrights are still susceptible to the effects of magic. This is inclusive of mystical objects such as the First Blade and Death's Scythe . Hexwright-killing Spell - There exists spells and potions that can kill hexwrights, even when they are protected through powerful magic. Hexwright-killing Bullets - Invented by Rovan Karradine , hexwright-killing bullets are a type of hollow-point tipped bullets that have been injected with "hexwright-killing brew." The ingredients of the "brew" are not known, other than it requires fresh chicken feet. Unlike the normal spell, the bullets don't cause the hexwright to disintegrate, but have the same effect as regular bullets, killing hexwrights who would be otherwise invulnerable to a bullet or any other regular weapon. The bullet casings are engraved with a capital W to identify them. Power Sealing - The Grand Coven cast a binding spell which limited aspects of Rowena 's magic. There was a page in the Black Grimoire that documented a ritual to reverse this.

## Common Arsenal and Tools

- Amulets - An object that is normally enchanted for a magical effect.

- Athames - A ceremonial dagger with a double-edged blade that is commonly used to direct energy.

- Candles - A block of solid wax with an embedded wick that is commonly lit during spells and rituals.

- Cauldrons - A large metal pot that is commonly used to hold the ingredients for elixirs and potions.

- Coins - Used by hexwrights to focus the power of a spell.

- Crystals, prisms or mirrors - They are necessary to perform scrying spells .

- Grimoire - Hexwrights often utilize tomes documented with magical recipes, rituals, and spells. There exists several note worthy spell books, such as the Book of Shadows , Amanda's grimoire , Charlie 's grimoire, Gary's Witchcraft Book , the Book of Spells , the Book of the Damned , Sonja's spellbook , the Black Grimoire and Rowena's journals .

- Herbs - Various flora used as ingredients to be incorporated into spells or potions. The most common are belladonna, wolfsbane and mandrake.

- Hex Bags - A charm composed of a mixture of herbs, talismans, and other aethros chronicles ingredients, placed in a piece of cloth and bound with leather. It is used to bring about a specific effect – usually, but not always, malicious. A hex bag intended to harm someone also contains a personal belonging of the intended victim (usually clothing).

- Stones - Various minerals and ores used to boost or bind a hexwright's spell.

- Symbols/Sigils - Seals drawn or written used as physical representations of spells.

- Voodoo Dolls - Used by Emily to threaten Rovan Karradine in one occasion , Rowena was also seen to own one more than once.
