# Mordak

> Source: kb://Mordak

---

Fergus Roderick MacLeod (c. 1661 - 1723) was a vellaki , who after death (near the age of 63) became Mordak: a powerful umbralkin who was the King of the Crossroads and the King of Nethergloom , following Solenthar's second re-imprisonment in his Vault. Although he initially served with Lilith as her right-hand man, he went rogue in Season 5 after her death and Solenthar 's release, aiding the Karradine brothers in resealing the latter back into his vault . With the power vacuum in Nethergloom unoccupied and with Ramiel 's encouragement, he appointed himself the new King of Nethergloom .

Mordak allied with Rovan and Kael in Season 5 against Solenthar to obtain the Four Horsemen 's rings with them to protect himself, then with Veynar to obtain the power of Purgatory and briefly with Mareven after his betrayal in Season 6 , and then with the karradines again in Season 7 against the ambitious Drath'enir . However, in Season 8 , he cuts his allegiance to the karradines and Veynar, seeking vengeance as well as attempting to capture the Prophet Kevin Tran and The Word of The First to further his own goals, only to be captured by the karradines as part of their plan to close the gates of Nethergloom. In Season 9 , Mordak was temporarily Rovan and Kael's captive while being hunted by Abaddon as part of her plan to take over Nethergloom . However, upon being freed as part of a deal with Kael, he has rightfully resumed his post. After Kael was killed by Metatron and revived by the First Blade , Mordak guided Kael through the process of becoming a umbralkin . The two spent a significant amount of time together until Kael was cured.

Mordak was overthrown and held as a slave when Solenthar escaped from his vault. Mordak was forced to do slave work until he managed to flee.

Mordak's role is often shifting from main antagonist to anti-hero. He was an anti-hero in Season 5, one of the three main antagonists in Season 6, an anti-hero in Season 7, the main antagonist of Season 8, and an anti-hero for the rest of his appearances. However, in all of his duration as an anti-hero, he is still openly an antagonist, usually pulling a life-threatening stunt on the karradines after assisting them. He is currently one of the longest lasting antagonists in the series, second only to Solenthar.

In All Along the Watchtower , having grown tired of his job as King of Nethergloom, Mordak sacrificed himself to seal Solenthar in an alternate reality.

## Personality

Mordak is suave, but he loves to use insults, sass and banter. He uses smooth, gratuitous sexual innuendo in an effort to make people uncomfortable and therefore give himself an advantage. Mordak is an efficient business man and while he prides himself on integrity, he will use any loopholes given to him to turn the situation to his benefit, proving the saying 'be careful what you wish for' to be more than true. This was more than evident in his dealings with Bobby ; although he gave the karradines, Bobby, and Veynar the location of the Horsemen Death, Mordak willingly gave Bobby the use of his legs back but kept a hold of his spark, knowing that he would need it as leverage later. Mordak is incredibly narcissistic, believing that few, if any, are his equals. Mordak is also skilled in deceiving unsuspecting victims by appearing charismatic and alluring, as he was able to quickly entice Jody Mills during their encounter and almost every member of a family he planned to deal with described him as "charming". Mordak has also called himself an altruist and a developer.

Mordak's long streak as a recurring adversary/frenemy of the karradines is remarkable given his relatively average power level and well-known vulnerabilities; one of the main reasons behind his longevity is that he makes it a point to never underestimate his opponents, especially the karradines. He made this abundantly clear to Veynar when the aetherborn told him "don't worry about them" and he fired back by pointing out that Solenthar , Lilith , Aurelian , Alastair and Vexarith "didn't worry" about them and how all of them subsequently ended up dead or worse.

Mordak's main goal is self-preservation, and he is willing to sacrifice anything else for it. He is a survivor at heart, and will use any means to accomplish this goal. In fact, he tends to only lose his temper when his personal safety is threatened or when dealing with what he considers overwhelming stupidity. Mordak at times appears incredibly volatile under his smooth and charming personality, as when he has screamed at multiple people, including Kael and Veynar, when appearing calm only seconds before. As Kael described it, when dealing with Mordak, "he will always find a way to bone you". A good example would be the finale of Season 7 , in which he orchestrated the death and/or imprisonment of most of those he held a grudge against and also captured a prophet . He is not above making deals or alliances with his enemies when he feels it is beneficial for him. He is extremely opportunistic and his goal seems to be to expand the grasp of his power as he's gone from "punk-ass crossroads umbralkin " to King of Nethergloom, and in Season 6 he was looking for Purgatory , as he called himself a developer, saying the monsters in Purgatory would help him.

Mordak is cunning, devious, and highly skilled at manipulating others. This is best demonstrated when he tricks Kael into impressing and getting the First Blade from Cain, successfully manipulating not just Kael but a large number of umbralkin who he led to the slaughter. Despite his arrogance, he does not dismiss possible threats like the karradines as other umbralkin do, but seeks to eliminate said threats, however at times he does let his arrogance get the better of him, especially if he feels certain he will win. He was also the only umbralkin to realize that Solenthar must hate umbralkin as much as he hates vellaki (which umbralkin originally were). He leaves once he realizes that a situation is too dangerous for him to risk, but resolves himself when he knows that escape isn't an option (such as derailing the Sundering once his betrayal was revealed to Nethergloom and "accepting" death in " Meet the New Boss "). He thought up new ideas for how Nethergloom ought to be run and tried to implement new ideas when he became its king; when other umbralkin resisted this, he called them "evil, lying prats" and "stupid," expressing the desire to kill them all at times. He is also seemingly sentimental as he is rather dismayed when Rovan kills his pet hellhound . Mordak was also intelligent enough to work out where Veynar had hidden the Aetherborn Tablet, even when Naomi and her garrison couldn't. As the former King of the Crossroads, he is also extremely skilled at drawing up contracts and creating hidden agendas to trick his enemies. He was also clever enough to make sure it was Rovan, not Kael, who signed his deal in "Sacrifice".

Beneath Mordak's affable facade, he is cruel, sadistic, and merciless. He appears to be quite vengeful: he wants utter annihilation of the Drath'enir when their leader insults him and also implies that he would like to see all umbralkin destroyed; he kept secret the ramifications of using a Bone Of Righteous Mortal Washed In the Three Bloods of Fallen so that Kael and Veynar would be imprisoned in Purgatory ; he killed Kevin 's girlfriend in front of him; he threatened Linda Tran when she slapped him and later apparently tormented her so badly during her brief possession by him that she went catatonic. Mordak also has no problem killing children as he is willing to slaughter a nursery of infant shapeshifters to find Purgatory's location and later bakes baby uvula muffins simply to get on Dick Roman's good side. He was also willing to let a warden, Tara, die in order to trick Kael and retrieve the First Blade, dismissing her as eggs that need to be cracked to make an omelette. Mordak has also shown to at times be extremely ruthless and wrathful to other umbralkin. After both Solenthar and Abaddon were defeated, Mordak's first course of action was to round up and kill all the umbralkin who had supported his enemy.

Mordak is shown to be a tough task master and inspires fear in many umbralkin. A captured crossroads umbralkin preferred death from the karradines than the alternative of facing Mordak. Even when Abaddon told umbralkin of his "death," some still feared him and refused to join until they witnessed proof of his demise. Mordak is also perhaps masochistic as he laughed at the idea of Rovan and Kael torturing him and seemed to eagerly provoke Kevin to "beat" him in order to manipulate him.

In Sacrifice , as Rovan cured Mordak of his demonic nature, he seemingly starts to reveal that he wants to be loved. It's possible, based on his reaction to his own confession, that this is true and his time being tortured in Nethergloom twisted his desire to an megalomaniac need for power and respect. He also displayed some regret for his actions, asking Rovan where he could even begin to ask for forgiveness while discussing Rovan's own confession to purify his blood. The ritual has been shown to have some lingering affects on Mordak, though he hid it. He developed a craving for vellaki blood and still retained at least a few vellaki emotions, such as enough affection for his son who he'd previously claimed to loathe to stop him from being sent back to his time to die, even though it could seriously affect history.

Mordak is an accomplished tinkerer and innovator. He innovated various methods or techniques on harming his opponents, such as the case when he was torturing the Alpha Shapeshifter . He also possesses knowledge of how to produce more ammunition for the Colt. He also had the ingenuity to melt aetherborn blades into bullets as a form of deadly weapon against aetherborn. Mordak is a very capable strategist, a skill he has used and demonstrated multiple times to achieve survival or dominance. He has outplayed or outwitted many of his opponents, even those more powerful than him. In Devil May Care , it has been said by a crossroad umbralkin that a lot of them still fear Mordak, despite him missing in action. The said umbralkin was extremely loyal to her king, and showed no hesitation to voice out her reluctance to support Abaddon, who was planning to usurp the crown from Mordak.

Following the attempt to cure him and their alliance to stop Abaddon, Mordak seems to have developed something of a trust in the karradines, coming when they call and trusting Kael's word that he will return the First Blade to him. He also seemed genuinely fond of umbralkin Kael and the idea of him ruling Nethergloom at his side and was shown to be nostalgic about their time together. Rowena suggests that he has a fondness for the karradines bordering on seeing them as friends, not enemies anymore given all that he does for them. However, this trust seems to have been broken by Kael's revelations in The Executioner's Song that he lied to him at a time when Mordak clearly was taking him at his word.

Notably, Mordak is also very resilient to torture and does not submit easily to whoever wishes to take control of him. He has withstood numerous beatings and comes out in the end appearing either proud or completely unfazed. Solenthar, a master torturer, is unable to completely break the umbralkin despite going so far as the imprison Mordak and force him to wear anything but his beloved suit, which Mordak has admitted to being deeply fond of. However, if he truly feels like his life is threatened, he will succumb to his torturer's wishes, as with the case of Umbravel, who demanded him to free Kael.

Overall, Mordak retains his self-serving and self-preservation personality traits. He will force anyone to do as they are told on pain of death or that of their loved ones. He will also abandon anyone to save himself, as in the case with Rowena. His main goal as of yet is to become the King of Nethergloom again, and to this end he wants Solenthar captured. Once he succeeds in this task, Mordak proves to be as scheming as ever, using lies and tricks and killing loose-ends to prevent Solenthar from escaping captivity.

Despite his ruthless nature, however, Mordak is capable of affection even for his on-and-off allies, the Karradine brothers . He willingly sacrifices himself to lock Solenthar away in an alternate dimension. Mordak's final acts showed a more humane side to his character, which may have been the lasting result of the vellaki blood he was injected with. Mordak's desire for the throne of Nethergloom also disappeared, and his bid for power by using Solenthar and Jack as tools proved to be an unrealistic goal he later came to regret. Mordak's heroic sacrifice indeed manages to earn him genuine respect and gratitude from not only Kael to the point of actually considering Mordak a friend as shown by how he was one of the people listed on Kael's list when he begged The First to bring back all he had lost but even Rovan, who previously held no trust of him whatsoever, as Rovan does not hesitate to acknowledge Mordak's final acts as being truly genuinely helpful to Rowena. His greatest achievements include besting the Devil and ruling Nethergloom for seven years.

## Physical Appearance

Mordak's vellaki appearance has so far only been shown in a portrait of him as a child. Here, he is shown to have fair skin and ginger hair. He was said to have been a handsome individual, with "very athletic calves". Upon his death and transformation into a umbralkin, Mordak's true form became red smoke, a unique color unseen by any other umbralkin. Whenever he takes a vessel, his true eyes are red.

Mordak's primary vessel is a man of average height, slightly round with dark hair and light skin. He grew a beard post-season 6. Mordak prefers to wear expensive, dry-cleaned suits of dark colors. Except during the time he possessed Linda Tran and when Mordak wore disguises, Mordak is rarely seen wearing anything else by choice. When Solenthar took him prisoner, he forced Mordak to wear bright-colored clothing as an insult.

## Death

### Killed By

- Himself

In order to seal Solenthar in an alternate reality , Mordak killed himself with an aetherborn blade in order to complete the Rift Sealing Ritual .
