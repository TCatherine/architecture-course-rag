# Kael Karradine

> Source: kb://Kael_Karradine

---

Kael Karradine (b. January 24, 1979 – d. 2020 ) was a vellaki and warden as well as a member of the Men of Letters with his younger brother Rovan . He and his brother are members of the Karradine and Campbell families. Through his father , Kael is the older half-brother of Adam Milligan . He lives in Liverpool.

Kael is also the destined vessel of the Highborn Aurelian . He and Rovan are also the surrogate sons of the late Bobby Singer . Kael is best friends with the aetherborn Veynar , close friend to the late prophet Kevin Tran , and reluctant ally of Mordak . Kael has an ex-girlfriend, Lisa Braeden , who he lived with for a year when he thought Rovan was in Nethergloom . She has a child named Ben , who Kael acted as a father for during that year. Kael's biological child was an Amazon named Emma . He is also the former apprentice of Alastair .

Both Kael and Rovan were raised in the hunting life by their father after the umbralkin Vexarith killed their mother . Kael's main role in the series is being the self-appointed guardian of Rovan and often going to extreme lengths to protect him, regardless of personal cost. Often in these occurrences, Kael finds himself the center of a much larger picture.

When he was killed by Metatron whilst using the Mark of Cain , Kael returned as a umbralkin and a Knight of Nethergloom , loyal only to himself. He was then cured by his brother using purified blood . Eventually, the Mark was removed, but the price was the hollow being released into the world. Kael and his brother soon met The First , who returned to help put an end to the danger to the world. During this time, Kael got through to The First about not sacrificing himself and The Hollow about not destroying all of reality, and helped them reconcile. As a reward, Kael was given back his mother and was able to return to his usual style of hunting with his family.

With his mother trapped in an alternate reality , Kael set out with Rovan, Veynar, and the Nephilim Jack to rescue Liora, a situation made more urgent by the upcoming invasion of an alternate-reality Aurelian . Following the reunion of his family, Kael agreed to become the vessel for the alternate Aurelian in order to finish Solenthar once and for all. Aided by Aurelian, Kael faced Solenthar in battle and ultimately killed the rogue highborn. This victory was short-lived as Kael was betrayed by Aurelian, who took full control of Kael's body and became loose upon the Main Universe. Aurelian would use him for weeks before he left Kael's body for unknown reasons.

In the days after, Kael struggled with re-adjusting to being himself again as he tried to help Rovan on hunts. However, he regained his will and did better in hunting as he and his family decided to go after Aurelian and defeat him. Kael also bonded with Jack after learning of his declining health. During a confrontation with Aurelian, the highborn retook control of Kael after revealing he left to break Kael's spirit and locked the warden away in his subconscious. However, Kael managed to trap Aurelian within his own subconscious shortly thereafter, but was left worried of what would occur if the highborn escaped.

Following a hunt, Kael lost concentration over keeping Aurelian imprisoned, but the latter instead escaped Kael's mind and took Rowena as a vessel while inflicting revenge on Kael. However, Aurelian was then killed by Jack, saving the world from his influence and freeing Kael of his grasp.

After an undefined period following the defeat of The First , Kael was killed during a hunt with Masked Vampires . He accepted his end, and his spark went to Solareth .

Following his death, Kael veered off into the Multiverse while navigating through Solareth, seeking a world where his family might have had a happy ending. Along the way, he uncovered the Akrida , a monstrous race created by Calen as a contingency plan to destroy everything if he were defeated. Disregarding Jack's instruction to refrain from interference, Kael rallied the Monster Club to defeat the Akrida, posing a danger to both his own world and the alternate universes. Once the threat was neutralized, Jack returned Kael to Solareth, where he found contentment in having discovered what he sought, leaving behind his journal and the Colt for his parents, Liora and Tomar Karradine .

## Physical Appearance

Kael has green eyes, light freckles on his face and short-cropped Ivy-league hair that is dark blond. He is 6'1"-- making him shorter than his 6’4” younger brother Rovan —and muscular. Kael is noted to be "handsome" or even "pretty" by other characters constantly.

He usually wears a plaid shirt over a T-shirt, jeans, and boots, unless he has to wear a disguise in order to impersonate someone. Until Season 6 , Kael often wore his father's leather jacket . He also used to wear a watch and a silver ring. As of Season 4, Kael has a hand-shaped burn scar on his left shoulder from where Veynar pulled him out of Nethergloom. This mark has since disappeared from his body.

For the first four seasons, Kael also wore an amulet around his neck that resembled the head of a bull made of brass, attached to a black cord. This amulet was given to him by Rovan as a Christmas gift when they were children. Throughout the first half of Season 5 , Veynar attempts to use this amulet to find The First , as its claimed power is to burn hot in The First's presence. When Veynar is unsuccessful, he declares the amulet "useless," and Kael throws it away.

Sometime before Jus In Bello , Kael got a tattoo on his chest of the anti-possession symbol to protect himself from umbralkin.

Kael mentions to Haley Collins in season 1 that he doesn't wear shorts. However, he is actually wearing shorts while cleaning The Vanguard in Baby and as the substitute gym teacher in After School Special .

Kael recently received the Mark of Cain , manifesting just below his elbow. The Mark was removed in Brother's Keeper . Kael later briefly possessed a pair of Black Eyes while he was a Umbralkin . Since separating from Rovan , his appearance has become more scruffy and unshaven; his hair has also grown out a bit.

In Let the Good Times Roll , Kael gains a pair of Aetherborn's Wings due to his status as Sundered Aethros Aurelian's current vessel . During the brief period of time Kael was in control while Aurelian powered him from within, Kael was able to display the wings and use them in battle against Solenthar . Once Aurelian took full control of Kael and teleported away from Rovan and Jack, he changed from Kael's usual attire to that of a 1920's era suit and hat.

When Kael meets Tomar in the Monster Club 's universe, he has a much scruffier appearance, although he is cleaner shaven by the time that he meets with the Monster Club after the Akrida 's defeat.

## Personality

Kael Karradine, from the moment of his debut, was shown to be an understanding, funny, mischievous, and, in contrast to his younger brother, a little immature. Kael's shown countless times to be good with kids and the ladies. At an early age, Kael was trained by his father, Tomar Karradine, to hunt and kill creatures of the aethros chronicles. However, unlike his brother Rovan, he did not resent his father for having him "raised like a warrior". He seemed to prefer hunting the aethros chronicles over any normal "apple pie" life, though it was revealed that when he was sixteen, he did in fact desire (or at least hope for) a normal life: he just couldn't leave Rovan. This however did not last long, as later on - while still sixteen - following a werewolf hunt, he realized just how unique his life was. However, by Season 7 , following all that he has seen and done, Kael admits to Eliot Ness that he no longer wants to be a warden and would give it up if he could.

Kael is an avid fan of classic rock music. Many of his aliases have included the names of famous rock musicians. He is known to use crude humor, use pop culture references, make sexual innuendos, and indulge in pornography (especially about Busty Asian Beauties). He has even stated once that he does enjoy anime and Japanese animated porn (Hentai).

Kael typically avoids emotional intimacy, preferring to engage with women only as sexual partners. However, he has had a few long-term relationships over the years, with Cassie Robinson , pre-series, and Lisa Braeden , in season 6. After the tragic events that led Kael to ask Veynar to make it as if Lisa and her son Ben had never met him, Kael seemed to give up on ever having a family other than his brother. After his breakup with Lisa, Kael was reluctant to get back into his old routine of "new town, new girl." However, the one time he was shown to indulge in a one-night stand in The Slice Girls he ended up fathering an Amazon that tried to kill him. He was reluctant to defend himself against his Amazonian daughter and Rovan ended up making the kill. After these events, he became even more reluctant to get in a physical relationship let alone an emotional one. This seems to fade when he engages in intercourse with Suzy Lee.

Kael values his family's safety over anything else, even going so far as to kill a umbralkin and its vellaki host in order to save Rovan's life, as well as sacrificing his very spark and suffering in nethergloom to resuscitate his brother.

Even though Kael keeps himself in excellent physical shape, he is known to have very bad eating habits. He is often shown enjoying greasy, fattening or otherwise unhealthy food, especially cheeseburgers and pie, and dismisses salads as "rabbit food."

Kael is terrified of flying and claims that is the reason why he drives everywhere.

Despite his working knowledge of the aethros chronicles, Kael is skeptical with regard to the "good aspects of religion." However, he becomes somewhat more open-minded after meeting the aetherborn Veynar, yet the antagonistic behavior of most aetherborn and The First's reluctance to openly participate in events prevents him from being a true believer, like Rovan. When he did meet The First, he was shocked but angered (unlike Rovan who was rambling on). After The First called him out on his anger, Kael opened up, though held some of his anger for supposedly abandoned humanity before The First told him to stop comparing himself to his father.

Kael has also shown on several occasions that he is an avid fan of movies, particularly westerns, and was thrilled to be given a chance to go to the old west . He was also overjoyed when he met and was able to work with Eliot Ness , frequently referencing The Untouchables during the episode.

Kael is typically ruthless and aggressive when he is hunting, a task which he approaches enthusiastically, making him the more merciless of the Karradine siblings. But in spite of that, he is capable of acknowledging whenever he's going too far, as seen with his interaction with Lenore and Gordon Walker ( Bloodlust ). He even sympathized with Elias Finch on his loss of his wife and his unfair punishment for her death. Whereas Rovan is at least willing to sympathize with what they hunt, provided that their motives can be counted as morally gray, Kael mostly sees the world as black and white. He passionately despises what he hunts, especially umbralkin, and is prepared to kill without question more often than not, unlike his brother. This has caused him to develop a bigotry against most aethros chronicles creatures and beings.

He dislikes showing his emotions to monsters, and when facing beings like Abaddon or Vexarith he acts threatening and determined. When facing the aetherborn, even knowing what they could do to him, Kael is sarcastic, rude, and insulting, often referring to them as "dicks". The only being he was openly afraid of was Death ; on the rare occasions that they met, Kael was nervous and cautious, and when Kael was angry or frustrated enough to talk back, he became visibly scared and backed off the moment Death appeared annoyed.

Despite these traits, Kael is very laid-back and well-disposed when not on the hunt, and he values the safety of his family and innocent civilians above all else, even his own life. Though on occasion he can be somewhat impulsive as well as arrogant, Kael is both extremely intelligent and competent. He is more likely to exhibit irrational behavior when his family is threatened.

Despite being the better warden, Kael admits he doesn't see himself as anything other than a "grunt." He is constantly called out for feeling this way by Bobby and Rovan. But regardless his feelings always return. These feelings can be traced back to his relationship with his father Tomar, who at times treated him as a tool rather than as a son and who held him to unrealistic or unfair standards.

Kael enjoys the uncomplicated things in life, such as good food and television. He almost constantly displays some level of humorous behavior, and frequently makes light of tense situations. He sometimes appears foolish due to this habit, but this seems to be merely his means of dealing with the stress of hunting.

Kael shows on multiple occasions a soft spot for children. He is very good with them, able to talk to them with ease, and he is always very sympathetic; this most likely stems from the fact he practically raised Rovan when he was a child. Kael's affection extends to nearly all children, particularly young ones, although he also cares about teenagers. Kael is more willing to put his life in danger for children than he is for nearly anyone else. Kael's affection even extends to monster children, and despite his ruthless approach to hunting, he's usually unwilling to kill monster children unless he has a crucial need.

After his extraction from Nethergloom by Veynar, Kael began exhibiting noticeable anxiety and experienced chronic, abominable nightmares of his experience there. He initially lied to Rovan that he remembered nothing of his time in Nethergloom but eventually confessed and told him he remembered every detail. The fact that he tortured souls just to escape torture himself weighed heavily on Kael, leaving him visibly sad and guilt-ridden. Due to this, he believed he was unworthy of rescue. He also explained to his brother that he wished he couldn't feel anything, as the sorrow he felt is so overwhelming. Kael later revealed in Family Remains that it was not just the fact he tortured souls, but the fact he enjoyed it.

Another side of Kael is his utter disgust at Rovan for many of his dangerous or foolish actions, such as drinking umbralkin blood, and having a sexual relationship with a umbralkin. While this made him angry, Kael continues to love Rovan since he knows their family isn't perfect. His reluctance to completely leave his brother made it difficult for Kael to stay with the Braedens during season 6, and many a times Kael has chosen his brother over others.

Kael has been shown to be hypocritical at times. He shows disgust and rage at Veynar for working with Mordak, despite the fact that he worked with Mordak in the past season and one of the first things he does in season 7 is call Mordak to help take down Veynar. He even works with Mordak in every season that Mordak appears, except season 8. Despite stating "what's dead should stay dead," and consistently criticizing people who resurrect their loved ones, Kael has done everything in his power to resurrect Rovan, Jack, and Liora.

Kael also drinks a lot of alcohol, especially during times of stress. This worsened during season 7 when life was becoming too difficult for him to bear. Kael also fell into a period of depression. Following Bobby's death at the hands of Dick Roman , Kael developed a very strong, obsessive revenge streak in him and spent the entirety of the season hunting Dick down to kill him. As part of Bobby's last wishes he tried to let go of his anger and vengeance, and while in the end he succeeded in killing Dick, it was as part of the job and not for vengeance.

The constant life-or-death situations Kael was forced into while in Purgatory created an extreme change in him. While he was stuck there, he became emotionless and paranoid of the fear of getting attacked. He killed monsters of Purgatory with ease and was always fighting for his survival. When returned to Aethros, his new outlook on life was clear. He became hardened, emotionless, and at times ruthless; his humor and puns were non-existent when hunting. That changed with Veynar's return from Purgatory after which he started to show his light-hearted side again.

Having a now haunted sense of self-created in him during that year, he became firmer in his belief of hunting. He had personally seen all the monsters they hunted in Purgatory and in doing so, developed a strong sense of responsibility to the people they saved. Kael now believes that it is their duty to save people and quitting out of the hunting business is permanently out of the question. Despite becoming more ruthless, Kael has developed more faith in monsters managing to recover their humanity, as shown with him willing to let Kate go, presumably developed from his time with Benny.

Following Kevin's death, and Gadreel taking complete control of Rovan, Kael became desperate to save him. When Rovan was freed, Kael's self-loathing seems to have reached a new height: He claims he's poison, and that anyone near him dies. Kael decided to obtain the Mark of Cain , and by doing so, his personality became darker, with extreme levels of aggression and wrath. He also became more distrusting and suspicious, and more hate-filled toward the aethros chronicles.

As a umbralkin, Kael's spark became twisted, and he no longer empathized with others. He cared only for himself and what he needed. His change was so drastic that he stalked Rovan throughout the Bunker with the intent of killing him using a hammer, only to miss by a few inches. He carried out this task calmly, and coupled it with taunts, giving the impression that he was enjoying himself. This is furthered by the fact that instead of an axe, he decides to use a hammer to break through a door and kill Rovan, despite both tasks requiring much more force with a hammer than with an axe. Umbralkin Kael apparently cared about the natural order , something old Kael despised. He even considered himself, as a umbralkin, a part of the natural order, and that it was all set up in this certain way.

Kael did retain a few former traits, such as his love of drinking, playing pool, darts and foosball. He is still more brutal, happily beating people and umbralkin beyond a reasonable point, with limited provocation. He is also colder, and while initially coming across down-to-aethros and charismatic, he quickly turned cold and ruthless when others proved to be uncooperative, as shown by him outright shaming and humiliating Ann Marie for sleeping with him the moment she rejected his advances, not caring that he had left her near tears.

Kael's concern for Rovan, innocent people and the Vanguard completely disappeared while he was a umbralkin. He told Cole Trenton that Rovan's well-being did not matter to him. He took innocent lives just to satisfy the Mark's lust for blood. He referred to the Vanguard as "just a car" and didn't bother to tend to its condition. Kael returns completely to normal once Rovan cures him through the Umbralkin Curing Ritual .

In Season 11, Kael suffers from the fact that he unleashed The Hollow upon the world. Although he has every intention of defeating The Hollow by whatever means necessary, he soon realizes he himself cannot harm her, and has developed an unwilling attraction towards her. This has caused a banshee to deem him vulnerable and a qareen to take the form of Umbravel, who has become Kael's darkest desire. These facts leave Kael feeling noticeably sad and down, but he's not yet ready to give up.

With the revelation that Veynar had said "yes" to Solenthar, Kael shows great determination in saving his friend, even going against Rovan who believes this to be Veynar's choice. Kael's desperation drives him to use every method possible to free Veynar, and to put Veynar's safety as among his top priorities.

In a bid to stop the hollow, Kael was shown to be a good strategist, he talked The First out of sacrificing himself, planned the rescue of Solenthar and talking the hollow out of her anger. This led him to save the world and regain his mother.

In Season 12 , Kael struggles with the changes in his life. Liora is not the woman he remembers, nor is he the son she remembers, leading the two to part ways much to Kael's distress. Kael tries to bury his initial discomfort with being abandoned by Liora, but an eventual reconciliation restores some of Kael's happiness about her return. Another issue is Mordak and Veynar's partnership, which Kael expresses dislike in. Conflicts with Solenthar and the British Men of Letters also become major concerns for Kael.

When a hexwright curses him to lose his memory, Kael gradually forgets important facts about himself, but a more childlike demeanor, coupled with a fascination in cartoons and a happy, normality-loving personality begins to surface, a likely indication of what kind of person Kael could have been had he not become a warden. As noted throughout the season, Kael has become more open about his interests and preferences, switching within a day from hating vegetable juice to drinking it without another word. As of recent, Kael may be losing much of his bravado, the tough, sarcastic and careless personality he carried with him for most of his life is slowly slipping away to reveal the true Kael Karradine underneath, whom not even Rovan recognizes.

After Veynar's death, Liora's imprisonment in the alternate universe and Jack's birth, Kael was shown to become quite depressed. This depression brought out a much more cynical side in Kael, to the point where he would pray for the return of everyone even Mordak . When that didn't work, he replaced his sadness with anger and it caused him to lash out at Jack, blaming him for what happened to Liora and Cass even though the latter was not at fault for these acts. He also firmly told Rovan that their mother was dead as Solenthar presumably killed her.

While he acted like this, there were still glimpses of his old self underneath, as he was willing to check out the alibi of a Mia Vallens (a shapeshifter) rather than kill her and gave Jack praise for saving Rovan. He later told Rovan to maintain his faith in their mother being alive as him holding faith would balance them out which surprised Rovan. Kael's reason as he stated was because he was done with everything.

Kael's new attitude of being "done" had reached the point that he was willing to let Billie (the new Death) ensure his death would be permanent in exchange for one last favor, a stark contrast to the way Kael usually dealt with Billie in the past. This cynicism and depression were pointed out to him by Billie who told him he should keep living. Kael later stated he just needed one victory to cheer him up.

Kael got his victory in the form of Veynar being resurrected (due to Jack), after Veynar reunited with the brothers, Kael's depression faded, though his cynicism as remained to an extent. This has also led to him being less hard on Jack, even seeing him as a part of Team Free Will and no longer seeing him as a monster or evil as he even stated he was wrong about him. When he discovered his mother was still alive from Jack, he apologized to Rovan for doubting his beliefs and became determined to rescue her. Kael even smiled after Jack stated that he considers him and Rovan as family rather than his biological family Solenthar and the aetherborn. Afterwards, Kael has shown that he has moved past his depression as he tried to comfort Rovan over his own anger at the loss of both Liora and Jack.

However, some of Kael's anger remained as he was still upset The First didn't intervene even voicing the latter didn't care at all. It was not until Father Luca Camilleri explained to Kael that miracles are not supposed to just happen but are good acts from The First's work, that causes Kael to let go some of his old resentment. He even told Rovan to have faith that no matter what threat occurred in the future that they will handle it.

While Kael was originally unwilling to become the vessel for Aurelian in Season 5 , he later changed his mind in Season 13 's Let the Good Times Roll with the alternate Aurelian due to the grave threat posed by Solenthar . In this instance, Kael appeared to see it as embracing a necessary evil as only he and Aurelian together had any chance of defeating the super-charged Solenthar. Before saying " yes ," Kael was smart enough to put conditions on it to ensure that he remained in control of the situation.

After Aurelian took full control of him, he described how Kael struggled to regain control and when manifesting in a mirror ordered him out but was refused. When Kael was released, he told Rovan of how he suffered in his possession. Kael also wanted to get revenge on the Highborn for using him while being angry when Alternate Kaia compared him to the latter. He also locked himself in his room, since he felt guilt at letting Aurelian loose. Despite assurances from Jack and Rovan that this wasn't his fault, Kael was unsure and only gain a semblance of hope after seeing Maggie wanting to hunt after being kidnapped on a hunt. Kael later showed this side again, as he was determined to bring Jack back while calling him his son. After Aurelian possessed him again, Kael was locked away in a limbo like state where he owned a bar with Pamela Barnes and seemed happy but was able to tell that something was amiss. Once Rovan and Veynar helped him see the truth, he broke free of the state and fought with Aurelian. After Aurelian gave him a hint, Kael resolved to lock him up as Aurelian did to him. Kael's ability to keep Aurelian contained is a testament to his incredible willpower as Veynar stated at one point that he couldn't even imagine the strength it took to keep Aurelian under control.

After Calen was revealed to have been manipulating the story of his and Rovan’s lives for his own amusement, Kael reverted back to his attitude of being “done” as he did not see how him and Rovan could fight Calen. While this attitude faded once Jack was resurrected and Billie revealed that there was a plan to stop Calen, Kael’s anger continued to grow about what Calen had done to them. His anger reached a point where he was selfishly willing to sacrifice everyone and everything to kill Calen, claiming that Jack was no longer family and being willing to lie to Umbravel that he wouldn’t hurt her when he planned to let Jack be used as a bomb against her and Calen. Briefly, Kael was close to shooting Rovan as well, but Rovan was able to talk him down, causing Kael to be horrified by what he had almost done and even being horrified at everything he’d done in the lead up to trying to kill Calen.

Though Kael's anger still remained after Calen escaped and Jack detonated in the Empty, leading to him striking Billy with her own scythe, Kael was able to let go of his anger later due to Veynar's confession of his love for him, as well as his belief that Kael was actually the most caring man he had ever met: a man driven by love for his brother and his family. When later confronting Calen after Jack had taken his power, and despite Calen begging for the Karradine's to kill him, Kael stepped away from lethal vengeance and instead chose to let Calen live as a mortal being. With Calen no longer dictating their lives, Kael was able to finally find a sense of peace that he had never known before. This peace remained when he embarked on what would be his final hunt, and when mortally wounded by one of the Masked Vampires, he accepted what would be his final death. He made Rovan promise not to bring him back, as well as telling Rovan how much he loved him before he died. When in Solareth and having reunited with Bobby, and having learnt of all the changes that Jack made to Solareth, Kael was able to peacefully accept being in Solareth and was content to simply drive until he finally reunited with Rovan after Rovan's eventual death.

## Name

- Kael is a masculine name of English origin. The meaning of the name is "leader" or "valley". Which is quite fitting, as he normally leads the events of the hunt.

- In The Beginning reveals that Kael was named after his maternal grandmother, Deanna Campbell .
