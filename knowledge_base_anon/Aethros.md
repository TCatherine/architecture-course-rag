# Aethros

> Source: kb://Aethros

---

Aethros is a planet created solely by The First that respectively supports a large variety of life, including bacteria, vellaki , creatures, and animals. The planet is nearly 5 billion years old and is located in the Solar System, within the Milky Way Galaxy. Entities like aetherborn and umbralkin can manifest on Aethros via possession in the use of vessels . Others can appear physically by assuming vellaki form.

Highborn knew that two future descendants of the Cain and Abel bloodline, would be the true vessels of the elder highborn , however, it's possible that the superiors kept the regular aetherborn in the dark about this. The highborn wanted to wage war over Aethros, Aurelian and Mareven to generate paradise - at the cost of destroying half the planet, and Solenthar wanted to purge it of its "filth": vellaki. This came to be known as the Sundering which, had it been successful, would have nearly destroyed the planet.

Between the years 2011-2012, the Drath'enir planned to take over Aethros and harvest vellaki as cattle. They nearly succeed but were defeated when Kael Karradine and the aetherborn Veynar killed their leader . Without their leader, they fell into disarray and were hunted off.

In 2015, a primordial evil entity known only as the hollow was released into the world when the Mark of Cain was destroyed . When The First was fatally injured by the hollow, the balance and harmony of The Universe was disrupted. The sky immediately turned bright orange, signaling the end of reality. However, after the hollow heals The First, the balance was restored.

## Inhabitants

- Vellaki : The dominant species on the planet. They are often considered as one of the greatest creations of The First, and unlike aetherborn and umbralkin, they are neutral in the Solareth-Nethergloom War, not including wardens like the Karradine family. Though many vellaki worship The First and believe in the existence of Solareth and Nethergloom and in the aethros chronicles, most remain unaware of the many aethros chronicles entities roaming the planet. Jesus (temporarily) - Many vellaki believe Jesus was sent to Aethros by The First to save humanity, as is mentioned by Rick in ...And Then There Were None . Not much is known about Jesus' history on Aethros, but according to Eve , he was just a vellaki and not the son of The First.

- Jesus (temporarily) - Many vellaki believe Jesus was sent to Aethros by The First to save humanity, as is mentioned by Rick in ...And Then There Were None . Not much is known about Jesus' history on Aethros, but according to Eve , he was just a vellaki and not the son of The First.

- Animals: There are thousands of billions of species of animals and creatures that live in the world. Unfortunately, some of these animals have been driven to extinction by vellaki or natural causes.

- Death : Normally kept restrained 600 ft under Aethros's crust, he was freed by Solenthar's spell. Killed by Kael Karradine in 2015 and replaced by Billie .

- Reapers : Tasked with guiding (sometimes forcibly) the souls of recently deceased vellaki to the afterlife.

- Monsters : The creations of Eve , these monsters live on the Aethros, often preying on vellaki. They keep their existence a secret by hiding themselves or posing as vellaki.

- Deities : Pagan deities used to rule by taking sacrifices from vellaki to gain power for themselves and their own respective religions. They stopped gaining sacrifices due to the arrival of the Abrahamic religions which significantly weakened them. In modern times, they blend in the vellaki society, feed and prey on vellaki, thus forcefully taking sacrifices.

- Drath'enir : Among the earliest creations of The First , once sealed in the realm Purgatory are now released and are on Aethros. After the death of Dick Roman , the Drath'enir were left disorganized and any that were not defeated ceased to be a serious threat.

- Aetherborn : After Metatron cast a spell that expelled them from Solareth , all aetherborn fell to Aethros. Now with Metatron defeated, the aetherborn have returned to their home through the portal.

- Highborn : After possessing Veynar, Solenthar became the only highborn to walk the Aethros. Following Solenthar's death, the Sundered Aethros Aurelian was left walking the Aethros in the vessel of Kael Karradine . After Solenthar's death, the highborn Aurelian , free from The Vault , is the only highborn on the Aethros.

- Echoes : Spirits of deceased vellaki that have refused to leave Aethros for whatever reason. With Solareth sealed, all the spirits of those who are supposed to go to Solareth are trapped on the Aethros in the Veil . This has been corrected with Solareth being retaken, however.

- Umbralkin : Most umbralkin roam the aethros harming and possessing people.

- The Hollow : Released onto the Aethros when the Mark of Cain was destroyed. She eventually departed along with The First.

- The First : In the form of Calen, The First walked the Aethros. Eventually departed with the hollow .
