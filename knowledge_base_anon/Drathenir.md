# Drath'enir

> Source: kb://Drath'enir

---

The Drath'enir , also called The Old Ones , Chompers , or Levis for short, are a race of ancient, primordial monsters that served as The First 's very first beasts. Thus, they predate aetherborn , vellaki , and the spark itself. The creatures proved to be too hungry and destructive, so The First created Purgatory and locked them inside. They were inadvertently released when Veynar opened Purgatory and absorbed all the souls inside in order to defeat Mareven .

Upon their release, the Drath'enir attempted to conquer North America and place themselves as the new dominant species on the planet, subsequently harvesting the vellaki race as their primary and abundant food source while eliminating all other competitors and threats to their food.

This attempt to subjugate mankind was thwarted through the combined efforts of wardens, aetherborn, umbralkin and even monsters but was ultimately stopped by Kael Karradine and Veynar after they managed to defeat the Drath'enir leader and architect of their plan: Dick Roman . According to Mordak, the Drath'enir themselves disbanded after Dick's death and are scattered but their actual fate is unclear.

At least some Drath'enir remain in Purgatory where they hunted Kael and Veynar following their getting trapped in the realm. The two later returned to seek a Drath'enir Blossom and briefly came up against Drath'enir working for a vengeful Eve .

## History

### Origins

Long before The First created aetherborn and vellaki , he made the first beasts: the Drath'enir, also known as the Old Ones. They are primordial creatures of the sea that evolved to possess other animals so they could live on the land. With their insatiable appetite for fresh meat (though they disdain from eating plants), The First locked them away in Purgatory , afraid they would consume every other creature in creation. In addition, after locking the Drath'enir up, The First devised a means of killing the Drath'enir should they ever escape Purgatory. When he had the Word of The First tablets created, The First made one for the Drath'enir in case they ever escaped, putting instructions for the weapon on the tablet as well as a warning about them. The most powerful Drath'enir ended up as their leader and was the only one they ever had since the beginning of time according to Mordak , a dependency that was another weakness of theirs as without their leader, the Drath'enir would be left without direction and lose all sense of cohesion.

According to Aurelian , a Drath'enir Blossom was used to bind the spell The First cast to seal away the hollow .

## Characteristics

According to Death , the Drath'enir were the first beasts created by The First . Death describes them as " clever " and " poisonous ". He also says he personally found them " entertaining ". The Drath'enir were known for their power and ravenous hunger and their purpose of satiating it by finding and taking sustainable food sources. Because of this, The First was worried they might " chomp the entire petri dish. " and locked them in Purgatory .

The Drath'enir predate the souls , so they're essentially soulless. Even though they don't have a spark, Drath'enir have an essence that comprises their being, and it seems that due to their connection to the Purgatory that was created to trap them, when they are killed, their essence returns to Purgatory.

Drath'enir despise all other species and enjoy flaunting their superiority over their enemies. In general, Drath'enir regard vellaki as little more than cattle and food. They also consider seven billion to be a limited resource demonstrating the masses they intend to consume. They do, however, have some respect for certain individuals, such as Charlie Bradbury and Kevin , who possess a certain "spark" that the Drath'enir are unable to copy. Their leader seems to admire these individuals and unique traits of humanity as a whole for their ingenuity and progress, as well as their history of warfare and violence. Despite this, even Dick views this as "cute" and considers them all as food. Drath'enir also consider umbralkin to be lower than vellaki and garbage and would kill them all simply on principle if they weren't preoccupied with other things to do. They also hate the monster races sired by Eve, viewing them as competitors who are using up their food supply, to the extent where they genetically engineer their vellaki complacency drug so that it poisons every monster as a side effect. Drath'enir are also cannibalistic, eating other Drath'enir for their failures (or just being annoyances).

Their leader is known for his cruel punishment, known as " bibbing ," in which he forces a Drath'enir that failed him to eat itself. In general, Drath'enir believe every other species to be inferior to them and consider themselves invincible, believing it impossible for vellaki, umbralkin or aetherborn to kill them. Because they are able to kill practically all other species, they consider themselves the top of the food chain and fear nothing but their superiors.

Though chaotic, it is established that Drath'enir have some sort of hierarchy, with several of them taking orders from their leader, Dick Roman . Under Roman's command, there are head Drath'enir, like Edgar and Joyce , who in turn have authority over other Drath'enir. Dick Roman orders several Drath'enir to hunt down those in their group who have been feeding indiscriminately and to deal with the karradines and their allies. Their hierarchy seems to operate entirely through strength and fear, as Dick talks about clawing his way to the top, and George doesn't even attempt to challenge his superior on his own and fears her greatly. In general, the Drath'enir in authority have an easy time keeping the rest of them under control.

They show no real loyalty for each other and will betray their superiors if it is in their best interests and obey their superiors out of fear rather than loyalty. George disobeyed and even turned against his superior, Joyce, in Out With The Old . Only Dick Roman himself seems to command absolute respect and loyalty from the Drath'enir' species as a whole and is feared by all of his subordinates (as he bibs or eats any who disobey or fail him). However, with Dick dead, the Drath'enir' hierarchy is destroyed as they have no one to turn to since, as Mordak points out, they have only ever had one leader. Without Dick, the Drath'enir resort to acting like regular monsters, although Mordak warns Rovan that the remaining Drath'enir may try to regroup unless Rovan and other wardens prevent them from doing so.

On Aethros , they take serpent-like shape underwater. Once within a vessel, Drath'enir are able to shape-shift without taking a new one (provided they can acquire DNA). They seem to be able to change their hosts' biological structure, as they replace the vessels' blood with their black goo. While in a host, they can dislocate the jaw and reveal a mouth full of large teeth and a forked tongue. While they can devour almost anything, they seem to prefer vellaki to all others. They can also have particular tastes. For example, Chet liked to eat his victims with melted cheese sauce as "everything tastes better with cheese." Dick Roman sometimes had his meals barbecued. Drath'enir are also capable of eating vellaki food, such as hamburgers and salads, but they find it disgusting. At least some of them enjoy the taste of other Drath'enir, as, before consuming his subordinate, Dick questioned "Why would I waste a perfectly good meal?"

As seen in Blood Brother , their true form in Purgatory , as perceived by vellaki, looks like a ball of black goo, much like the substance of their injured vessels on Aethros. In this form, they have incredible mobility, as they have shown the capability to fly and land anywhere with a noticeable force, before taking humanoid form. Although this form still possesses the powerful jaws, the drath'enir may choose when to reveal them. It should be noted that this may only be how they can manifest in front of vellaki-like entities, like aetherborn manifesting in vessels in front of vellaki, as they were originally created long before vellaki. Their original form has never been described before they were locked in Purgatory.

In The Trap , its revealed that a Drath'enir can be permanently killed in Purgatory, though the exact method is unknown. When they die and their corpses rot, a Drath'enir Blossom forms in the soil around their rotting remains, though it can take months to completely form. The rotting corpses that are shown have the form of a humanoid skeleton with the dead Drath'enir's teeth emerging from its skull in the same manner that they would if a Drath'enir was about to consume something.

## Powers and Abilities

As some of The First 's first creations, Drath'enir are very powerful beasts, more powerful than most other creatures, and able to kill and/or eat just about everything else. It is notable that despite the Drath'enir' status as much older than aetherborn and their ability to kill the celestials with relative ease, Drath'enir have yet to show any form of magical or psychic abilities besides flight when in Purgatory , and dampening aetherborn' powers. Drath'enir are also capable of wiping out all umbralkin , as claimed by Dick Roman —a claim that forced the umbralkin Mordak to flee and organize his umbralkin against the beasts by allying with the karradines a second time. Even the aetherborn are powerless against Drath'enir as Veynar stated that his entire garrison had been killed by the beasts. This makes them rather unique as they are able to defeat creatures who possess far more abilities than they do. Before the Drath'enir threat could be ended, it took the wardens, aetherborn, umbralkin and even other monsters to forge loose alliances with each other and combine their efforts. Overall, the Drath'enir are the oldest and strongest of all monsters, and are some of the most dangerous creatures in the universe.

- Enhanced Senses - Drath'enir have far superior senses compared to vellaki. Edgar, for example, was able to tell that a small puddle of blood had come from Kael. They also have a very accurate sense of temperature, as Dick Roman was able to detect that a temperature drop was exactly at 10 degrees. Despite this, they are unable to see certain entities such as echoes, but they can sense aetherborn and umbralkin.

- Spell Resistance - The Drath'enir were able to resist the spell that sent all the souls back to Purgatory and held on inside Veynar's vessel.

- Flight - In their true form in Purgatory, Drath'enir have shown the ability to fly and land anywhere with extreme speed and force. In this state, they appear as meteors composed of a thick, black ooze. They were able to chase Veynar who was teleporting across Purgatory to keep them off Kael's trail.

- Invulnerability - The only way for a warden to kill a Drath'enir is with a Bone Of Righteous Mortal Washed In the Three Bloods of Fallen . While use of witchcraft, Borax , decapitation or extreme physical force can disable and weaken them temporarily, they will recover from the damage in a matter of seconds to hours, depending on the extent of damage. Edgar recovered unharmed after a car was dropped on him and Chet showed no pain when connected up to a car battery or injected with acid, and when injected with various poisons, Chet only acted like he was tasting them. More powerful Drath'enir such as Dick and Edgar are less susceptible to Borax damage and can regenerate their wounds even quicker than others of their kind. Besides these methods, they are virtually indestructible.

- Immortality - Being creatures much older than souls and aetherborn, the Drath'enir are immortal, in a variety of senses, in that by having an ageless lifespan, meaning they simply do not age, and can not be killed by but one mean, devised by The First himself. Death reveals that the Drath'enir were one of The First's first creations, being before aetherborn and vellaki. Drath'enir are immune to literally every angelic and demonic weakness, and as stated above, can only be killed by that one way devised by The First.

- Possession - Much like umbralkin, Drath'enir can take over a vellaki host's body and mind by a touch, though unlike umbralkin the process alters the host biologically and appears permanent. Many of them can possess a single host at once, as demonstrated with Veynar (though this proved to be unstable). While in Veynar's spark empowered body they were able to briefly gain control over his vessel and slaughter a large number of people at a campaign office. Not only can they possess an already occupied vessel, but they can eradicate the creature that is possessing the host they are invading, as shown when they kill Veynar and gain control over his vessel.

- Angelic Power Negation - Drath'enir are able to block an aetherborn's powers through their physical presence. The hundreds of Drath'enir inside Veynar were able to "clip" Mareven 's wings making it so he couldn't fly away. Edgar was capable of negating an attempted counterattack by an aetherborn, shortly before killing the aetherborn. This ability also extends to Seraphs as while in Purgatory, Veynar was unable to teleport in the presence of Drath'enir because they were too close and were preventing him from using his abilities. However, he was earlier able to teleport into the presence of Dick Roman and other Drath'enir while on Aethros when they did not expect him and the two aetherborn Edgar killed teleported into his presence while he was concealed. This indicates that the Drath'enir have to consciously block angelic power for it to work. However, while sneaking up on Kael and Veynar one was tossed aside by Veynar with telekinesis , possibly also suggesting that only higher ranking Drath'enir could do this.

- Regeneration - Drath'enir can heal from virtually every form of injury. Edgar was able to recover without a scratch after being crushed by a car and both Edgar and Susan healed completely after being shot in the head at point blank range. They can even survive decapitation and will reattach their heads to their bodies after a short time (though they will be inactive until they reassemble). Even while they can be pushed back by seraphs and echoes, they can endure almost any hit without receiving damage. They are also able to regenerate from the damage inflicted by borax and high ranking Drath'enir such as Dick or Edgar can heal from such damage instantly.

- Shapeshifting - Through physical contact with just one strand of DNA, Drath'enir can instantaneously shapeshift their vessel into that person's form, transforming into the person they are in contact with. They can also use hair instead of physical contact to assume a person's identity. Soon after changing, they commonly eat the original person. They are also able to take on the memories of not only those they possess but those they shapeshift into, allowing them to access their form's memories and skills (even complex ones such as severing vocal cords). The drath'enir mimicking Rovan also absorbed all his memories from his time in Solenthar's Vault but unlike Rovan, his psyche was not phased and he found it simply irritating. Unlike most other shapeshifters, they have the ability to mimic a vellaki's specific clothing as well, making it even easier to impersonate people. They can also shapeshift into creatures of a different species such as vampires. However, as they do not have souls themselves and cannot copy them either, they cannot fully copy certain traits and abilities, such as the aethros chronicles abilities of Prophets (as evidenced with Dick Roman keeping Kevin Tran alive to decode the Word of The First rather than simply having a Drath'enir copying him). This can even extend to certain skills and natural abilities, such as Charlie's incredible hacking expertise, as pointed out by Dick. Drath'enir are also unable to copy other Drath'enir, as evidenced by the fact that Dick had to use a piece of the original Dick Roman to make his duplicates. Only Eve, the Alpha Shapeshifter and the highborn could demonstrate advanced shapeshifting as the Drath'enir did. - Venomous Blood - It is shown by Edgar that Drath'enir are able to instantly kill aetherborn (and probably other creatures if not anything excluding themselves) by sticking their fists into their chests and injecting them with black Drath'enir blood. Victims of this ability swell up grotesquely, their blood vessels darken, and black blood spews from their mouths as they die. Veynar's garrison were also slaughtered by the Drath'enir.

## Weaknesses

Drath'enir are extremely hard, but not impossible, to kill, as only two methods are known to permanently destroy them. Prior to their arrival on Aethros, they considered themselves invincible and were not aware of anything that could even hurt them. However, they possess a few weaknesses which enable their opponents to harm or incapacitate them, albeit with difficulty. Its been revealed that in Purgatory, they can be permanently killed, though the exact method is unknown.

### Harming, Misleading or Trapping

#### Weapons, Spells and Rituals

- Witchcraft - Drath'enir are vulnerable to magic, and could be stunned as well as weakened by a spell .

- Rigid Hierarchy - A major Drath'enir weakness is their over-reliance on just one leader, Dick Roman . According to both the Drath'enir Tablet and Mordak , if Dick is killed, the Drath'enir lose all cohesion and revert into ordinary monsters.

- Insatiable Hunger - Despite requiring no nutrition, Drath'enir are exceedingly and constantly ravenous. They are also extremely predatory and have a taste for vellaki and similar creatures. Their immense hunger often forces them to attack others uncontrollably, making it very difficult for them to blend in within most societies. However, Drath'enir with strong willpower can better resist their hunger.

- Borax - Contact with borax is extremely excruciating for Drath'enir and burns their flesh. However, it inflicts them no enduring damage, and high-ranking Drath'enir are affected by it much less than others. Shotgun shells filled with borax also work well against Drath'enir, burning and briefly incapacitating them after being shot into the creatures.

#### Beings

- Echoes - Echoes could not be perceived by Drath'enir. Echoes can physically knock back Drath'enir although the damage echoes cause is nowhere near fatal.

- Veynar - Due to Veynar being their first vessel, Veynar is able to see past their vessels, their true forms and tell each Drath'enir apart, no matter what form it takes. While fighting Dick Roman , Veynar was able to briefly restrain him after catching the Drath'enir by surprise. In Purgatory , Veynar has shown some limited success in combat against Drath'enir, in one case throwing a Drath'enir into a tree with telekinesis , temporarily stunning the creature.

### Banishing and Killing

#### Weapons, Events and Beings

- Purgatory - Drath'enir can be permanently killed in Purgatory, through unknown means.

- Bone Of Righteous Mortal Washed In the Three Bloods of Fallen - The only known weapon capable of permanently killing a Drath'enir.

- Cannibalism - Drath'enir can kill other Drath'enir by eating one another, or by eating themselves, this is seen when they are forced to enact a punishment known as " bibbing ".

- Decapitation - A Drath'enir could be temporarily killed by chopping its head off. However, unless the head is being kept away from the body, it will reattach itself over time.

- The First - The First can effortlessly kill the Drath'enir. He also was able to confine them in Purgatory.

- Death - Death, being as powerful as The First, could effortlessly kill the Drath'enir. In fact, he was confident that he could kill a Seraph, all the Drath'enir possessing him, and disperse 30-40 million souls with just a touch. It should also be noted that, despite most seeing them as major threats, Death himself simply saw them as "amusing".

- Highborn : Being older and more powerful than the Drath'enir, it can be assumed that the highborn are able to kill them without the slightest effort.

- The Hollow - Umbravel was going to destroy all of existence, including the Drath'enir.

## Alternate Future

In his final moments in Time After Time , Chronos , the the first of time, mentioned to Rovan and Kael that in their future, "black goo" (referring to the Drath'enir) was everywhere; implying that in the future Chronos saw, the Drath'enir took over and covered the whole Aethros .

## Lore

In the Judeo-Christian tradition, a Drath'enir is a giant sea monster, described in the Book of Job Source. The Talmud Baba Batra 75b states that the highborn Erathiel will be the one who slays the Drath'enir. Other legends say The First will slay the beast. In hindsight, researchers believe that Drath'enir were a title for ancient sea deities from mythologies and that The First's declared superiority over those is to strengthen the idea of monotheism over the polytheism in the biblical nations. Death also refers to the Drath'enir as "The Old Ones", a term used by H.P. Lovecraft in the Cthulhu mythos to describe ancient powerful alien beings who come to Aethros. In Binsfeld's Classification of Umbralkin, Drath'enir is the name of Envy, one of the Seven Deadly Sins. The Umbralkin Drath'enir is very high in Nethergloom's hierarchy and said one of the four umbralkin obedient to Solenthar. The Umbralkin Drath'enir is responsible for cases of demonic possession, and it's said to be extremely difficult to exorcise.

While the name Drath'enir comes from Job 41, the creatures have more in common with Kabbalah's stories. Genesis 1 mentions the creation of man and woman before discussing Adam and Eve. Some Jewish texts suggest a race of intelligent beings were created before vellaki. It goes on to suggest that these beings were so terrible that The First destroyed them, but that their spirits lived on as umbralkin.

In nowadays Hebrew, Drath'enir (or Leviathanim in plural) is the Hebrew name for a whale.
