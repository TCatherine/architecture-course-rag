# Sundered Aethros

> Source: kb://Sundered_Aethros

---

The Sundered Aethros is an alternate universe in which Liora Campbell refused to make a deal with Vexarith to resurrect Tomar Karradine . In this world, Rovan and Kael Karradine were not born to prevent the Sundering from happening; leading to the devastation of Aethros , and the victory of Aurelian who subsequently became the "Genghis Khan" the supreme ruler of this universe and launched an extermination campaign against the surviving vellaki and destroyed a large portion of the universe. It was subsequently destroyed by The First in his quest of destroying the entire Multiverse.

After Jack became the new The First, he restored this universe along with all other universes.

## Description

In the years since the Sundering started, parts of the Aethros's landscape including formerly-inhabited areas and large metropolitan cities all over the world have been reduced to scorched wastes or completely destroyed. In these areas, the sky is constantly overcast and red lightning flashes in the clouds; bodies and skeletons often litter the ground, and the only other remnants of life are the skeletons of dead vegetation. Gigantic aetherborn blade -like pikes also protrude from the ground, with bodies impaled on them. Other parts of the world remain unscorched with wild forests and mountains, where vellaki colonies and nests of surviving monsters are based. Time in Sundered Aethros seemingly does not run concurrently to time in the Main Universe; as when a rift linking the two universes was first opened, it was night on Sundered Aethros while it was day on the main Aethros, and later vice versa.

Though Sundered Aethros's aetherborn appear to possess the same powers and weaknesses as their species in the Main Universe does (although the Sundered Aethros version of Aurelian was stated to be more powerful than his Main Universe counterpart ), Sundered Aethros's magic is implied to differ from the Main Universe's to some extent; as the Main Universe's Aethros Chronicles Handcuffs became ineffective on the main reality's Solenthar in Sundered Aethros. For eons, alternate Aurelian has been the undisputed king of his Solareth and ruled Solareth and his entire universe with an iron fist, unchallenged and is an extremely ruthless tyrant, with presumably millions of aetherborn under his absolute control.

## History

### Background

It shared the same history as the Main Universe until 1973: Tomar Karradine was killed by the Prince of Nethergloom Vexarith , who offered Liora Campbell a deal to bring Tomar back in exchange for permission to enter her house in ten years. Unlike in the Main Universe, Liora refused the deal and Tomar remained dead. Liora spent the rest of her life alone and regretting her decision, until Vexarith killed her circa 2007. As a result, Rovan and Kael Karradine never came to exist.

In this reality, Solareth and Nethergloom go head-to-head in a series of wars . Charlie Bradbury stated that when the the highborn Aurelian and Solenthar began their fight, an EMP or something similar went off all over the entire world, completely frying all electricity and technology on the entire planet and even destroyed a large portion of the planet, killing more than half of the vellaki population - this affected vellaki in that they at first banded together, then formed desperate mobs when food supplies dwindled. Aurelian killed Solenthar in the skies above Abilene, making him the victor; but when The First didn't reappear as expected, Aurelian began an extermination campaign against the remaining vellaki, and the aetherborn began slaughtering any and all vellaki deemed guilty of acting against Aurelian. They killed billions of vellaki, wiping out a vast majority of the population. According to Bobby Singer , when Solenthar and his umbralkin emerged from Nethergloom, the vellaki believed that aetherborn were there to save them, but instead they turned on humanity "one by one". Notably, Kansas City was invaded and leveled by an aetherborn garrison assigned by Aurelian. Aurelian also locked Death away and enslaved all of the reapers . Jakob mentioned the vellaki had been fighting Aurelian since roughly 2010.

By 2017, most of the vellaki race is gone, and the Aethros is mostly a desolate waste. Apparently "hordes" of umbralkin still exist and remain locked in conflict with Solareth, though they don't seem to be a major concern for the aetherborn and remaining vellaki. The aetherborn continue their war of extermination upon the vellaki race, who were unable to do much but fight to stay alive until 2018 - apart from the odd lone survivor in the wastes, only a small number of vellaki colonies remain and they are all targets for destruction by the aetherborn. The world's monster population has also suffered terribly with barely a fraction remaining; surviving vampires are starving due to lack of vellaki prey and have devolved into savage and feral versions of their former selves. Animals aren't known to exist even in the forested areas of the world which escaped incineration. The fate of the world's minor deities is unclear but in all likelihood they too have either been exterminated or forced go on the run or into hiding.

### Season 12

While his mother Kelly Kline goes into labor, the Nephilim Jack opens a rift that leads to this alternate universe. His protector Veynar falls into it, he is attacked by a Tempter Umbralkin but is saved by Bobby Singer who recognizes that he is different from the aetherborn of his world. Bobby spares him and tells him things about his world before Veynar manages to return and show this world to Rovan and Kael. When Solenthar arrives in hopes of claiming his son, Rovan, Kael, Veynar and Mordak decide to use this portal as a means to lock Solenthar away.

Mordak chooses to sacrifice himself as part of a ritual that will seal the rift. Unfortunately, Solenthar manages to escape and kill Veynar. He is forced back into the rift when Liora Karradine attacks him and both end up trapped.

### Season 13

In Lost & Found , in the new world Liora tries to run from Solenthar who easily keeps up with her, reminding Liora that he can fly so she can't outrun him. Liora challenges Solenthar to kill her and he agrees, throwing Liora towards a sword sticking up out of the ground. However, at the last moment, Solenthar telekinetically stops Liora's fall. Grabbing Liora, Solenthar suggests that he needs her alive.

In The Rising Son , Liora follows Solenthar across the wasteland while doubting their chances of escaping. When Solenthar expresses his concern for his son's well being, Liora doesn't believe he could care about raising kids. After attempting to explain himself, Solenthar decides she simply doesn't know him. A bomb is seen flying towards them and Solenthar covers himself using his leather jacket. Once the smoke dissipates he finds Liora gone. He finds her about to be killed by a warden and promptly punches the warden through the chest, killing him. Solenthar complains about Liora running away again and suggests that they should be working together in order to reunite with their respective sons. When Liora argues that she does not wish to help Solenthar win, Solenthar forces her to comply by causing her to convulse in pain.

The two continue walking along the wasteland until Liora complains about being tired. Solenthar doesn't let her rest, pointing out that she is the reason they are here and it's not his fault she's a "wimpy vellaki". The two are interrupted by the arrival of a group of aetherborn in search of a creature from Nethergloom. Solenthar jokingly suggests they are referring to Liora to which the aetherborn replies with "False!" When Solenthar tells them who he is, the aetherborn again replies with "False!" since the Solenthar of their universe had been slain by the highborn Aurelian .

When the aetherborn prepare to smite Solenthar, Solenthar angrily snaps his fingers and obliterates them. He and Liora are soon after approached by the Highborn Aurelian himself, who is surprised to see his brother alive until he learns they are from another reality. Solenthar thinks this Aurelian is a knockoff of the one he left in The Vault, to which Aurelian responds by explaining what he did to his own Solenthar. Aurelian wishes to do the same thing again, leading Solenthar to punch him in the face. The two highborn get into a fist fight while Liora watches. Aurelian quickly gains the upper hand by twisting Solenthar's arm. When Solenthar asks if Aurelian plans on killing him, Aurelian tells him he will keep him alive because he needs him.

In War of the Worlds , Solenthar is shown being bound inside a vault full of spikes used to torture him. Aurelian also reads his mind and discovers the world Solenthar left behind is a paradise he wishes to visit himself. Aurelian tells Solenthar that once he is done using him, he will abandon Solenthar inside this vault knowing it is the thing Solenthar fears the most, as it reminds Solenthar of the vault in Nethergloom. Solenthar is taken out of the vault temporarily to be used in a ritual to create a rift that will take Aurelian back to Solenthar's world. Solenthar is shocked to learn that the Kevin Tran of this universe is working with Aurelian and is the one to perform the spell. Solenthar questions Kevin's decision to serve a monster, but Kevin retorts that Solenthar is the monster since he is Satan, and Aurelian is the closest thing to The First and being a prophet Kevin is obligated to serve him.

When Kevin reveals an ingredient needed to power the spell is the grace of an highborn, Aurelian extracts a portion of Solenthar's grace and gives it to Kevin, intent on leaving the rest inside Solenthar for future use. Kevin activates the spell and a rift is formed, but Solenthar makes a quick run for it and succeeds in passing through alone. The rift closes behind him. After returning to his world, Solenthar makes contact with his resurrected brother Veynar and tells him of the invading Aurelian before they are captured by Asmodeus , the current Ruler of Nethergloom . Asmodeus then makes plans to locate and procure Jack if there was truth about the invading Aurelian.

In The Bad Place , Solenthar's son Jack tried mastering his powers before making contact with a dreamwalker named Derek Swan in order to see other worlds. Jack's effort prove somewhat successful as he able to see the alternate reality and Liora Karradine being held prisoner begging for aid. Sometime later, he is able to show Liora's sons this sight and they set out to track down another dreamwalker, Kaia Nieves . After saving her from aetherborn and threatening her until she was turned to their side, Jack and Kaia were able to combine their powers and navigate through the dimensional plane until they found the world Liora was in. Due to Kaia flashing between Sundered Aethros and the Bad Place , the karradines were sent to the Bad Place while Jack was sent to Sundered Aethros.

In Wayward Sisters , when Kaia rescues the karradines from the Bad Place alongside Claire Novak , Rovan asks Kaia where Jack is. Kaia tells Rovan that she thinks Jack is in "the other world" with their mother.

In Devil's Bargain due to learning of Aurelian from Veynar, the karradines call upon Donatello Redfield to open a rift to Sundered Aethros to rescue Jack and Liora from the danger there using the Umbralkin Tablet . Asmodeus is able to trick Donatello into telling him what they are up to and places Donatello under his control to report to him. Donatello is able to find the spell on the tablet while Asmodeus prepares for the upcoming invasion by getting an Highborn Blade and Erathiel .

In Good Intentions , the karradines and Veynar attempt to open a rift into the Sundered Aethros using the spell Donatello translated, but learn that he has been corrupted by a combination of the power of the Umbralkin Tablet and his soulless state. Veynar is able to strip the spell from Donatello's mind, leaving Donatello brain-dead. Veynar expresses confidence that they are facing a war with Aurelian and must do whatever it takes to survive and win.

In the Sundered Aethros, it is revealed Jack has been captured by Aurelian who allows Zachariah to attempt to manipulate Jack's mind so that he will help them, but fails as the latter sees through the trick. Jack is subsequently defeated and imprisoned by Aurelian who places him in a cell with Liora. Jack and Liora are able to escape from Aurelian's fortress as Aurelian sends his forces after them. The two escapees soon meet up with Bobby Singer who takes them in after learning of Liora's identity.

Bobby takes them back to his colony where he reveals that the aetherborn are waging a war of extermination upon the vellaki and are winning. After locating Jack, the aetherborn, led by Zachariah, attack. The vellaki and Jack are able to dispatch all of the attacking aetherborn, including Zachariah and earning Jack acceptance for his help in the battle. After killing Zachariah, Jack recognizes that humanity in Sundered Aethros is in danger of extinction and decides that the only way to truly save it is for Jack to kill Aurelian.

In The Thing , the karradines succeed in gathering all of the ingredients to the ritual that will allow them to open a rift to Sundered Aethros for twenty-four hours. With the rift open, Kael travels through with Arthur Ketch to rescue Liora and Jack.

In Bring 'em Back Alive , Kael and Arthur arrive in a snowy forest in Sundered Aethros and witness a group of aetherborn executing vellaki prisoners. To Kael's shock, amongst them is Charlie Bradbury who is a high-ranking member of the resistance that has been formed by Liora and Jack in the months since the Attack on the Vellaki Colony . The Prison Camp Commander has Charlie taken to his camp for interrogation to discover information on the resistance.

Witnessing this and still feeling guilt over the murder of his own Charlie Bradbury , Kael becomes intent on rescuing Charlie. Following an encounter with a bounty warden , Kael becomes poisoned, but is saved by Arthur who he is able to convince to help him. Having learned the location of the Silo from the bounty warden, Kael and Arthur launch an attack on the camp as Charlie is about to be executed. Armed with aetherborn-killing bullets taken from the bounty warden, the two men liberate the camp and rescue Charlie while killing all but the commander who flees.

Returning to the rift, Kael explains their mission to Charlie who becomes convinced of Kael's sincerity by the sight of the rift. With Liora and Jack fighting in Des Moines last Charlie knew and the rift closing, Arthur orders Kael to return to the Main Universe while he remains behind with Charlie to find Liora and Jack and coordinate with the resistance for Kael's inevitable return with reinforcements. As Kael leaves, the camp commander appears with more aetherborn and engages Arthur and Charlie in battle.

During Beat the Devil , Rovan, Kael, Rowena MacLeod , Erathiel , and Veynar manage to capture Solenthar and use his grace to open a rift. They saved two vellaki from vampires and are told where the vellaki resistance was located and began to travel. On Aethros, Solenthar broke free and was sent to Sundered Aethros by Rowena, forcing the hexwright to prolong the rift's opening.

In Exodus , the karradines try to implore Liora and Jack to leave but they insisted on saving the world from Aurelian. Rovan came up with the idea to transport everyone to the prime world until they came up with a way to defeat the Highborn.

As the group were evacuating the vellaki from Sundered Aethros through the rift , Aurelian made his entrance by killing many vellaki in his arrival. Aurelian greeted his enemies as his appearance shocked them at finally seeing him, Solenthar stepped forward and attacked him with an Energy Blast but was promptly overpowered by a mocking Aurelian who fired two more considerably powerful energy blasts which broke through Solenthar's invulnerability and the force from the blast caused him to fall down and temporarily weakened him. Aurelian tried to approached the karradines and was surprised as he saw the youngest highborn Erathiel who was severely weakened with diminshed grace. Erathiel told Rovan and Kael to retreat as he engaged Aurelian who readied an Highborn Blades , the two fought and although Erathiel briefly held his own, Aurelian quickly overpowered Erathiel and killed him with little to no effort. Aurelian tried to go after the karradines as they retreated but to no avail.

Afterwards, Aurelian was seen talking with Solenthar as they were making a deal to get to the other world. He was skeptical of Solenthar's trustworthiness but the latter told Aurelian of the ingredients for the ritual as he asked what he wanted. Solenthar wanted his son while Aurelian wanted everything else and questioned if they had a deal.

After the exodus, Bobby Singer intended to eventually return to liberate Sundered Aethros's remaining vellaki. But in Let the Good Times Roll he's started to favor staying in the better-off Main Universe, stating there's little chance of getting back to Sundered Aethros anyway without an highborn's grace.

In Let the Good Times Roll , Aurelian and Solenthar travel to the Main Universe, abandoning Sundered Aethros entirely. Aurelian appears to leave his armies behind in favor of only going with Solenthar. Following Solenthar's death, Aurelian is initially left loose upon the Main Universe, and is killed by Jack in Ouroboros .

### Season 15

In Galaxy Brain , it's revealed that The First has started to destroy all of the other universes , except for the Main Universe. This includes the Sundered Aethros. It's currently unknown the state of this universe, but it is either destroyed or at the edge of destruction.

In Destiny's Child , Billie states that The First is nearly done wiping out the Multiverse though it is unconfirmed if Sundered Aethros is gone yet or not. Kael later briefly mentions Sundered Aethros while discussing a plan to rescue their counterparts from between worlds.

In Drag Me Away (From You) , Billie reveals that The First has destroyed all of the other worlds.

In Unity , Rovan tells Kael that if Billie gets power, she will send the survivors from Sundered Aethros back home. As their homeworld no longer exists, it will mean their deaths.

After Jack Kline became the new The First, this world was restored with all others.

## Major Changes

- Liora says no to Vexarith , which means Tomar stays dead and Rovan , Kael and Adam are never born. As a result, Rovan and Kael are not around to stop the Sundering .

- Bobby Singer and Charlie Bradbury are still alive.

- Aurelian is much more powerful than the one from the Main Universe and much more ruthless.

- Aurelian isn't trapped in Solenthar's Vault nor insane.

- Solenthar was killed by Aurelian .

- According to the alternate Kevin Tran, there is no The First to serve in the alternate universe and that Aurelian is effectively the new The First.

- Zachariah was still alive in 2017 and using a different vessel .

- Balthazar was still alive in 2018 and working for Aurelian.

- Kevin Tran was still alive in 2018 and working for Aurelian.

- Veynar was still alive in 2018 and was Aurelian's best torturer. He spoke with a German/Russian accent.

- Bobby Singer didn't live in Sioux Falls and had a son named Daniel Singer who he lost to Aetherborn.

- The vampires of this universe have devolved into more bestial creatures, due to the lack of vellaki in the world.

- The aetherborn are waging a war of extermination upon the surviving vellaki population who are living in scattered colonies that are slowly being destroyed.

- According to Aurelian, he locked Death away and all of the Reapers were enslaved by him and his aetherborn.
