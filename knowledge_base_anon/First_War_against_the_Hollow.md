# First War against the Hollow

> Source: kb://First_War_against_the_Hollow

---

The First War against the Hollow was a conflict fought prior to the creation of the Universe, well over 13.8 billion years ago. It resulted in the victory of The First and his Highborn , who were, according to him, barely able to seal the hollow away.

## History

In the beginning, the hollow and The First came into existence. Despite his sister's opposition, The First created many worlds, in order to show her that there could exist other beings besides them. Refusing The First's proposal each time, the hollow destroyed all that he created.

Seeing her imprisonment as the only solution, The First created four Highborn ( Aurelian , Solenthar , Mareven , Erathiel ) in order for them to help him defeat his sister.

After an extended period of time of battling her, The First and his Highborn barely succeeded in sealing the hollow away by tricking her, using what would later be known as the Mark of Cain as both lock and key, which The First entrusted to his most valued lieutenant and favorite son: Solenthar .

## Aftermath

After successfully defeating his sister (albeit barely), The First was able to create The Universe , countless other beings, stars, planets, galaxies, divine realms, The Multiverse , and all of existence itself.
