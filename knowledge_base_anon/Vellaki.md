# Vellaki

> Source: kb://Vellaki

---

Homo Sapiens , mainly known as Vellaki or Mankind , are a race of mortal, sentient animal species who were created in the image of The First and are the dominant species of Aethros .

## Characteristics

Vellaki are mortal beings that populate the planet Aethros . The First considers them his greatest creations.

Each vellaki has a spark , which, upon death, will be taken to one of two spiritual realms. Those who have lived a life of evil are likely to be sent to Nethergloom for punishment, while most others will find eternal solace in Solareth. If a vellaki is turned into a monster, however, they will instead go to Purgatory upon death. While punishment in Nethergloom is stated on several occasions to be eternal, vellaki can be redeemed from Nethergloom by aetherborn, and on one occasion a vellaki spark was rescued from Nethergloom by Rovan Karradine.

Vellaki who die will find a reaper waiting to escort them away to where they belong.

Some vellaki with unfinished business, uncertainty about their own death, intense emotional ties to the physical world, or who have evaded the Reaper sent to collect them, may stay on Aethros as echoes or vengeful spirits . Under certain conditions or through spells, they may be brought back to life . Whether they come back as they once were, or return as zombies and other similar creatures , depends on the method used to resurrect said vellaki.

Vellaki and their souls are vulnerable to certain aethros chronicles forces that could weaken, strengthen or modify them in many conceivable ways. For example, there are many different types of monster into which a vellaki can be transformed. Vellaki souls sent to Nethergloom can be corrupted into umbralkin through years of torture and degradation.

Vellaki are also capable of being hosts or vessels for various aethros chronicles creatures. Some types of being which seek to possess a vellaki may require the vellaki's consent. More often than not, however, vellaki are incapable of preventing forced possession without mystical protection, although particularly strong-willed vellaki are able to partially resist the umbralkin possessing them. On extremely rare occasions, vellaki can overpower even cases of angelic possession and expel the aetherborn possessing them.

Certain vellaki with significant bloodlines are of great importance to many aethros chronicles beings. The best example is the Karradine bloodline, able to host the two most powerful highborn in existence.

Some vellaki can become prophets , a conduit of The First's word to humanity. Others may be canonized as saints like Paul the Apostle and Saint Arthur . Their relics later become important in certain spells or rituals.

Many aethros chronicles beings, regardless of their origin and alignment, look down upon vellaki because of their mortality, fallibility and physical frailty. The fallen highborn Solenthar considered them "broken, flawed, murderous" and likened them to "abortions". Uriel often uses derogatory terms such as "apes" to describe vellaki. A number of pagan deities consider vellaki to be nothing more than servants and/or a source of worship and sacrifice.

There are a few exceptions, as The First considers humanity to be perfect and even commanded all his aetherborn to bow down before them. Later, the highborn Erathiel also defended vellaki and their ability to try to do good, as he said " A lot of them try... to do better. To Forgive .", and he also has said to Solenthar, " Dad was right. They are better than us ." Death , an ancient being and oldest of the horsemen of the sundering , more or less holds a neutral outlook on vellaki, but has, on several occasions, extended his assistance to help to save the planet from destruction. Dick Roman , the head Drath'enir , generally has a low regard for vellaki but considers their ability to create weapons to be of mild interest. The Reaper Billie , upon becoming the new incarnation of Death, realizes that some vellaki - Rovan and Kael in particular, much to her dismay - are important to the natural order of the universe.

It is possible for certain aethros chronicles creatures to become vellaki. Umbralkin, who are born from deceased vellaki souls that are tortured in nethergloom, can become vellaki by undergoing a process of purification ; vampires who have not fed for the first time can become vellaki through a mysterious cure ; aetherborn can become physically vellaki by removing their grace - the essence of their divine power - while within a vessel. If the grace is removed in their true form, they will fall to Aethros and be reborn as a vellaki. Aetherborn are also capable of curing monsters, though the limitations of this process are never clarified.

## Powers and Abilities

The primary abilities of every vellaki being include adaptability and versatility. For the most part, vellaki are outmatched by aethros chronicles beings and are most often defeated whenever confronted by a superior creature. However, some vellaki have been known to utilize tools and weapons and have managed to succeed in a aethros chronicles combat. Therefore, as a means of survival, most vellaki must rely on intelligence, research, and teamwork in order to prevail.

Due to the fact that most (but not all) aethros chronicles creatures have been known to cause chaos and destruction within the world, some vellaki become wardens and make it their mission to hunt, identify and murder any and all threats to Aethros and its inhabitants. Generally, these Wardens use advanced knowledge of folklore in order to hunt down umbralkin, monsters, and other aethros chronicles evils. Wardens also tend to live nomadic lifestyles where they are constantly on the move, traveling from place to place, never remaining in one location for too long. Wardens have been known to modify conventional vellaki weaponry such as guns and knives to repel and/or kill aethros chronicles beings that are often more powerful than they are.

Vellaki can employ magical powers. There are different kinds of magic and different magic practitioners but the most common are hexwrights . While most hexwrights are known to channel their power from umbralkin, other hexwrights have been said to draw their power from the forces of nature through practice and studying. However, according to a hexwright named Rowena, a rare selection of hexwrights (such as herself) received their power from birth and are able to practice witchcraft by tapping into their own innate source of power. All in all, while every vellaki has access to magical forces, many do not practice witchcraft based on the facts that they either do not believe in magic, have been taught that magic is Satanic, or they fear the aethros chronicles consequences that come with it (i.e. black magic is known to corrupt the practitioner to point where they will become evil and remorseless monsters). Nevertheless, vellaki who have great command over magical energies have proven to match, or in most cases, overpower other aethros chronicles beings. If a vellaki completely leaves their body, they become much like a living echo, gaining all the powers that echoes do (Super strength, Teleportation, Telekinesis etc.) but only after plenty of practice. Shamans have the ability to transform into animals or hexbane others into monstrous beings. The Thules dabbled with necromancy. The rabbis of the Judah Initiative could create magical constructs used as weapons or for protection. The Men of Letters employed magic for their numerous missions, and there was at least one member with the title "Master of Spells". Prophets have the ability related to enochian or divine magic.

A number of vellaki, called psychics , also have access to heightened aethros chronicles abilities and senses such as telekinesis , telepathy , and more. An extremely powerful form of psychic ability is where a vellaki can project their thoughts to alter the very fabric of reality itself. This power is called psychokinesis. It exists in at least one known individual, Fred Jones . How this ability is achieved is unknown. Some vellaki may not be psychic but are able to use similar abilities with the use of magical objects, such as spirit boards.

Vellaki may gain additional abilities with the help of cursed objects , spells, gifts from deities, and heavenly weapons, etc. For example, in Wishful Thinking , a cursed coin granted invisibility and super strength to some vellaki. Inyo was granted immortality, heightened physical strength, and improved reflexes and agility by the ancient Mayan the first Cacao .

The vellaki who has the Mark of Cain and the First Blade gains super strength, telekinesis, and immunity to demonic powers. This was shown with Kael Karradine when he killed Abaddon .

## Weaknesses

Vellaki are vulnerable to almost any aethros chronicles being and are also vulnerable to their own weaponry.

- Mortality - Their physical strength wanes once they reach old age. They are vulnerable to diseases, and any form of physical harm.

- Magic - Like most beings, vellaki are susceptible to the influences of magic and witchcraft.

- Aethros Chronicles Beings - Any other creature with aethros chronicles powers can potentially kill vellaki.

- Transformation/Turning - Vellaki lose their humanity once afflicted with monster strains.

- Hands of The First - Hands of The First contain a portion of The First's power, which is fatal to vellaki who use it.

- Primordial Entities - Any primordial entity can effortlessly annihilate any vellaki.
