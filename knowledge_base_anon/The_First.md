# The First

> Source: kb://The_First

---

The First , also known as Light , The Lord , Yahweh , Christo , and many other names, including his alter ego " Calen Vorath ", is one of the two main antagonists (alongside Solenthar ) of the Aethros Chronicles franchise . He is portrayed as the overarching antagonist in the CW television series, appearing as a supporting character in Season 4 and 5 , the central protagonist of Season 11 , the ultimate antagonist of Season 14 , and the main antagonist of the final season . He stands as the archenemy of Rovan and Kael Karradine .

He is an ancient primordial being who created the Universe and nearly everything it contains, including the vellaki spark . Umbravel , his twin sister, is the only entity surpassing his power and, prior to his demise, Death comparable to him in power. Together, The First and The Hollow serve as the balance in the Universe, as the dark cannot exist without the light and vice versa. If either one of them is lost or destroyed, existence itself would cease. His sister couldn't resist destroying whatever The First created, so The First created four highborn and, with them by his side, waged war against her and eventually locked her away with a mark which he entrusted to his most loved and valued aetherborn, Solenthar . However, over time, after The First created the Universe, the mark corrupted Solenthar and drew him to rebel when The First created humankind . After Solenthar was bound in Nethergloom and Gadreel was locked away in Solareth's dungeon , The First left Solareth and went into seclusion on Aethros .

After thousands of years, The First eventually reappeared and partially aided in The Sundering . After The Sundering was averted, he disappeared again. Six years later, when his twin sister Umbravel was released, The First was originally willing to let his creations be wiped from existence and let Umbravel take over. But after being convinced by both Metatron and the karradines , The First eventually decided to help stop Umbravel. After being fatally injured by his sister in a failed attempt to stop her, The First was then healed by her and they both departed the Aethros together.

The First later returned to Aethros once he received Veynar 's prayer, The First revealed that he had no method in helping with Jack Kline and opted to have them kill him instead. However, Team Free Will saw through his ploy and accused him of manipulating events, resulting in him personally killing Jack and plunging the world into chaos to punish humanity for their defiance. However, during the confrontation, he was shot by Rovan with The Equalizer , leaving The First in a weakened and diminished state. He has since recovered and is at full power. Unknown to The First, Billie has taken the opportunity to resurrect Jack and help the young Nephilim become powerful enough to kill The First which is revealed to be a plan developed by Adam over the course of 300,000 years. Using Jack to kill Calen and Umbravel turns out to be another one of The First's plans to get the karradines to kill each other, although it ultimately fails.

Following his final attempt at getting the karradines to kill each other, The First merges with Umbravel and begins wiping everyone from existence, planning on resetting the universe. The Karradines are left with Jack powerless and most of their allies gone, including Billie who was planning to kill The First in order to take power for herself. However, they are left with Calen's Death Book , which reveals how The First can be killed after the first attempt failed. The First is later stripped of his power by Jack, rendering him mortal and making Jack the new The First . The Karradines and Jack choose to leave Calen to live out a mortal life, eventually to age, sicken and die, alone and forgotten. As Rovan continued to get older, so did Calen. By the time Rovan died, it is unknown if Calen also died, or if he is somehow still alive.

After Calen's defeat and Kael's death, it was discovered that Calen had left behind a failsafe in case of his defeat: the Akrida , a race of monsters that Calen had created to wipe out everything. However, with the help of Kael's intervention, the Akrida were destroyed by the Monster Club , foiling Calen's last plan.

## Personality

The Hollow describes her brother as being prideful and egocentric, saying he created other things because he wanted to "be big" - although The First admits this is true, he had also hoped to change her nature by showing her there could be things other than themselves, but he later deemed this as stupidity and naïveté on his part. When faced with the opportunity to reconcile with Solenthar , if he'd simply apologize for locking him away, Calen opted to put all the blame on Solenthar in front of the karradines to save face. However, shortly after apporting the karradines away, he revealed that he'd only punished Solenthar because he hated himself for what his actions had done to his favorite son and apologized. After billions of years of observing his creations and living among them, his disappointment caused The First to become somewhat cynical. He considered vellaki nature to be toxic and was willing to let Umbravel destroy everything seemingly out of spite against vellaki , for fighting wars in his name and then begging him to fix everything, and generally not taking responsibility for their failures, as well as Aetherborn out of disappointment of their actions. He also considers his creations to be experiments and also revealed that he abandoned the aetherborn and humanity because they'd disappointed him. He later admits to Kael that it's more than disappointment: while he'd once taken an active role in his creations' lives, he realized that he was hindering them more than anything and he needed to let them find their own way like a parent with their child. Despite everything that's happened since he did so, The First believes he made the right decision. However, following the revelation that Calen views Rovan and Kael, and seemingly his creations generally, as story characters for him to manipulate and observe for his own entertainment, much of this has been implied to be a façade masking his true colors. Solenthar suggested that Calen's apology to him was merely because Calen needed his help against the hollow which, given what has subsequently been seen of his personality, is likely true. However, there are some ideas that even Calen finds too disgusting to include for his characters, as shown by his genuine disgust at the idea of him being responsible for the relationship between Kael and Umbravel.

Alternate Aurelian suggests The First creates other worlds and abandons them once they fail him in a way. While it has not been confirmed whether this is true, his dismissive reaction to Rovan questioning him on it may suggest there is some truth to it, though at the same time he did seem offended when Rovan appeared to believe Aurelian, but whether it was a faked reaction to keep his true intentions concealed remains unknown.

Despite his sister's claims that he encouraged religion, which she called "monuments to his ego", The First often asks people not to refer to him as "The First", and to use the moniker "Calen" instead. He also stated that people bowing before him had always made him feel "deeply, deeply uncomfortable". When Metatron did this upon seeing his true form, The First pulled him up and asked him not to do it again since it made him uncomfortable. However, The First is fully aware of his nigh-omnipotence and does not hesitate to point out that he could do things most beings could not, as he simply said that he is The First when Rovan questioned on how he simply snapped his fingers to cancel Jack's influence and insisted that the Equalizer could kill anything. The deity Fortuna states The First created her kind, as while he was angry humanity didn't honor him, he saw a purpose for the deities to serve as the fall guys for natural disasters. Despite his immense power, however, The First is shown to be rather capable of taking potential threats seriously, as he not only indicated with apparent concern when Rovan stated he feared what Jack could become that it was true but even though he could have continued manipulating Rovan and Kael by just letting Kael spare Jack, he insisted on Jack being killed and even did it himself. Jack himself admitted the reason why his grandfather chose to kill him was because he was scared he could be able to kill The First.

At first, despite his disappointment and cynicism, The First apparently still maintained a level of compassion and care for his creations deep down and it was just buried by the resentment he felt over how both vellaki and aetherborn had failed him. This was proven by how he appeared to be worried about Veynar as he was about to be killed by Mareven and patted him on the shoulder to comfort him in his rebellion, resurrected Veynar on three occasions, despite what he had done, secretly helped Kael in averting the Sundering , and being pleased at how Marie put his books in a play. Even at his worst with everything that had happened caused by the aetherborn , with particular disappointment towards the highborn , as evidenced when Calen strummed faster in an irritated manner just from hearing Metatron ask him to write more on them, and vellaki, where he at first allowed Umbravel to destroy them, he appeared to show some feelings of genuine regret and sadness when Metatron confronted him for leaving even despite bitterly stating that they had disappointed him and was ultimately moved when Metatron revealed the flaws of The First's approach with a moving speech of humanity's better traits into finally taking action in order to save an infected town from his sister. He was even willing to surrender himself to Umbravel so that his creations would be safe, showing a lack of concern of what would happen to him that baffled even Kael. He also comforted Kael on his background with his father but told him not to compare him to Tomar . Even though he had bitterly resented Solenthar for his rebellion, even lashing out when Rovan and Kael asked for his rescue, he still showed a level of guilt and remorse for his fall, as he sadly said Solenthar wasn't a villain despite having been earlier irritated by Metatron's reminder of Solenthar's rebellion , and even appeared to have been willing to forgive him after he decided to fight alongside him, healing him of his injuries in their reunion and doing everything to talk him out of the spite he felt towards him, even eventually apologizing.

Like Death , The First seems to have a taste for what Kael calls "cheap food". He also likes taking long showers. The First was described by Metatron in Sacrifice as "Larger than life, gruff, a bit of a sexist. But fair, eminently fair". This fairness seems to be shown by how The First, while willing to forgive Metatron for his offences, refused to give back Metatron's powers. Despite mainly writing all that Rovan and Kael experienced, including their thoughts even when they were thinking of negative things, The First did seem to have limits to what he wanted to let know on in his books, as he chose to not let Rovan's addiction be known to prevent him from appearing unsympathetic.

He has a humorous side as when asked by Metatron as to why he hid himself, he explained that he wanted to see everything while being in plain sight and admitted he enjoyed acting as a vellaki to fool those around him.

While not easy to anger, once enraged, his anger is considerably large as he can easily be cold, violent, insulting, and even somewhat sadistic, as shown by how after being outraged when Metatron called him a coward, The First swiftly responded with fury, throwing him out of the bar and coldly telling him that while Metatron and the rest of the aetherborn may consider Metatron's position as the Scribe of The First to be special, to The First, Metatron was never anything special, just the closest aetherborn to the door, and took time to show Metatron of how a town is going to be destroyed by the fog unleashed by Umbravel, relishing in his devastated expression. Later on, this rage is shown when Kael and Rovan asked Calen to rescue Solenthar. At which, Calen, frustrated by their insistence, immediately expresses his bitterness of Solenthar's disappointment. Although Metatron was actually gleeful on how he managed to make The First angry and Solenthar showed no qualms in angering The First; Rovan and Kael, having never seen The First in a rage, were visibly shocked and intimidated enough to be put to place swiftly and not talk on it anymore. His rage is well-known even though he has only been seen by a few aetherborn, to the point that the usual petty and arrogant Zachariah was terrified enough at the prospect of The First unleashing his wrath upon him to immediately back down from pursuing Rovan and Kael. At first, when Rovan, Kael, and Veynar called him out on manipulating their lives and defied his will, The First, although clearly angry at their defiance, tried to reason with them to make them do it and offered to bring back Liora for Rovan and Kael in exchange and when they persisted, The First merely responded by giving them a cold smile and personally killing Jack (his own grandson) painfully, appearing to not find them worthy of unleashing his anger fully, but upon being shot by Rovan with his own created gun, The First lost control of his anger and mercilessly declared the end for them, punishing them and the world by starting The Sundering again. Notably, despite their defiance of him, all three of them expressed genuine fear when The First raged at them. Later on, when Rovan and Kael once again managed to stop themselves from killing each other, Calen displayed great rage at them for continuously defying him, even physically slamming his hands together as he raged on how his best efforts were for naught and coldly telling Kael screw you when Kael told him to screw off. The First also did not hesitate to destroy Aurelian for his brief betrayal but this was only after he thanked him for giving him a warning about the karradines.

In the time since Rovan and Kael have turned on him, Calen has shown himself to be in actuality incapable of viewing anything besides himself and his sister as anything more or less than toys, which exist to entertain him and for him to manipulate as he wishes; a trait consistent with sociopaths and psychopaths. He's shown a callous side to humanity since opening the Rupture to Nethergloom ; when he smote Umbravel's masseuse for no reason, and massacred an entire casino and forced the sole surviving staff-member to continue pleasing him and serving drinks under the subtle threat of doing the same to her. It also turned out that Calen sent the spark of Kevin Tran to Nethergloom instead of Solareth despite the latter doing nothing to deserve that, and only let the souls of Tomar Karradine and Bobby Singer into Solareth because he favored Rovan and Kael at the time. He also bestowed good luck on Rovan and Kael, preventing them from suffering normal things but took it away after they fell out of favor. One of the most apparent examples of Calen's lack of empathy was when he decided to wipe out every alternate universe he'd created except the Main Universe, committing the most evil and terrible act any Aethros Chronicles villain has ever successfully enacted (though Umbravel and Alternate Aurelian previously threatened to do the same or similar). He even walked out of the RadioShed with a smile on his face before destroying Aethros 2 . Calen later reveals that the main reason that he decided to destroy his creations was because he had lost all hope and expectations in them: He had tried his best to find contentedness and happiness in his creations and the karradines, but in his eyes, no matter how hard he tried, they only continued to disappoint and defy him. Later on, he decided to exterminate humanity just to punish the karradines and Jack by forcing the trio to live on an empty planet and ruthlessly killed Aurelian despite him having turned to his side for his early betrayal. This lead to his deserved ending at the hands of Jack and the karradines, which is in irony what he had tried to inflict on the karradines and even worse: He is left as a powerless mortal who can get sick and eventually die, with no hope of ever regaining any sort of power, and will live the rest of his vellaki life forgotten and alone with no one caring about him.

Calen asked his sister to go with him to another universe where they can start over. However, his actions were a ploy for her to heal his injury and restore his power. He was disappointed at her refusal and criticism of his treatment to the world though his attempt to get her to leave with him might've been genuine. After being severely weakened and diminished by Rovan's shot with The Equalizer , Umbravel realizes that The First's now terrified. However, she sees that he is still the petulant and narcissistic being he always was while Umbravel has changed, she wants nothing to do with her brother, accepting coexistence with him but not wanting to be anywhere near him while doing it. After a talk with Becky Rosen , he appears to have overcome his fear and put in writing an ending to Rovan and Kael . He showed a sadistic side and thought of ways of getting to the ending. This is further seen when he banished Becky and her family not too long after revealing his status as The First to her and taking pleasure in her grief, though he did say he hadn't just killed them (possibly planning to create a story using them, or showing genuine mercy). He appears to express some genuine sympathy towards his sister when she breaks down, having believed Kael really cared for her, and reveals that he still hasn't given up on creating, wishing to create something new together and establish a new balance.

According to Metatron's annotated notes on the Umbralkin Tablet , The First has a secret fear that is always there and which he only shared with his favorite. Though this is believed to be Aurelian as Solenthar was locked away at the time the tablet was written, it may also not be given The First's numerous worlds and multiple variations of Aurelian.

When The First attempts to cut into Rovan's shoulder and remove the thing that is tying them together, he hesitates and visibly grimaces. Rovan realizes that despite his great power, The First actually doesn't like to get his hands dirty and prefers watching others do his work for him. While The First dislikes getting his hands dirty, when he was confronting Rovan and Kael after killing Aurelian and everyone else on Aethros, he decided to get his hands dirty to make them suffer, and showed no hesitation in beating the two to a pulp.

The First later reveals to a RadioShed clerk on Aethros 2 that none of his other worlds bring him the joy of the Universe, his original creation. This is primarily due to Rovan and Kael who The First is shown to respect the fact that they challenge, disappoint and surprise him constantly. The First calls them "the ones" before deciding to destroy every other universe to get rid of all of his distractions.

True to Umbravel's claim that Calen created things because he wanted something else to be bigger than, it's indicated Calen enjoys lording over and feeling superior to others. Calen expressed frustration at Rovan's persistent defiance in particular, when he unsuccessfully tried forcing Eileen to remove the Equalizer bullet. He later showed a similar attitude when Rovan and Kael once again foiled his plans for them to kill each other over Jack's sacrifice, frustratingly commenting on how stubborn and broken they were and likewise showing irritation when he commented on how Veynar of the main universe was the only one who defied him. Before destroying Aethros 2, Calen visibly reveled in the RadioShed clerk's desperate groveling, petting him on the face in a master-to-dog like manner and assuring the clerk everything would be fine. He also shows extreme arrogance, laughing at the idea of Jack killing him and believing that he cannot be beaten even while not able to read his Death Book and opting to beat the karradines hand-to-hand, continuing to do this despite being able to effortlessly kill them in order to truly break them, which ultimately lead to his complete defeat as the power released by both his brutal attack on Rovan and Kael and his murder of Aurelian made Jack impervious to The First's previous ability to smite him easily and he was subsequently effortlessly overpowered and drained of all his powers in moments. The deity Fortuna stated The First was initially outraged that humanity refused to acknowledge his benevolence and created the Deities to serve as fall guys for when things go wrong.

After being defeated and having his powers drained by Jack, Calen was speechless to see that he had been outsmarted by the karradines. Calen showed no ill will to being defeated, complementing the karradines for their ingenuity, stating that's why they are his favorites. He even asked that they be the ones to kill him now that he was mortal, since it would be an honor to die by their hands. However, it turns out that Calen is actually afraid of having to live as a mortal and be forgotten and alone, as he was visibly horrified when Kael and Rovan reveals that their revenge will be Calen having to live a mundane existence as a vellaki and when he dies, no one will care or remember him and was swiftly reduced to groveling and begging them not to leave him to live a mundane existence as a mortal, to no avail, showing how Calen is so desperate to have his way with things that even if he loses, he still wants to go out in a way he wants and completely breaks down when he is refused it. Furthermore, during his fruitless begging, he never said even one word of apology, indicating his lack of shame or remorse for any of his misdeeds, only pleading for them to help him, showing how Calen is so unashamed of his crimes that he still has the gall to ask for the help of those who he had earlier tortured and revealing he can be reduced to being a total coward while still not feeling any remorse and only concern for himself, showing his egomania. In turn, the karradines are shown to no longer consider him worthy of their time or any kind of a threat as they simply ignore Calen and drive away.

## Characteristics

While his twin sister Umbravel is darkness and nothingness, The First represents light and being.
He is one of the two fundamental components of reality itself, while the other one is his sister. If one of them dies, reality itself would cease to exist.

## Physical Appearance

Fortuna : Yeah, that's him.

The First's true visage has only been seen by a few beings, but it appears to be similar to the appearance of an aetherborn's grace - a blinding white light - though unlike an aetherborn's true form, The First's real visage has no ill-effects on vellaki. As seen in Alpha and Omega, he takes the appearance of a smoke of light. Only his twin sister, Umbravel has seen The First, as well as the Horseman Death , since they have existed alongside The First from nearly the beginning of time. Anna Milton once mentioned that, out of all the Host of Solareth , only four of them have seen The First (most likely the four highborn ). Additionally, although he does not see him, it was originally assumed that the aetherborn Joshua talks to The First, however it is later revealed to be the other way around. Joshua says that he thinks The First talks to him because he (Joshua) can sympathize for The First, as a fellow gardener but also because The First gets lonely.

When The First wanted to physically communicate with Rovan and Kael , he took the form of a vellaki and assumed the role of a prophet named Calen Vorath. Calen's appearance is that of a man in his late 30s to his early or mid-40s, with a beard and messy hair. He has light blue eyes and is rather short in height. The First considers this form "a gift", and describes it as "super cute". When Kael described The First to Fortuna , she recognized him, suggesting that The First has used this form for millennia.

## Confirmed Creations

The First created the universe and nearly everything that resides in it. These include:

### Realms

- Unknown Worlds - The First created other worlds before creating the universe.

- Solareth - The First's divine domain for noble and righteous souls, as well as aetherborn.

- The Universe - Some time after The First imprisoned the hollow, The First created the Universe. Aethros - The First's very last masterpiece, according to Solenthar .

- Aethros - The First's very last masterpiece, according to Solenthar .

- Alternate Universes - The First has created more universes than the main one, so many that he has lost count.

- Purgatory - The First's prison for monsters, specifically the Drath'enir.

- Nethergloom - The First's prison for evil souls, umbralkin , Solenthar, and other dangerous creatures. Solenthar's Vault - The First's prison created specifically for Solenthar.

- Solenthar's Vault - The First's prison created specifically for Solenthar.

### Creatures/Beings

- Highborn - The first and greatest of all celestial beings; their power and formation is of primordial creation.

- Deities - The pagan gods of vellaki mythology whom The First created to take the blame when things went wrong, and because they made for epic stories.

- Drath'enir - The first and most rancorous beasts, whom The First locked in Purgatory to prevent them from wreaking havoc on the rest of creation.

- Aetherborn - Celestial beings that are weaker than highborn and serve as the soldiers of The First.

- Hellhounds - Intended to be "Creator's best friend," but proved to be too vicious. The First intended to exterminate them all until Solenthar rescued one .

- Vellaki - The First's most beloved creations.

- Souls - When The First created vellaki, he gave them a spiritual essence (the spark) that also serves as a powerful energy and makes vellaki unique.

- Akrida - One of The First's final creations which he made as a failsafe to destroy all of Creation in case of his defeat.

### Other

- The Word of The First - The First ordered Metatron to write down the Aetherborn, Drath'enir, and Umbralkin tablets as instructions for his creations.

- Purgatory portal - A portal created by The First to act as an escape hatch for any vellaki trapped in Purgatory .

- The First's Bar - The safest place in all of creation, and The First's hiding place from the hollow.

- Mark of Cain (on Umbravel) - The lock and the key to the hollow' prison, which became the very first hexbane. However, the hollow claims to be the "Original Mark," indicating that the Mark wasn't created by The First but was always a part of the hollow, and The First transformed it into a sort of seal to lock her away.

- The Equalizer (destroyed) - A gun created by The First to kill Jack Kline , which has the same effect on the user as it does the being who is shot. It is revealed that the gun is powerful enough to cause serious harm to The First himself even with a shoulder wound. Lilith later destroyed the Equalizer at The First's request.

- Rupture to Nethergloom ( sealed ) - A tear or rupture in the Aethros leading into Nethergloom intended to release all of the souls and umbralkin of Nethergloom upon the world. Created after the karradines refused to kill Jack and located in Harlan, Kansas . The rupture was described as not quite a portal, but rather a tear in the fabric of the Universe that connects Aethros and Nethergloom, as if "The First stamped his foot and Nethergloom ripped open." Rovan , Kael and Rowena MacLeod managed to seal the rupture by use of the Sanetur Acre Vulnus spell.
