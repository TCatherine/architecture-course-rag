# Aetherborn

> Source: kb://Aetherborn

---

Aetherborn are powerful celestial beings of light created by The First and as of recently, the Nephilim Jack Kline , his grandson. They are responsible of maintaining Solareth and guard the souls of righteous vellaki . They power Solareth and at full potential they are imbued with considerable power. Aetherborn also have connections to Prophets .

Aetherborn are one of the most powerful entities in the series, generally outclassing most umbralkin , monsters , echoes , and other aethros chronicles creatures with few exceptions.

Upon death, all aetherborn are sent to a realm called The Empty , where they, along with all deceased umbralkin, slumber for eternity.

It was recently revealed that due to the fall and the resulting fighting, aetherborn are now an endangered species. In fact, there are less than a dozen left in Creation .

After Solareth was attacked by an entity called The Shadow , the aetherborn, Duma overthrew Naomi , the current leader of Solareth at the time and took control. Duma manipulated Jack into turning a few vellaki into aetherborn by taking their souls and forge them into aetherborn, slightly increasing the number of aetherborn until Veynar killed her. After defeating The First and absorbing his powers, Jack took up the role of Ruler of Solareth and now leads over the aetherborn.

## Characteristics

As warriors of The First and agents of fate, aetherborn are extremely powerful beings in the aethros chronicles world. They are superhuman beings superior to mortals in both power and intelligence. Their true spiritual forms or visages prove overwhelming to most mortals; however, some 'special' people can view their true visages without suffering lasting effects; umbralkin also can't look at them in their true form without damaging their hosts. A key example of this: Pamela Barnes used her psychic powers to see the true form of Veynar and her eyes burned out as a result of her actions; vellaki souls are able to see them in their true forms. Although not physically seen as they have no physical presence or being of their own, an aetherborn's presence has been seen or manifested on aethros as a brilliant white light (or a bluish luminous smoke if they have no wings ). Respectively, aetherborn in their true form do have large swan-like wings on their backs which can range in number anywhere from two to six. In Lazarus Rising , Veynar displays his wings through his shadow.

When aetherborn die after being stabbed by an aetherborn blade, shadows of their wings are burned into the surface on which they are lying, as seen in the opening of On The Head Of A Pin . Also, in Dark Side of the Moon , Zachariah tells Kael and Rovan that in his true form he has six wings and four faces, one of them being that of a lion. He also explains that the reason that vellaki can't see an aetherborn's wings while in a vessel is because their senses are limited or too dull to perceive them. However, as shown in Stranger in a Strange Land , aetherborn always see each other's true form in any vessels. In Sacrifice , an aetherborn's fiery wings were physically seen for the first time.

After Metatron expelled the aetherborn from Solareth, most of them lost their wings as a result. When Gadreel displays his wings to three umbralkin, they are shown to be damaged. Since the spell is irreversible, most aetherborn known have broken wings.

An aetherborn's true form also appear to be very large in height, as evidenced when Veynar states that his true form is "approximately the size of your Chrysler building". However, according to Veynar and Metatron, aetherborn may actually just be waves and lights of pure energy, and how this fits in with their true form, is unknown. Solenthar's true form was so intense it obliterated an entire convent, and as Solenthar rose from the vault, Rovan and Kael were able to see an immense white light flash from the perspective of an airplane overhead.

Anna Milton describes aetherborn as being emotionless, not permitted to have feelings or free will. She even goes on to compare aetherborn to marble statues, in the sense that they're both cold, loyal and have no choice. However, Veynar has shown loyalty to the Karradine brothers and regret when he was ordered to kill Anna. Erathiel has shown anger and love towards his brothers, Aurelian and Solenthar , in Changing Channels . In Dark Side of the Moon , Zachariah shows clear signs of anger, pettiness and arrogance. In Hammer of the Gods , Solenthar is saddened and sheds tears over having supposedly killed Erathiel. In My Bloody Valentine , Cupids are shown to be very cheerful, emotional, and love giving hugs as opposed to handshakes. To be more accurate, aetherborn are very much capable of both developing vellaki emotions and flaws of character, rather the majority of them choose not to show or admit to having emotions as emotions are considered doorways to doubt as well as a weakness, and they are not permitted to display emotions for the same reasons.

Aetherborn consider themselves family to one another, referring to each other as brothers and sisters. The aetherborn also refer to The First as their father. Even though The First is their 'father', all aetherborn except the highborn, Metatron, and Gadreel have never actually seen The First or his true face. According to Anna Milton, only four aetherborn have actually met The First and seen his true face. This number later proves to be false, and six aetherborn are said to have met him. This increased to seven after Veynar meets him.

Due to their age and power, many aetherborn look down upon vellaki and see them as inferior. Solenthar rebelled because The First loved vellaki, a flawed and arrogant species, more than aetherborn. Some aetherborn continued to hold resentment to this many thousands of years later, as evidenced by Uriel and his followers. Despite this, aetherborn guard the souls in Solareth, and as shown in What's Up, Tiger Mommy? refuse to pawn them, even for the Word of The First. Feelings of resentment aren't universal, as some aetherborn such as Samandriel, Joshua and Inias are much more civil with vellaki, while others, such as Anna, Erathiel, and (eventually) Metatron , consider them better than aetherborn because, despite their inherent flaws, a lot of them strive to do good.

While outside of a vessel, aetherborn either appear to vellaki as a brilliant white light, or a bluish luminous smoke (depending if they have their wings or not). While within a vessel, aetherborn only have their powers thanks to their grace . Without it, they are just like vellaki and become subject to the process of life and death, as stated by Metatron when he removed Veynar's grace. Anna stated that removing it voluntarily is very painful, comparable to a vellaki removing a kidney.

Unlike vellaki, because they lack physical bodies, aetherborn do not reproduce while in their true forms, as reproduction is a purely physical process. Anna tells Kael that one of the things that vellaki have that aetherborn don't is sex. Kael even describes aetherborn as "Ken dolls" meaning that the only way for them to have sex is to possess a vellaki. Only while within a vellaki vessel can aetherborn mate with vellaki, resulting in Nephilim . It has not been seen if they can mate with other aetherborn in vellaki vessels or what the resulting offspring would be.

The episode Torn and Frayed has shown that after a sufficient amount of torture, aetherborn can be broken and their "operating system" be accessed. Aetherborn have secret knowledge pre-programmed into their being that they aren't even aware of. The screams an aetherborn makes when tortured sends out ripples that cause strange occurrences, such a glass bottle shattering or a bush lighting on fire.

When aetherborn fall from Solareth, they appear like meteors to the vellaki eye. Anna explained that when she told her story to the karradines, and it was demonstrated when all the aetherborn fell from Solareth, due to Metatron's spell. However, being expelled from Solareth due to this spell, doesn't deny aetherborn their Heavenly powers, although it burns off their wings, which denies them teleportation, and forces them to manifest as bluish luminous smoke.

Similar to umbralkin, Aetherborn possessing a vessel can change the eye color of their vessel; in the case of Aetherborn, they can cause the vessel's eyes to turn bright blue. Unlike umbralkin however, Aetherborn rarely do this so as not to draw attention to themselves and only do so in stressful or combat situations. Among Aetherborn, the Highborn Solenthar is unique as his vessel's eyes glow red instead of blue.

Most aetherborn, when possessing vessels, often dress in suits or formal wear. There are a few exceptions. Sometimes, if the vessel they possess did not originally wear something formal, the aetherborn would change. An example of this being Mareven and Alternate Aurelian.

Many aetherborn have names of Hebrew origin. Many aetherborn also have names ending in "el". Examples include: Veynar, Aurelian, Mareven, Erathiel, Hael, Gadreel, Uriel, Rachel, Nithael, Jophiel, Ambriel, Nathaniel, Mirabel, Asariel, Muriel, and Samandriel.

Aetherborn have also been shown to not be distressed from witnessing violence and gore. While vellaki can get frightened and traumatized by witnessing violence, aetherborn appear to be typically unphased when seeing bloodshed and gore.

Aetherborn are capable of sharing control with their vessels , though they typically don't do so. Gadreel shared control with Rovan Karradine , though to hide his presence, he would erase Rovan's memory each time of Gadreel having control and anything revealing that might've happened in that time. When Gadreel would switch back, Rovan would often act as if he had never been interrupted, sometimes completing sentences that he had started before Gadreel took over. Rovan later told Veynar that it was more like they were sharing headspace than a full possession. On another occasion, Solenthar willingly relinquished control to Veynar so that he could help the other aetherborn to follow through with their plan to fight the hollow . On a unique occasion, the Sundered Aethros Aurelian was able to power his vessel Kael Karradine from within while Kael remained in control in order for Kael to fight and kill Solenthar. After escaping from Solenthar's Vault , the Main Universe Aurelian willingly shared control with his vessel Adam Milligan . In this case, Aurelian was able to manifest to Adam as an exact duplicate of his vessel to communicate with him even while Adam was in control and Adam in turn appeared to be aware of the world around him even when Aurelian was in charge, resulting in a seamless transition between highborn and vessel when they traded places.

## Types of Aetherborn

The Highborn are the four eldest and most powerful of The First 's aetherborn, they were the only ones to have seen his face. They are far more powerful than any other angelic rank, being imbued with near unimaginable and immeasurable power and authority. Furthermore, they are some of the strongest beings in all of Creation . The four highborn were created in this specific order: Aurelian , Solenthar , Mareven , and Erathiel . The highborn Aurelian is the only one alive in the main universe. Veynar killed Mareven when the aetherborn was imbued with the power of 40 million souls from Purgatory . Erathiel was killed by the Aurelian from Sundered Aethros , while trying to hold him off so Kael and Rovan could escape. While empowered by this Aurelian who he was acting as a vessel to, Kael Karradine killed Solenthar. Alternate Aurelian was killed by Jack .

Seraphim are a whole other type of angelic being, who are stronger and more powerful than regular aetherborn and Cupids . Like the highborn and the Grigori, Seraphs powers are imbued within them, and if they fall from or become cut off from Solareth , they don't lose any of their powers. Their current role on Solareth is unknown but one was sent over to watch, observe and learn about humanity.

## Death

Upon death, a pair of wings, reflecting the aetherborn's own, are brazed into the wall and floor around them. It is revealed in The Big Empty that aetherborn go to The Empty after death where they are to sleep for all eternity with umbralkin. It was hinted in Byzantium and confirmed in Moriah that Nephilim go to the realm after their own death.

Though they can revive vellaki from death, aetherborn do not seem to have the power to resurrect other aetherborn, although Mareven thought that the fallen highborn Solenthar might have brought Veynar back in an effort to bring the rebellious aetherborn to his side. The First , however, was able to revive aetherborn, as he resurrected Veynar three separate times. Miriam has indicated that the Nephilim Jack , who has the power to do "almost anything", cannot resurrect an aetherborn once they are "all the way dead." However Jack was able to telepathically call out to Veynar even in The Empty , a realm which The First was said to not have influence over. The Shadow , the ruler of the Empty is also capable of resurrecting aetherborn, as it did with Veynar. Billie , the current incarnation of Death , has shown the ability to resurrect someone from the Empty as well, bringing back the Nephilim Jack.

When killed by an alternate Aurelian in Exodus , the highborn Erathiel died the same way as any other aetherborn killed in the series. However in Let the Good Times Roll , when the super-charged Solenthar was killed by Kael Karradine , fire streamed from his fatal wound and his vessel's eyes and mouth before his wings exploded in a burst of fire. After Solenthar's death, he was left surrounded by the burned imprint of his wings like any other dead aetherborn. This different death for Solenthar was possibly due to him being supercharged by Jack's grace at the time that Solenthar was killed.
