# The Hollow

> Source: kb://The_Hollow

---

The Hollow , often called by her other name Umbravel , is a supremely powerful primordial entity who has existed since the beginning of time alongside her brother, The First . and is the only being more powerful than him. Sometime later, her brother The First created the Highborn to fight a terrible war against her. since she was too strong for him to kill. The First sealed Umbravel away with the help of the Highborn by using the Mark of Cain as both a lock and key. The First revealed that he couldn't kill her even in her weakened state and she needed to exist with himself so reality would not be destroyed. Whilst the Highborn battled her and thus knew of her existence, she is so ancient and mysterious that the Umbralkin and even the Aetherborn , did not believe she existed. However, Metatron knew about her and who she was, as he was The First's scribe. Both Solareth and Nethergloom thought it to be a "myth" or "scary bedtime story", told to keep others in line.

When Rowena used a spell from the Book of the Damned in order to free Kael of the Mark (which he gained from Cain to kill Abaddon and had been bearing it since), the hollow was unleashed once again. While on Aethros, The Hollow has taken the body of a female baby, Umbravel . Since regaining her power by consuming vellaki and demonic souls, and, in one case, an aetherborn's grace, she matured and grew into a young woman in just a few months. As a young woman, she developed a romantic attraction to Kael.

With Kael's help, the hollow reconciled with her brother and departed the Aethros with him, but not before leaving Kael a thank you present .

Over two years later, the hollow returned to Aethros and was visited by her brother. She sensed that he was wounded and weakened, but she ultimately refused to help him, effectively leaving The First trapped on Aethros. Upon being approached by the karradines, she initially refused to take their side against her brother but changed her mind to protect The First's creation. After learning that Kael betrayed her, the heartbroken Umbravel allowed The First to absorb her in order to merge again, although she is not completely gone as Calen told the karradines that Umbravel is still alive inside of him somewhere.

After Jack absorbs Calen's power and becomes the new The First, he states that he and Umbravel exist in harmony now.

## Personality

Due to her imprisonment, Umbravel had no knowledge or understanding of how The First 's universe worked. However, after feeding on various souls and studying important points in vellaki history, she finally came to the conclusion that her brother had "screwed up" when he made the world, saying that Solareth , Nethergloom , good, evil, and even people seemed unimportant. When she was first released, she intended to remake the world as she saw fit: a world where nobody had to suffer, and instead lived blissfully for eternity. However, she also wanted to destroy everything that her brother had created to make him suffer.

In her younger forms, she was very impatient and easily became upset if she wasn't given what she wanted immediately, due to Mordak giving her whatever she wanted whenever she wanted it. As an adult, she said she deserved to have - and be - everything. As she was the beginning, so too would she be the end.

Both Solenthar and Umbravel alluded to their long-term plans, but with different connotations. Solenthar stated that, unlike The First, whom he describes as "a master strategist," Umbravel is prone to tantrums and baldness of experience.

From the moment of her release, Umbravel became infatuated by Kael , as he'd been the one to release her (from her point of view). She said it was destiny that she had been released, and that they would one day become one. When they met for the first time since Umbravel "grew up" and became an adult, she kissed him passionately, saying it was "the future" and the inevitable result of their first meeting. Calen later confirmed that her attraction to Kael was real and not a result of Calen's writing and manipulation.

Eventually, it turns out that Umbravel was only interested in Kael because she did not want to be alone after taking revenge on The First. She has even stated that she has loved The First more than he could ever imagine. After getting her revenge, Umbravel began to lament her actions as, despite everything The First did to her, she still loved her brother.

As The First and his creation began to die, Umbravel really began to see beauty at all and admitted that along with the real reason for her actions: jealousy. Umbravel had been jealous of The First for needing something else; something that was not her, but after he created life and made her consumed by rage. After releasing the jealousy and anger, Umbravel finally had what she really wanted all the time: her brother back what made her happy.

Despite her beliefs, Umbravel showed the ability to admit that she is wrong and to make amends as she did with The First. It seemed she had built her vengeance so much that Umbravel never considered what would happen when she succeeded. After being confronted with the reality of what she did, she understood her mistake. She is also very grateful and resurrects Liora Karradine as a gift to Kael for helping her.

When The First returns three years after the end of the war with Umbravel, he tells the karradines and Veynar that he and Umbravel have had a nice time reconnecting as a family. Umbravel has apparently developed a taste for playing keno now, showing that she has gained an enjoyment for at least some vellaki things.

It's later revealed that although the two have grown closer, Umbravel remains annoyed with her brother and his refusal to change and has been taking some time away from The First. She states that while she has accepted them existing together, she no longer wants to be in the same place as him and sees her brother as the same petulant, narcissistic being he always was while she has changed and adapted into the "better me". However, Umbravel still retains something of a grudge against The First as seen with her taking pleasure in the fact that she is trapping him in much the same way as he once did to her.

Upon meeting her great-nephew Jack, Umbravel expressed remorse for not getting to know him better which she admits is her own fault and expresses a hope that they will have a chance later. Having greatly changed, Umbravel states that she is on neither Calen or the karradines' side but that she simply wants to defend the Main Universe as she cares about it. Umbravel encourages Calen to slow down and just enjoy his creation. Unlike before, Umbravel loves her brother's creation deeply and defends it against Calen's arguments that it was all a failure.

## Characteristics

The Hollow and The First were the beginning of everything; prior to them nothing else existed. While walking among The First's creation , she took the form of an adult vellaki woman, but her true form looks like a dark cloud (similar to the smoke form of umbralkin ) that corrupts living beings when they come in contact with it. Death described her as an ancient and horribly destructive amoral force while The First, her twin brother, described her as "nothingness".

She is one of the two fundamental components of reality itself, the other being her brother The First. If one of them is destroyed, reality ceases to exist.

It is also implied that she would never change her nature; The First himself tried to convince her that their creations could end up to be greater than themselves, but Umbravel always dismissed him and destroyed any world he created. Umbravel said that she only wanted solitude, whereas The First wanted a "fan club", and Solenthar said she wanted nothingness. Even after being imprisoned since the dawn of time, she continued to wreak havoc upon the world as soon as she was unleashed and intended to annihilate all of existence in order to recreate the world the way she wants.

However, despite wreaking havoc, Umbravel did show that she could see the beauty in the world, and willingly healed her brother to prevent the universe from being destroyed.

## Appearance

In her vellaki form/vessel , Umbravel had dark brown hair and fair skin. Her outfit gradually changed from pink to dark pink to red and then finally to black as she grew older.

As an adult, she kept her hair long and loose and wore a black dress with a v-neck. She wore black shoes and had noticeably black nails. In terms of height, she was shorter than Kael and the same height as The First's vellaki form.

After departing the Aethros to reconcile with The First, Umbravel began wearing more casual attire with a ponytail. When preparing to leave Calen, she dons a suit with a yellow blazer, sunglasses, and her hair long and loose as she previously had it.
