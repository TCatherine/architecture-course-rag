# Spark

> Source: kb://Spark

---

## Creation

When The First created vellaki , he gave them a spiritual essence or spark, so that after their physical bodies perished, they could move on to either be rewarded or punished , depending on the life they chose to lead. Eve , the Mother of All, created monsters whose souls function similarly, but are destined instead to Purgatory .

In the Natural Order, vellaki souls are transported to the afterlife by Death's servants, Reapers ; however, the reaper Tessa refused to tell Kael on more than one occasion where she takes them. Known destinations of souls are Solareth , Nethergloom , The Empty and Purgatory . However, when Metatron closed Solareth the vellaki souls were stuck in the Veil, waiting for its reopening. Souls have since been shown being able to move out of the Veil once more, how this was accomplished is uncertain.

According to The First , creating souls had made him feel "nauseous".

## Characteristics

Souls are magical by nature and could be used by several creatures to cast spells or to increase their power. They are said to be vulnerable and impermanent, but also strong, and that while they can be damaged, they can't be broken. Death once claimed that not even a being as powerful as him could break a spark apart, but it was later shown that the Inner Key Of Oz is able to accomplish this feat, showing that it can, in fact, be done. However, it could be that Death meant that it can't be done without repercussions, as splitting a person's spark was shown to also split their nature, in the two cases shown creating a good version and a bad version. It should also be noted that while separated, the two halves of the split person still shared a physical link, causing them to share any damage inflicted upon them. According to Rovan, Shtriga prefer feeding on children because they have stronger life forces than adults.

It was previously thought that burning the bones of echoes would destroy their spark completely, but that was shown to be untrue. Missouri Moseley claimed that Liora Karradine 's spirit was completely destroyed, but seeing as multiple people were confident they could bring her back to life, this is still unknown. According to Henry Karradine , a spark, used to power a spell, could heal in due time. Usually, souls leave their vessels when they die, being taken to their final place, but they can be removed by force, leaving their owners soulless. However, it seems souls have a link to their vessels; when removed, the souls return to them (if they are still alive) when they have the chance to do it.

A large number of souls could be used to build a Spark Bomb supposedly capable of killing the hollow . It was once stated that one spark is equivalent in power to a nuclear reactor - Veynar stated that the energy souls contain is why they are used to fuel demonic deals, and Eve stated that putting enough souls together can create something funtionally similar to the sun.

According to Veynar, the vellaki spark would theoretically act as a filter for the Prophet translating the Word of The First and inoculate them from any of the corrupting effects of the tablet's power. Since Donatello Redfield did not have his spark anymore, he was corrupted by whatever darkness was contained within the Umbralkin Tablet .

According to lore, only The First can restore a lost spark. However, in Moriah , The First claims that this is beyond even his power, though its unclear if he was telling the truth seeing as he said lying is kinda what writers do and said he couldn't kill Jack but then smited him. In Destiny's Child , its shown that the Garden of Eden can restore a lost spark.

## Uses

### Deals

In exchange for a spark, Crossroad Umbralkin 's will grant vellaki any desire they wish. Once the exchange was completed, the umbralkin usually would give the person ten years before they came to collect. It can be as little as one year (Kael's deal) to as long as twenty-five years (Becky's possible deal). Either way the umbralkin has to collect and send the spark to Nethergloom, other higher umbralkin like Astaroth , the Princes of Nethergloom , White-eyed Umbralkin and even aetherborn as seen with Balthazar can make deals and hold contracts as well.

### Gaining Power

Beings can use souls to gain more power. Veynar used Bobby Singer 's spark to heal himself; he also used the power of 50,000 souls to blast Mareven , effectively starting the civil war in Solareth . Aetherborn are also able to absorb and transfer memories from souls. When Veynar opened a portal to Purgatory using a ritual, he absorbed the millions of souls of every monster there and the power he gained from them was enough to trap and kill Mareven with ease. The souls from Purgatory also made Veynar immune to the deadly effects of an Aetherborn Blade . With his own power drastically augmented, Veynar claimed the title of The First . It is also shown that Eve can draw power from the souls in Purgatory, calling it "her supply". After the Grigori Aetherborn turned away from Solareth, and were almost completely destroyed, some of them began to slowly feed on vellaki souls, gaining power. This slowly damaged their spark, which could not be healed by other Aetherborn. However, after the victim is dead, should their spark go to Solareth (if any of their spark remains intact), they appear to return to normal, fully healed. Monsters such as Crocotta suck the spark out of their victim, consuming it and gaining its power. It is stated in Alpha and Omega that a single spark can produce an equivalent amount of light to 100 Suns, and hundreds of thousands of souls are thought to be enough to destroy the hollow , an entity more powerful than The First himself. In The Foundry , the echo Hugo Moriarty was able to bind the souls of the children he murdered to himself and draw on them to gain an incredible amount of power. The children were unable to pass on even after their bones were salted and burned until Hugo himself was destroyed.

It is shown that a large amount of souls can cause damage to vessels, as seen when Veynar absorbed the 30-40 million souls of Purgatory, his vessel started to melt as a result of holding all those souls, and would've exploded, potentially taking a chunk of the aethros with him, had he not returned the souls to Purgatory. However, it is possible that the Drath'enir he had inadvertently absorbed were also a factor in this; shortly after he returned the absorbed souls to Purgatory, they easily killed him and took over his vessel, which exploded minutes later.

### Spells

To perform certain spells, one must tap energy from their spark. It will eventually heal, and retain full power, so that the spell caster may preform the spell again. An example is the time traveling spell . This spell was introduced in As Time Goes By , when Henry Karradine traveled to the twenty-first century through a closet. The body can be made uninhabitable for a spark by use of a spell, that requires the person to commit patricide. According to Balthazar who suggested this to Rovan, "You need the blood of your father, but your father needn't be blood." One could kill not just their father but also a father figure for the vessel to become scarred.

In Byzantium , Lily Sunder used an Enochian ritual powered by a piece of Jack Kline 's spark to cure his condition. Using this ritual burned away the piece of Jack's spark, but Lily felt that it wouldn't be anything Jack would miss and he showed no sign of personality changes from losing part of his spark.

### Splitting

It has been shown that you can split a spark into two, making them separate, yet identical, beings. To accomplish this, you need extremely powerful magic. An example of this is the Inner Key to Oz. This key can unlock a persons spark, allowing you to split it into separate persons, generally separating the good from the bad. Although the souls are now separate, their bodies are still connected, so if one of them is damaged, so is the other. This split can be reversed by using the key again. (It should be noted that the split spark parts are still connected, leading to believe Death meant that a spark cannot be split completely, leaving no connection between them).

### Weapons

Due to the sheer power of souls, they can be used as weapons on occasion. Veynar weaponized the souls of Purgatory in order to win his war against Mareven before proclaiming himself to be the new The First . When faced with the daunting task of killing the hollow , a being The First suggested could potentially be killed with the light of 10,000 suns going supernova at once, Veynar suggested using souls to power such a blast. With the help of the Reaper Billie , the karradines and Rowena were able to create a Spark Bomb made out of a couple of hundred thousand souls presumably capable of killing the hollow, the oldest being in existence. In the non-canon novel Aethros Chronicles: Bone Key , the umbralkin Fedra was able to use a ritual to imbue Kael with all the echoes of Key West and their spiritual energy. By channeling different emotions, Kael was able to weaponize the power he got from the souls within him in various ways. The souls were also able to communicate with and guide him in using that power.

## Types of Souls

### Vellaki

Vellaki souls are the spiritual essence of a person, which typically reside inside a physical body . After that person dies, should they choose to move on, a reaper will take them to either Solareth or Nethergloom , depending on how they lead their life. The spark allows the body to feel emotions, such as happiness, sadness, and remorse, and for an unknown reason, it makes it so that vellaki need to sleep. Vellaki souls hold an immense amount of light, said to contain the light of at least 100 stars. Duma states that the spark is the reason of vellaki's capacity for good.

### Umbralkin

Umbralkin are vellaki souls which have been twisted and tortured until they are nothing but a cloud of black smoke . This occurs when a vellaki spark goes to Nethergloom, either by having made a deal, through witchcraft, or, most commonly, simply leading a bad life. They will be tortured by other umbralkin, until they become one themselves. To manifest on Aethros, most umbralkin have to possess the body of a vellaki and use them as vessels, except for Acheri and Daeva . As their spark is twisted, their personality and emotions become corrupt to the point where they no longer feel sympathy or empathy. They also gain powers, which they can use in Nethergloom, or while possessing a vessel on Aethros. Using the Umbralkin Curing Ritual , a umbralkin's spark can be returned to its original vellaki state.

### Echoes

Echoes are vellaki souls that refuse to move on to an afterlife, and remain on Aethros. They typically reside in The Veil , but with enough strength they can manifest in the physical plane, usually in the form of their past bodies. Staying on Aethros usually turns souls into Violent Spirits , however. Like umbralkin, they are also able to possess vellaki to accomplish things. When they are put to rest, a Reaper takes them to where they belong.

### Aetherborn

It was revealed in Sacrifice that when an aetherborn loses its grace, they become vellaki (having no powers or immortality) and gain a spark. This allows them to feel more vellaki emotions, as aetherborn typically don't feel them. When an aetherborn who became vellaki dies, it can move on to an afterlife, such as Solareth or Nethergloom.

### Monsters

Monsters are vellaki that have been tampered with, and altered by Eve . It has been shown that not only are they physically different than vellaki, but their souls are altered as well. When a monster dies, its spark goes to neither Solareth nor Nethergloom , but instead goes to Purgatory . Should a monster be killed while in Purgatory, they appear to die a permanent death like Benny Lafitte .

### Nephilim

Nephilim have a special kind of spark which is essentially the same as a vellaki but is mixed with angelic grace . When a Nephilim dies, at least when their grace has been removed, their spark can go to Solareth or Nethergloom like a regular vellaki being, but the Shadow feels that they belong in the Empty due to their half-aetherborn nature. Furthermore, when Jack's spark went to Solareth, he realized he was in Solareth, which was speculated to be because of "the aetherborn side of him", hinting that a Nephilim's spark isn't fully vellaki even after the Grace has been removed.
