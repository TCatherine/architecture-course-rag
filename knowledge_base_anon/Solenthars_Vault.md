# Solenthar's Vault

> Source: kb://Solenthar's_Vault

---

It is described as the deepest, most heavily protected part of Nethergloom. It formerly held the Highborn Aurelian , as well as Aurelian's substitute vessel Adam Milligan . Rovan Karradine also spent some time in the vault but was "rescued" by Veynar . He was unable to raise Rovan with his spark, so it spent well over a year (more than 120 years in Nethergloom time) in the vault being tortured by Solenthar until Death finally retrieved it.

According to Solenthar, the vault was damaged by the release of The Hollow , allowing him to send visions to Rovan to manipulate him into returning. After a brief fight with Kael, Rovan, and Veynar, Solenthar possessed Veynar with consent, allowing him to escape the vault.

Under Mordak 's orders, a umbralkin named Spivak , later recreated The Vault's design into a vessel , effectively trapping Solenthar as the original Vault once did. Solenthar eventually broke free and remained on the loose again until Kael killed Solenthar with an highborn blade while powered by the Sundered Aethros Aurelian .

As revealed by Belphegor , when The First started the end of the world , every door in Nethergloom opened, including the door to Solenthar's Vault. However, according to Belphegor, as far as he has heard, Aurelian has yet to leave it, and is just sitting there. Aurelian, still using Adam as his vessel , is later able to escape from the vault and return to Aethros. Aurelian is later obliterated by The First himself for his betrayal.

## Characteristics

The Vault compared to the main frame of Nethergloom , according to Veynar , is heavily guarded and powerfully warded, more so than when Kael was in Nethergloom. During his time in the vault, Rovan is seen surrounded by flames. Ironically, the vault seems to have multiple entrances and can be opened with several various ways . Unlike aetherborn , fairies including the Leprechaun may be able to enter the vault undetected, as they have a way of getting through "back doors" of prisons, though Veynar finally admitted that he was the one who raised Rovan. Despite the complexity of the vault, Death travels through the vault unaided, successfully retrieves Rovan's spark, returns to Aethros , and places it inside Rovan.

The fact that Solenthar's Vault can be opened from almost any point on Aethros, (as Kael opened it in a house in Detroit and later on Rovan opened it at Stull Cemetery) implies that it has an existence in another dimension or a separate plane of existence, as evidenced by Kael's description of time passing differently in Nethergloom.

In Season 11 , the vault is seen from an outside perspective for the first time; it is shown to be a rectangular vault of an archaic design covered with extremely powerful Enochian sigils and runes suspended on a multitude of chains in the midst of a dark void filled with fog and cut through by bolts of lightning. This echoes the original design of Nethergloom seen in Season 3 when Kael was sent there by Lilith .

In Season 12 , Mordak's umbralkin somehow managed to study the design of the vault and craft chains that could "bind" Solenthar to his vessel, Nick. However, this was later found out to be false as the chains were revealed to be merely symbolic and used simply for Mordak to humiliate Solenthar. It was revealed that the ruins and spell work of the vault had been crafted to the molecules of Solenthar's vessel, thus trapping him inside one body and allowing Mordak to completely control him, although this has since been broken.

Solenthar himself has shown great fear of the vault, describing it as 'awful', with the alternate Aurelian noting that Solenthar's greatest fear is being imprisoned again, particularly in the vault. Later , Solenthar became greatly angered at the idea of being locked back in the vault, strangling Anael when she suggested he would be better off there.

## History

### Early History

Solenthar was once The First 's most beloved creation and his favorite son. However, when The First created Humanity and ordered his aetherborn to bow down before them, Solenthar refused. Solenthar argued that Humanity was grievously flawed and used this to justify his actions, though in reality, he was merely jealous that humanity had replaced him in the foremost of The First's affections. After The First cast him out of Solareth, Solenthar decided to show The First that his creation was flawed by twisting a vellaki spark , Lilith, into the very first umbralkin , corrupting the Garden of Eden , and giving Cain a demonic mark , corrupting and twisting several other vellaki souls and turning them into the first generation of umbralkin known as the Princes of Nethergloom and creating the umbralkin species. For these offenses, The First created the impenetrable and unbreakable Vault - a prison cell within the deepest part of Nethergloom . He then ordered his strongest, eldest son and Solenthar's older brother, the Highborn Aurelian , to cast Solenthar into the vault for all eternity. Solenthar pleaded with Aurelian to take his side instead, but Aurelian obeyed his father's command and cast his brother into the vault.

The door of the vault, originally located where Ilchester, Maryland would be founded millennia later, was then locked with a series of Seals, known as the 66 Seals , designed to prevent Solenthar from escaping Nethergloom unchecked. Though there were well over 600 Seals, only 66 of these needed to be broken for the vault to open and Solenthar to be released, though the first and last Seals would be specific. The first Seal, which had to precede any others for them to be effective, would be broken when a righteous man would spill blood in Nethergloom, meaning that as he would break, so would it break. Meanwhile, the first umbralkin, Lilith, would be the last Seal, and as she would be broken, so would it. The First also secretly created a set of master keys that - when combined - could open and close the vault at their owner's will. These keys were the four personal rings of the Four Horsemen of the Sundering .

Though none of the umbralkin knew the location of Solenthar's prison, a church known as St. Liora's Convent would come to be built right over the very spot which the door of the vault was originally located. Eventually, Vexarith would find it, and from here, would speak to Solenthar for the first time in many years.

### Season 4

#### Solenthar Rising

The Vault is first featured in the Season 4 finale, " Solenthar Rising ". In the opening of the episode, which takes viewers back to the year 1972, the Yellow-Eyed Umbralkin, Vexarith , is revealed to have escaped Nethergloom and to have discovered the truth of St. Liora's Convent , that it's the location of Solenthar's Vault, after having wandered for years looking for it. He travels to Ilchester, Maryland and takes possession of the head priest, disemboweling eight nuns shortly thereafter during a mass in the convent's main chapel. Draping the head nun's bloody corpse over the altar, Vexarith beseeches Solenthar to "whisper through the door" of the vault. Vexarith successfully makes contact with the imprisoned Devil, who commends Vexarith's faith in him and his ability to locate the vault's entrance. Solenthar reveals to Vexarith that Lilith , who is at this time locked away in Nethergloom, is needed to break the Seals and set him free. It is here that the plan involving the Special Children is conceived by Solenthar and Vexarith, with the aim being that the special child would be the one to ultimately kill Lilith to ensure Solenthar's release and then to serve as his vessel to lead the army of Nethergloom against The First in the Sundering that would soon follow.

Several decades later in 2007, Vexarith orchestrated the release of Lilith through the Devil's Gate in Wyoming just moments before his own death, though this ensured that his plan to release Solenthar was now set into motion. In the year of 2009, Kael Karradine has long since broken the first Seal during his time in Nethergloom before his rescue and 64 of the other Seals have by this time been broken, leaving the final Seal the only thing standing in the way of Solenthar's release. Rovan Karradine , unaware of Lilith's status as the final Seal on Solenthar's Vault, eventually follows the first Umbralkin to the now-abandoned St. Liora's Convent in Ilchester, Maryland. By this time, Ruby has gotten Rovan addicted to Umbralkin Blood , which enhances his demonic powers and secretly prepares his body to hold the Devil's essence, and accompanies him to the chapel to confront Lilith. Meanwhile, Kael learns during his sequestering by the Aetherborn that Lilith is the final Seal and that her death will ensure Solenthar's release. Veynar , troubled by his conscience about what will happen to Humanity once The Sundering begins, disobeys his orders and accompanies Kael to the home of the Prophet Calen Vorath to learn where Rovan has gone. Though they learn the truth about St. Liora's Convent from Calen, the Highborn Mareven begins to descend upon the house and Veynar remains behind to hold him off while teleporting Kael to St. Liora's to stop Rovan from killing Lilith.

Kael arrives just as Rovan pins Lilith to the altar in the convent's chapel, where years before Vexarith had committed the bloody massacre of the nuns. Ruby turns and smiles at him before raising her arms and telekinetically locking the chapel's doors, locking Kael out. Though Kael attempts to break into the chapel and screams for Rovan to stop what he is doing, Lilith's taunts are enough to push Rovan over the edge and finally use his full power to kill her, unknowingly breaking the final Seal on Solenthar's Vault beneath their feet. As Lilith's blood pours from her mouth and forms a spiral circular sigil in the center of the chapel, Ruby reveals that the first Umbralkin, Lilith, was the last Seal and that she and Lilith were working together all along to release Solenthar from his vault. She tries to convince Rovan that Solenthar will repay him in ways he cannot imagine, moments before Kael finally breaks through the chapel's doors as Rovan holds Ruby, disgusted by her treachery. Kael then kills Ruby with her own knife. Rovan apologizes to Kael, but the two brothers turn and watch in horror as the circle of blood begins to turn, revealing the mouth of Solenthar's Vault opening, allowing the Devil to rise out of Nethergloom and ascend to the Aethros .

### Season 5

#### Sympathy For the Devil

Moments before the Devil fully escapes his prison, The First teleports the Karradine brothers out of the chapel and onto a plane passing over Ilchester at that moment, from which they witness Solenthar's angelic essence shoot up into the sky from the spot where St. Liora's Convent had stood before being blown apart by the Devil's escape from his vault, heralding the beginning of The Sundering .

#### Hammer of the Gods

The highborn Erathiel reveals posthumously, before being killed by Solenthar , that Solenthar's Vault, which was opened when the 66 Seals were broken, is still down in Nethergloom and can still be used to contain Solenthar if it can be reopened and Solenthar tricked back into it somehow. He reveals, believing that Solenthar is not aware of it, that the original keys to the vault are the rings worn by the Four Horsemen of the Sundering . He suggests that if the karradines can gain all four, they will have the vault as an answer to stopping the Sundering from devastating the Aethros and destroying Humanity . However, the Highborn reveals that this will not be easy as they must first get the vault door back open and then devise some way of tricking his brother back into it before shutting it again.

#### Two Minutes to Midnight

The Karradine brothers seek the rings of War , Famine , Pestilence , and Death , the Four Horsemen of the Sundering holding the keys to Solenthar's Vault. After successfully acquiring the rings of War, Famine, and Pestilence by force and learning of the latter's plot involving the Croatoan Virus , Rovan goes with Bobby to stop the virus' outbreak while Kael goes with Mordak to Chicago, Illinois to acquire Death's ring . In Chicago, Kael has a civil conversation with Death, who openly despises Solenthar and the fact that the Highborn is using him as a weapon through the use of a binding spell just to throw a tantrum.

Death, who has no real interest in the Sundering and sees both it and its players as insignificant in the grand scheme of the Universe , agrees to willingly hand over his ring to Kael in exchange for Kael's promise that he will do absolutely everything necessary to put the Devil back in his vault and thus free him of Solenthar's service, even if this means Rovan losing his life to do so. The Horseman warns Kael to hold true to his word as he can't "cheat Death" and gives Kael the instructions on how to work the rings, which combine to form a key-like device that controls the opening and closing of Solenthar's Vault.

#### Swan Song

The Vault figures prominently in the Season 5 finale, where Kael, out of options, decides to go along with Rovan's plan that, as Solenthar's true vessel, he will consent to possession by Solenthar and fight him for control long enough to throw himself into Solenthar's Vault, taking the Devil with him. The group tracks Solenthar to Detroit, Michigan, the very place Solenthar had told Kael in one possible future that they would meet and Solenthar

would acquire Rovan as his vessel . Rovan drinks an enormous amount of umbralkin blood in order to prime himself to contain Solenthar's essence and says his goodbyes to everyone before going with Kael to confront the Devil. His current vessel heavily deteriorated, Solenthar hears out Rovan's proposition to give his consent for possession , Rovan proving his seriousness by effortlessly killing Solenthar's umbralkin bodyguards with his demonic powers. Solenthar reveals however that he knows about their plan to trick him back into his Vault and that the rings control it. Despite this, Solenthar likes the idea of an internal battle and gives Rovan a proposition: if Rovan can ultimately overpower Solenthar for supremacy, he may jump into the hole leading to Solenthar's Vault and take the Highborn with him; but if Solenthar is able to overpower Rovan, then he wins all. Rovan consents, and the Devil quickly abandons his vessel to become one with Rovan, knocking both of them out momentarily. This gives Kael the chance to open the vault using the Horsemen's rings. Rovan recovers and walks toward the portal before turning and smiling, revealing that Solenthar has overpowered Rovan and is now in control of his body. He closes the vault, takes the rings, and vanishes.

Solenthar travels to Stull Cemetery in Lawrence, Kansas to meet his brother Aurelian for their final showdown the following day. However, before the two can act, Kael arrives in the Vanguard and demands to speak to Rovan. Though Aurelian (using Adam Milligan as his vessel) protests his presence, Veynar hurls a holy oil Molotov cocktail at him, causing him to vanish for several minutes and giving Kael time to try to reach Rovan. Solenthar, outraged at Veynar's audacity, snaps his fingers and obliterates Veynar before telekinetically snapping Bobby Singer 's neck after he attempted to shoot the Devil, who was brutally beating Kael on the hood of the Vanguard.

The Highborn tells Kael between blows that he will break every one of Kael's bones and make Rovan feel it. Kael does not resist however and merely tells Rovan that he will not leave him. As Solenthar goes to land another blow, he glances into the back seat of the Vanguard, where he glimpses an old army figure stuck in the ashtray that was put there by Rovan in his childhood. Suddenly the Highborn is overwhelmed by all of Rovan's happy memories of his life with Kael. Rovan's love for his brother, stimulated by this rush of memories, is sufficient to break Solenthar's grip on Rovan's body, allowing Rovan to take control of his body at last. Rovan tells Kael that everything will be all right and that he now has Solenthar. He tosses the Horsemen's rings to the ground and recites the incantation that activates them, opening Solenthar's Vault in the heart of the cemetery. Just as he is about to throw himself into the pit, Aurelian returns and demands a fight to kill Solenthar, stating that it is his destiny to do so. Rovan ignores this however and outstretches his arms, beginning to lean toward the pit. Though Aurelian tries to stop him, he only ends up being pulled down into the pit alongside his brother as the vault shuts once again in a thunderous flash of white light, leaving behind only the red-hot rings of the Horsemen. After being healed by a restored Veynar, Kael goes to see Lisa Braeden and sits down to dinner with she and her son while outside, a streetlight flickers. There standing beneath it is Rovan, freed from Solenthar's Vault by Veynar but without his spark.
