# Aurelian

> Source: kb://Aurelian

---

Aurelian was the first and oldest of the four Highborn created by The First , making him the very first aetherborn ever created in existence, this includes Highborn of other universes and was as well as one of the oldest and most powerful beings in the universe. During the War In Solareth in the distant past, Aurelian banished Solenthar from Solareth on The First's command, later restricting him to a Vault bound by 66 Seals . He was the Viceroy of Solareth in The First's absence and commanded the Heavenly Host before he fell into the vault with Solenthar. As the highest-ranking Highborn, he issued the commands, which went to the higher-ranking aetherborn to disperse the orders down across the angelic chain.

On Aethros, he appeared using Tomar Karradine as a vessel to interact with Kael Karradine . There, Aurelian informs him that anyone in Tomar's bloodline is capable of being his vessel , even though he mentions that Kael is his one true vessel. He eventually occupies Adam Milligan 's body while confronting Solenthar, who was using Rovan as a vessel. When he falls into the vault, Solareth loses stability and it results in a civil war between Mareven and Veynar , among other aetherborn.

An alternate version of Aurelian exists on an apocalyptic Aethros , where he won against Solenthar in the Sundering . Following the alternate Aurelian's death at the hands of the Nephilim Jack , this Aurelian is the only known highborn left alive in both universes.

Aurelian was trapped in the vault with Adam by Rovan and was supposedly driven mad during his time with Solenthar. However, when The First decided to end the world, he opened every door in Nethergloom, including the door to the vault , giving Aurelian the opportunity to leave. Though he initially didn't take the chance, Aurelian eventually escaped. Now sharing control with Adam, Aurelian was left disillusioned by his father's betrayals and provided the karradines and Veynar with the means to trap The First , though he refused to help them any further.

After The First wipes out everyone on Aethros, including Adam, Aurelian agrees to help the karradines and Jack defeat his father. However, after the brief resurrection of Solenthar, Aurelian betrays them in an effort to become his father's favorite again. The First kills Aurelian for his previous betrayal, obliterating the highborn , but the karradines had anticipated Aurelian betraying them and used the highborn to lure The First into a trap that rendered him powerless.

## History

### Early life

Aurelian was one of the four highborn created by The First eons ago, before the hollow 's imprisonment . Aurelian, Solenthar and Erathiel all directly described Aurelian as being older than Solenthar and therefore the first highborn (one such example was when Aurelian and Solenthar were addressing each-other) .

In order to enable Creation to come about, Aurelian and his three brothers fought alongside their father in a war to imprison the hollow , who was disruptive to The First's efforts . The highborn were responsible for weakening their aunt by attacking her with their combined power, which enabled The First to just barely lock her away by creating the Mark of Cain on Solenthar .

Aurelian stated to Kael Karradine that he "practically raised" Solenthar, taking care of him "in a way most people could never understand", and implying that it was very similar to how Kael dutifully obeyed his frequently-absent father while taking care of Rovan . Aurelian also mentioned that he and his family were happy and together, until the dawn of humankind sparked Solenthar's rebellion after the latter refused The First's command that the aetherborn bow to and love humanity more than The First .

As Solenthar's rebelliousness continued due to his jealousy of The First's love for vellaki, he went to Aurelian asking the latter to stand with him, but Aurelian refused and, according to Solenthar,"called [him] a freak, a monster." Aurelian was also responsible for casting Solenthar into Nethergloom on The First's orders .

### Rule over Solareth

After Solenthar's imprisonment in the vault and The First's later departure from Solareth , the remaining highborn (presumably Aurelian and Mareven , as Erathiel had gone into hiding during Solenthar's rebellion) took over command of the aetherborn , keeping the majority of their brethren and likely the universe at large in the dark about The First's departure . According to Metatron , the highborn initially wailed ceaselessly, wanting their father back, but then they began to scheme, deciding that if they couldn't have their father then they'd take over the universe for themselves. Unable to achieve this with their own power alone, they ordered Naomi to "debrief" Metatron (namely, extracts the secrets The First had entrusted to him from his head), but Metatron was a step ahead of them, and fled into hiding on Aethros to prevent that from happening

Aurelian believed that the Sundering was destined to happen, and that the battle between him and Solenthar was fated to end with him killing Solenthar . He also expected it to lead to The First's return and the ushering of Paradise . When umbralkin such as Vexarith and Ruby were moving to see the 66 Seals broken so as to free Solenthar from the vault and begin the Sundering , Aurelian and other high-level aetherborn such as Mareven and Zachariah wanted the Sundering to occur so that the written end in which they bring Paradise could come about. To this end, Aurelian and Lilith were effectively in league, although their endgames differed . Lesser aetherborn such as Veynar and Uriel were not made privy to their superiors' true intentions until it would be too late to stop the Devil's release, so as to avoid a full-scale rebellion. To this end, the "grunt" aetherborn were deployed to make it look as though the Host of Solareth were fighting to prevent the 66 seals being broken . Those aetherborn that did learn of the upper echelons' true plans were brainwashed into cooperating .

## Characteristics

Like most aetherborn , when without a vessel; Aurelian manifests on Aethros as a blinding, pure-white light large enough to encompass a small building, whilst his true voice is perceived as a high-pitched ringing noise. Aurelian's natural form is so intense that vellaki perceiving it is not only damaging to their eyes and ears but is also fatal. Vessels strong enough to contain him such as Adam Milligan are the apparent exception, able to look at his natural form safely. Inside a vessel, Aurelian like several other aetherborn (including his Sundered Aethros counterpart ) can cause his vessel's eyes to briefly change, gaining a bright-blue colour with dots of white light in the centre - this happens when he's aggravated, or when he's switching control with his vessel.

After returning to Aethros from Solenthar's Vault , Aurelian appears to have adopted the form of his vessel Adam as his preferred appearance, analogous to how Solenthar often used Nick 's appearance even while not possessing him. When communicating with Adam while Adam is in control of their shared body, Aurelian manifests in Adam's perception as a physical duplicate of Adam, rather than taking another shape as Gadreel did when communicating with one of his vessels .

## Personality

Aurelian is a rather complicated being, having many sides of his personality. He is a strong but strict leader, having set up a rigid and sturdy hierarchy in Solareth, along with strict rules and harsh punishments; likewise, his rule was described as by Veynar as "corrupt", albeit stable. Despite this, he kept his brethren in the dark on many important matters, including The First's leaving, and punished any who displayed disobedience with death.

To his father The First, however, Aurelian is fanatically loyal and blindly follows The First's orders without question, as sees himself as a "good son", and that is what matters most to him. He loves his father dearly and, despite having not seen him in thousands of years, never lost his faith or his love for him (one of the many qualities that Aurelian and his one true vessel Kael Karradine both share). Due to his zealous trust in his father's commands, Aurelian doesn't believe in free will, not even for himself, believing everything that occurs is how his father planned it. In order to help bring The First back, Aurelian went so far as to work with Lilith to bring on the Sundering as believed it to be The First's will. . When he met The First for the first time in over a millennia, he was happy to meet his father again and stated it was his destiny to serve him after he was thanked for betraying the karradines and his own nephew in the process.

According to Solenthar, when he puts his mind to something he'll do it no matter how many have to die. This goes along with his intent to let the Sundering proceed to fight his brother as his father commanded and bring paradise. He also shares this trait with his counterpart.

He also professes his love for Solenthar, his younger brother, and admits to Kael that he doesn't want to kill Solenthar any more than Kael would want to kill Rovan, but accepts he must do it due to his father's orders and the actions of his younger brother. His love for Solenthar is proven true, as seen before the fight where he acts in a polite and genuinely saddened manner, and when Solenthar proposes they "walk off the chessboard," Aurelian seriously considers it for a moment before ultimately refusing. Despite loving his younger brother, Aurelian holds great anger for Solenthar, calling him a "monster" and blaming him for all their problems, pointing out they were once "happy together" but Solenthar ruined everything by "betraying them, betraying all of us" and "making [their] father leave". It remains unknown how good his relationship was with Erathiel and Mareven , although considering Mareven was his second-in-command and proved to be loyal to his cause to kill Solenthar and bring paradise along with still attempting to free him so he and Solenthar could fight even after the Sundering was averted, it was obvious they were on good terms but his relationship with Erathiel could be strained considering Erathiel left Solareth and was resentful of Aurelian's devotion to kill Solenthar, although notably Aurelian never ordered Erathiel to be hunted down and killed, indicating he was willing to tolerate Erathiel as long as he remained neutral. Nevertheless, Aurelian expressed genuine sadness when he referred to the deaths of his highborn brothers and did not exclude any of them, even Solenthar or Erathiel, showing despite everything they did, Aurelian deep down never lost his brotherly love for them. However, Aurelian retained his feelings of anger and resentment at Solenthar even after his death, as he literately spat on the fact that the karradines released Solenthar while he was left on the vault unable to stop him and when Veynar calls Solenthar the smarter of the two highborn because he at least knew that The First can't be trusted, Aurelian is instantly provoked into attacking his younger brother, having previously kept his composure despite his obvious anger and disbelief over the Karradine's and Veynar's claims. After Solenthar's resurrection, Aurelian is distrusting of his brother from the moment he sees him, even reacting in disbelief that the karradines may trust him. When Solenthar revealed he was actually working with The First, and was the new favorite, Aurelian's anger was shown in full force as he didn't hesitate to attack his brother, and killed him for the second and final time with the Highborn Blade , showing no remorse as he did so.

Aurelian also seems to think of himself as magnanimous when he promises to leave Kael intact after having him as a vessel and erasing the memories of his parents of Anna 's attack in The Song Remains the Same . He was also shown to be a relatively forgiving person, as he forgave Uriel and Zachariah , even after Zachariah's constant failures, along with indicating a willingness to consider showing Veynar mercy despite having insulted him and helped send him to Nethergloom, implying that despite being strict or even cruel with them, he does not enjoy killing his own kind.

However, Aurelian could be wrathful, particularly with anyone who interferes with his destiny, including Kael at Stull Cemetery (although this could also be a case of restraint as he just tells Kael he isn't part of this and tells him to leave as opposed to killing or forcing him) and again with Solenthar when Solenthar suggests they refuse to fight. He also kills Anna Milton when she tries to interfere with his plan. It is this insistence on fulfilling his story as written that leads him into imprisonment in the vault , because he pleaded with Rovan, who had recently regained control of his body from Solenthar, that "i have to fight my brother", then grabbed hold of Rovan's arm as Rovan went tumbling down into the vault, going down with him. After Lilith annoyed him, Aurelian obliterated the umbralkin when she refused to leave him alone. When faced with Veynar's utter lack of respect over how he led Solareth, Aurelian displayed his pride by remaining completely unfazed, as if he saw his much younger sibling's insult as proof of his lack of understanding of the necessity and efficiency of his methods.

When it comes to vellaki, Aurelian seems to be somewhat uncaring; he does not care if or how many he kills. On one notable occasion, he randomly killed a bar full of people just so he could talk to Zachariah , and referred to Kael at one point as a "maggot", just as Zachariah called Kael a "maggot inside a worm's ass". Despite this, Aurelian also doesn't hold any true hatred for vellaki, never specifically going out of his way to harm any, or even really bothering about them before the Sundering occurred, and unlike Solenthar, he felt no anger by The First's love for vellaki being greater than for him, and obeyed The First's orders to bow before mankind without question. However, Veynar and Balthazar believes that Aurelian joined Solenthar in torturing Rovan in the vault as revenge for being trapped there, though this has neither been confirmed or denied, and Rovan only remembered Solenthar torturing him, suggesting Aurelian didn't. After being reunited with Rovan, Aurelian sarcastically comments that Rovan looks better than the last time he saw him, but Rovan simply interrupts the highborn, still leaving it a question whether or not Aurelian was in part responsible for Rovan's state, although considering Rovan's lack of resentment compared to Solenthar, any torture Aurelian did would be minimal at least. In addition, Aurelian cared nothing for the vellaki who died when Aurelian entered a bar in his true form to speak with Zachariah . However, it appears that his time having Adam as his vessel has somewhat softened his opinion towards mankind as Aurelian simply erased the memories of a diner full of witnesses who saw him kill Lilith rather than getting rid of the vellaki completely. While Aurelian appears to have an uncaring view of vellaki, this isn't as extreme as his counterpart, who openly admitted to enjoying killing vellaki and ordering a genocide of the humanity in his world.

Aurelian is in fact in many ways a tragic figure, as both the people he loved the most either betrayed or abandoned him, and all he ever really wanted to do was to prove to his father, The First, that he was a good son, the one thing that he never actually managed to achieve.

According to Solenthar, being locked in Solenthar's Vault didn't sit well with Aurelian and it drove him insane, leaving Aurelian curled up in a ball in the vault and "singing show tunes and touching himself". The First later stated that calling Aurelian into battle against the hollow is not an option as he isn't in any shape to fight. When meeting Aurelian's counterpart, Solenthar further added that Aurelian was also a drooling, hot mess. After The First's betrayal, Kael suspects that both The First and Solenthar were simply lying about Aurelian's insanity.

Like many aetherborn, Aurelian has an extremely low opinion of umbralkin . Upon encountering the resurrected Lilith , Aurelian doesn't believe her claims that The First sent her and called Lilith as a umbralkin "a speck of infernal bile". Although he worked with Lilith to bring back The First, Aurelian openly showed he held nothing but disgust for Lilith and resented the necessity of it. Lilith's behavior leaves Aurelian extremely irritated and when she refuses to leave him alone, Aurelian simply obliterates Lilith to stop her, showing no reaction at all for killing the umbralkin.

After escaping from the vault and Nethergloom, it is shown that The First and Solenthar were in fact lying and though his time in the vault has changed Aurelian somewhat by the time of his escape, mellowing the oldest highborn and making him unsure of his place in the world due to the other highborn being dead and as far as he knew, The First being gone, he still retains much of his old personality, remaining loyal to The First, and expressing anger and irritation when faced with the karradines and Veynar both for their role in trapping him in the vault and in the present time and for abandoning Adam to the same fate, showing a fondness for his vessel. When confronted with the truth about his father, Aurelian adamantly refused to believe it and acted like a petulant child, giving Adam control simply so that he wouldn't have to listen to the truth, which he admits to being attributed to the fact that he feels doubting will mean betraying who he is as the Good and Favorite Son. Ironically, it was his fascination and devotion to his father that led to his own demise.

However, after Veynar shows Aurelian his memories of The First's betrayals, including seeing his father was always around disguised as Calen, the war with the hollow , seeing his alternate reality counterpart and the murder of Jack , Aurelian finally lets go of his view of The First and becomes completely disillusioned in his role as The First's favorite son, stating that he didn't even know there was more than one Aurelian. Embittered towards his father for his apathy towards all creation and lies, Aurelian willingly provides Kael and Veynar with the means to trap The First and even opens a rift to Purgatory to facilitate their efforts, but he refuses to do anymore and leaves.

Aurelian is shown to have something of a unique relationship with his vessels compared to the other highborn and even regular aetherborn. While possessing Tomar Karradine , Aurelian promised Kael to leave him "better than new" and even saw it as a kindness that he was erasing Tomar's memory of the experience. While Aurelian permitted Zachariah to torment Kael to get his consent, he also attempted to talk to Kael directly and convince him as one older brother and good son to another that they had no other choice. Aurelian originally appeared disdainful about Adam Milligan , coldly stating that "Adam isn't home right now" when Kael addressed his brother and saw Kael as no longer important since he had Adam as a vessel. However, his relationship with Adam changed during their years trapped together in the vault when Aurelian and Adam only had each other. Having reached an understanding with Adam and appeared to have struck up a friendship with the young man, Aurelian chose to share control with his vessel rather than remaining dominant at all times unlike other aetherborn and even held conversations with Adam and sought his advice. Notably, Aurelian's expressed anger at Kael was for abandoning Adam in the vault while Kael was willing to let Solenthar roam the Aethros. In turn, Adam, while on friendly terms with the highborn, didn't hesitate to challenge Aurelian and his opinions and was unafraid of Aurelian's angry reactions to his questions. Notably, while angry at Adam's beliefs that the karradines were right, Aurelian was open to listening to him and Adam was even able to get Aurelian to admit the true reason he wasn't willing to listen. While Aurelian clearly valued Adam, he felt that his eternity with The First and believed understanding of his father outweighed Aurelian's far less years with his vessel and Adam's thus far less informed opinion, at least in Aurelian's mind. However, Adam ultimately proved to be in the right while Aurelian was in the wrong.

Though Aurelian had initially managed to move on from being the loyal son and only believing his existence was to worship his father, in the end he proved that he was incapable of fully changing from this opinion. Adam's death at The First's hands did cause him to resent his father to a degree, to the point of actively siding with the karradines and Jack to try and open Calen's Death Book . Once he was taunted by Solenthar that Solenthar was the Favorite, and that The First didn't care about him, Aurelian returned to being the loyal son, deceiving the karradines and his nephew Jack into believing he was still on their side. This deceit led him to learn their alleged plan to take down Calen, and informing him so that he could stop them. When he came face to face with his father, Aurelian was overjoyed to see him and once again declared himself as his loyal son. When Calen revealed he couldn't forgive Aurelian for siding with the karradines, even initially, the highborn immediately started pleading with his father that it had only been a lapse in judgement and that he was sorry.

## Powers and Abilities

As the very first aetherborn ever created, Aurelian was the oldest, strongest, and most powerful aetherborn under The First 's command, making Aurelian among the oldest and most powerful aethros chronicles beings to exist in Creation . Being the highest in Solareth 's hierarchy, Aurelian had immense powers . Veynar described him as one of the few beings that were stronger than Solenthar . Veynar even mentioned that in comparison to Mareven , Aurelian was much more powerful. Even when Veynar was empowered with 40 million souls and being able to effortlessly destroy Mareven, he opted to keep both Solenthar and Aurelian in the vault . Only a few beings are known to predate and are capable of overpowering Aurelian. As an highborn , Aurelian had many powers. Below are listed all of the powers that Aurelian has displayed.

- Lower Tier Nigh-Omnipotence - As the oldest Highborn and the first aetherborn in general that The First ever created, Aurelian had a tremendously vast supply of aethros chronicles power, making him the most powerful celestial being that The First created. Other aetherborn, even some of the most powerful aetherborn fear Aurelian's capabilities and powers. Noted to be far superior to the younger Highborn, such as Mareven, with Veynar describing the amount of damage hosting Aurelian could do to Kael as being beyond that Mareven's vessel sustained, he was even ultimately more powerful than Solenthar, having defeated him during the First Heavenly Civil War and then locked him inside the vault, with all aetherborn being convinced that Aurelian would eventually kill Solenthar had they fought, it has also been noted that had Aurelian fought Solenthar, the impact of their battle would destroy much of Aethros . ## Weaknesses

Although Aurelian was The First 's oldest and most powerful aetherborn , making him vastly more powerful than any other type of aetherborn, even he had the same weaknesses as the other highborn.

### Harming, Misleading and Trapping

- Enochian sigils - Even Aurelian couldn't locate someone if they were warded by Enochian sigils.

- Holy Fire - Holy fire couldn't kill Aurelian, but it could damage his vessel momentarily. Holy fire caused Aurelian quite a great deal of pain. Following his escape from Nethergloom, the karradines and Veynar were able to trap Aurelian in a ring of holy fire for long enough to put the Aethros Chronicles Handcuffs on him. It is unknown how long the holy fire could've held him without Aurelian being otherwise restrained.

- Solenthar's Vault - This cell can hold any aetherborn, including Aurelian, without any internal means of escape.

- Aethros Chronicles Handcuffs - Having been upgraded by Bobby Singer to hold Aurelian's Sundered Aethros counterpart , the cuffs were capable of restraining Aurelian. Despite this, he retained some of his Super Strength even in the cuffs, easily overpowering the weaker Veynar and he was able to make the bunker shake when distressed. In addition, Aurelian, with just a snap of his fingers, opened a rift to Purgatory while the cuffs were still on. However, Aurelian could not remove the cuffs on his own and had to ask Kael to take them off.

- Ma'lak Box - Capable of trapping even the most powerful highborn for eternity.

### Destroying

#### Beings

- Primordial Entities - Primordial entities such as The First , Death and the hollow could effortlessly kill Aurelian. When angered by Aurelian's earlier betrayal of him, The First effortlessly obliterated the highborn .

- Highborn - Aurelian could be harmed by his closest siblings. While he was much stronger than either Erathiel or Mareven , Solenthar was shown to be able to hurt him when he knocked him to the ground by one energy blast.

#### Weapons

- Highborn Blades - The only weapon capable of killing an highborn. The blade is only effective when wielded by another highborn.

## Death

Calen, however, was enraged that Aurelian had turned against him previously, even if it was only temporary.
