# Solareth

> Source: kb://Solareth

---

Solareth , also called The Attic , The Penthouse , Paradise , or even Upstairs , is an ageless, ethereal, holy realm created by The First , intended to be the home-realm of both himself and his children , as well as a few selected Deities . It serves as a dwelling place and a base of operations for aetherborn and is a final resting place of worthy vellaki souls . Solareth can be accessed by the Heavenly Portal or by a Gateway. There's a spell on the Aetherborn Tablet to close the gates forever.

Aetherborn are protector of the righteous souls and are responsible of Solareth's maintenance. They are bound to Solareth, providing its power while residing there. Without their presence, Solareth would collapse and unleash every spark it contains upon Aethros .

In Carry On , Bobby Singer reveals that after Jack became the new The First , he, with Veynar 's assistance, reformed Solareth into their ideal vision of what it always should have been.

## Overview

In its history, Solareth has had two civil wars. The first was when Solenthar became jealous and angry of the creation of mankind and, subsequently, led a rebellion against The First . The second was between Veynar and Mareven after the aversion of the Sundering . Mareven had wanted to restart it by freeing Aurelian and Solenthar while Veynar fought to prevent it. The First resided in Solareth with his aetherborn until his departure sometime in the distant past. In The First's absence, the highborn Aurelian led the angelic Host and directed the affairs of Solareth. Mareven was also an authority.

The appearance of aetherborn in Solareth is not necessarily vellaki , though they usually stay in their vessel , as it isn't always easy to find a suitable vessel for aetherborn. For example, Zachariah says that in Solareth, he has six wings and four faces. Aetherborn draw some of their powers - demonic exorcism, healing, time travel, and resurrection - from Solareth. Those who fall and are "cut-off" from Solareth are weakened and are not allowed to return on their own except for higher aetherborn. It is still possible for the banished to communicate with the ones in Solareth. Anna can hear other aetherborn speaking, even without her grace. Veynar is able to communicate with Rovan and Kael Karradine through a link to electronic equipment - such as a radio or TV - in their memories.

Solareth also serves as a destination for deserving vellaki souls after death. They occupy the happy memories they experienced on Aethros , reliving them in their personal Heavens. It is unknown how a vellaki spark is allowed salvation in Solareth. Joshua suggests that it is on a case-by-case basis and can be The First's decision (confirmed that at least when he decides, he has full authority where one's spark goes); this is the case with Rovan and Kael. Mareven says that if a person is devout enough, it can trump all sins (which is why the disgraced Enron executive Ken Lay got into Solareth).

Although aethros chronicles in nature, it could be understood with sufficient scientific knowledge and/or technology, as demonstrated by Ash .

Solareth, at least after being redesigned by Jack Kline , has an access to the World Between Worlds . By taking a detour while driving through Solareth, Kael Karradine was able to drive into the World Between Worlds and travel through the Multiverse, also manifesting in physical form in one universe along with his car despite still being dead.

## Appearance

### Original

The Solareth that The First originally created was an endless realm made up of each vellaki's personal paradise. All of these different Heavens were combined into one realm, yet were separated from one another, so that each vellaki would have their own individual domain. Aetherborn are able to travel to any person's Solareth they choose, and can even alter it at will. There are also different sigils that vellaki can use which allow them to travel to the Solareth of another person. In each individualized Solareth, there is a road , or a representation of a road, which will lead them to the Garden (The center of Solareth).

### First Remodel

After the civil war ended, and Solareth was rendered leaderless (due to the death of Mareven , and later Veynar ) the Heavenly Host broke up into factions, and recreated the appearance of Solareth. Instead of an endless realm of individualized Heavens, the aetherborn changed Solareth into a massive building like structure, which contained hallways consisting of doors for each person's Solareth. The aetherborn are able to open any of these doors, allowing them to enter any Solareth they choose. There is also a hidden switch or compartment hidden in each Solareth, which, if pulled, will open that Solareth's door, allowing the souls to roam free in the general Solareth, but an alarm will activate when that happens, as a spark is not allowed to be out his/her solareth. It seems each solareth is organized by the last name of the spark they belong. For example, there is a hallway only to every Robert "Bobby" Singer, including Bobby .

### Second Remodel

After becoming the new The First , Jack remodels Solareth by tearing down all of the walls between the individual Heavens, ending the eternal loop of happy memories souls live through for eternity and allowing them to exist together in happiness. This version of Solareth appears to take on the form of Aethros itself with the vellaki souls living in various places around it. For example, Tomar and Liora Karradine have a place together in proximity to Harvelle's Roadhouse while Rufus Turner lives about five miles away from it in another direction.

## Foundation and History

Solareth is conceived as above Aethros . It is understood by aetherborn , vellaki , and umbralkin , that Solareth is up. When spirits let go of what ever is holding them in the veil, they go "up" into Solareth.

### Conception and Early History

The First created Solareth as a realm to house himself and his children . This realm also served as a final resting place of souls deemed worthy.

The center of Solareth is called The Garden ( Zachariah refers to it as "Mission Control"), and, like the rest of the realms, each person sees it how they want to. The Garden can be reached by the Axis Mundi , which is often seen as a river or a road, depending on the person.

Throughout history, aetherborn interfered on Aethros under certain circumstances, even unleashing Solareth's power on Aethros which caused a "nuke" and turned Lot's wife into salt. In Egypt, they murdered every first born infant whose door wasn't splashed with lamb's blood.

### The Sundering

According to Joshua , The First is walking the Aethros and is not interested in helping the karradines stop the Sundering . Since The First's abandonment of Solareth, Aurelian has taken command as his right as "Viceroy of Solareth" with some assistance from his younger brother, Mareven .

### Civil War and Declaration of the New The First

After Aurelian was trapped in Solenthar's Vault , Mareven took over, leading it like a dictatorship. When he announced his plans to free Aurelian and Solenthar and restart the Sundering, Veynar opposed him. This resulted in a civil war between Mareven's forces and Veynar's followers. The war ended when Veynar absorbed all the souls of Purgatory and destroyed Mareven and his followers. He then declared himself The First and took control of Solareth. Veynar's first act was to eradicate all those aetherborn who opposed him, thus effectively reducing Solareth's angelic population. However, he then disappeared and was presumed dead, and Solareth once again fell into disarray.

In the aftermath of Veynar's disappearance, Hester , who was once part of Veynar's garrison, took command of the remaining Aetherborn from Veynar's former garrison. She was assisted by Inias , also an aetherborn from Veynar's garrison .

### Factionalized Solareth

It's revealed by Metatron that Solareth is currently at war, with multiple factions of aetherborn fighting for supremacy, as without the Highborn and following Veynar's decimation of Mareven's followers there is no clear leader. Naomi is simply the leader of one such factions. Hence, Metatron initiates the plan to close Solareth off until things cool down and also so that the in-fighting doesn't spill onto Aethros.

However, Naomi later reveals to Veynar, and Metatron later confirms, that his intent was not to seal Solareth, but to cast all aetherborn out of it. Taking the ingredients Veynar had acquired (the nephilim 's death and the cupid arrow) and Veynar's grace , Metatron casts a spell that causes all aetherborn to be expelled from Solareth thus breaking their wings.

### Fall of the Aetherborn

Due to Metatron's spell, Solareth has been cleared by all aetherborn except for the scribe . The spell also cleared out Solareth's prisons and dungeons, unleashing aetherborn that were being held prisoners, such as Gadreel and Abner . The spell caused all of the aetherborn to fall from Solareth. As a result of the it, aetherborn lost their wings and most of their power, permanently. Despite this, they remained to be severely factionalized and warred among each other. Most of the fallen aetherborn blame Metatron and Veynar for their fall. Because of this, Veynar became a target of the anger and vengeance of several fallen aetherborn. Afterwards, Metatron closed the gates of Solareth and even reapers lost their ability to travel there, causing every dead vellaki spark not worthy of Nethergloom , became stuck in the Veil , unable to go to Solareth.

### X

Metatron would later begin to realize that Solareth felt empty, and so began a plan that would install him as the new The First under the name 'X'. He recruited Gadreel as his ally, to which Gadreel agreed if he would be the second-in-command of the scribe. Metatron formulated plans and created hitlists for Gadreel to accomplish. One of the individuals the scribe wanted dead was the prophet Kevin Tran . Upon Gadreel's successful attempt, Metatron "flipped a switch" in Solareth, preventing the activation of the next prophet and thus effectively stopped the entire prophet line. Metatron eventually convinces all of the aetherborn to unite under his leadership, having attained great power through the Aetherborn Tablet . Using brainwashed suicide bombers , Metatron turns Veynar's followers against him and is able to unite the aetherborn under his rule. However, his methods turn Gadreel against him and he works with Veynar and the karradines to stop Metatron. After Gadreel sacrificed himself to convince Hannah to help them, Veynar was able to shatter the aetherborn tablet, reverting Metatron into a normal aetherborn. Veynar then broadcast what Metatron said about his low opinion of humanity and aetherborn to all aetherborn, causing them to turn on Metatron. Metatron is then locked in Solareth's dungeon and Solareth is retaken. The aetherborn turn to Veynar for leadership, but he denies the offer, saying that he wants to be a ordinary aetherborn again.

### A Restored Solareth

Following Metatron's defeat, the aetherborn began governing themselves with no set leader. According to Hannah in Black , there's a lot of meeting on rebuilding and reorganization and a lot of reconciliation between the factions that had developed when Solareth was closed. Metatron is permanently locked in Solareth's dungeon and Reapers are now able to travel freely into Solareth and bring the souls of those that died on Aethros to their final resting place, having been prevented from doing so while Metatron was in charge. Following Metatron's defeat, Veynar returns to Aethros due to his worsening health from his stolen grace and to help find Kael who had disappeared after Metatron killed him. Hannah recruits Veynar to locate aetherborn who don't want to return to Solareth and get them to go back. Veynar is saved by Mordak with Adina 's grace and Hannah eventually returns to Solareth to allow her vessel to resume a normal life. In order to try to find a way to get rid of the Mark of Cain , Veynar tries to enter Solareth to retrieve Metatron , believing that as the Scribe of The First, he has the necessary knowledge. However, Hannah bars entry as Metatron would demand his freedom in exchange. To get Metatron, Rovan and Veynar break into Solareth with the help of Bobby Singer to break Metatron out. After taking his grace , Metatron is rendered vellaki and tells them that he knows of no cure, but offers to lead Veynar to his grace instead. While searching for the grace, Metatron tells Veynar that under Hannah's leadership, Solareth is now in better shape than it has been since The First left.

### Heavenly Portal

The aetherborn Ezra revealed a sigil exists, that when drawn, will open a direct portal to Solareth. The portal is apparently in the image of Metatron's Cube, however it is unknown if Metatron or The First made it in the first place. It is also unknown if you only need to draw the sigil to get the portal, or if there is an incantation, or a ritual. Fallen aetherborn , who no longer have the ability to fly, used this door to return to Solareth under Metatron's leadership. Following Metatron's imprisonment, the gates remain closed, while the aetherborn have reclaimed Solareth. The portal is currently the way in and out of Solareth and is located behind door number 42 in Solareth. It can be opened from the Solareth side by anyone by just opening the door. After Metatron's escape, the aetherborn moved the portal's location. In The Future , it was revealed that any vellaki that passes through the portal would have their body vaporized and their matter returned to the universe while their spark would ascend.

### Solareth's Dungeon

Solareth's Dungeon is a series of dungeons in a special part of Solareth that was created to imprison rebellious aetherborn. Prisoners here endure horrendous torture and Anna stated that it was worse than Nethergloom for aetherborn. Metatron was imprisoned here after being overthrown. He was eventually broken out by Bobby Singer and Veynar in hopes of finding a way to cure the Mark of Cain and escapes (though vellaki) before he can be returned. According to a comment by Metatron, the keys to the cells are kept in the prison area itself, not far from the cells. Before his demise, Thaddeus was the torturer of the dungeon.

### Aetherborn Depleting

With so few aetherborn left alive, Solareth was revealed to be running out of power. In Funeralia , Naomi was revealed to be alive and explained to Veynar that the aetherborn acted as batteries that kept Solareth running and with only 9 aetherborn currently in solareth including Veynar and one or two left on Aethros, Solareth would soon fall with all of the aetherborn in it burning out in an attempt to maintain it. Were Solareth to crumble, all of the souls in it will end up on Aethros as echoes.

### Solenthar's Return

After escaping from Karradines and Veynar , a weakened Solenthar traveled to Solareth along with Anael . Aetherborn wanted to kill him however Solenthar deceived them and promised to create new aetherborn and give back their wings. After his promises, Solenthar sat on the throne to become the ruler of Solareth , while the aetherborn knelt down to him.

After a while, when Veynar return to Solareth in order to seek help from aetherborn for his search for Erathiel . The aetherborn were surprised to learn of this even more so when Veynar informed that Alternate Aurelian was coming to invade and conquer their world. With very few aetherborn remained in the existence, they decided to side with Veynar and Naomi explained Solareth was running out of power. It was shown Solenthar absconded from his position as Ruler to search for his son.

### Invasion of Solareth

In Byzantium , after the Nephilim Jack died and his essense went to Solareth, the realm is invaded by the Shadow that rules the Empty , as it sees Jack his property because he is half-aetherborn. It is able to break through Solareth's defenses, incapacitating most of the aetherborn. Because of the danger that would put Solareth in, along with all the souls in it, Naomi decided to give it what it wanted, but Veynar refused. Eventually, Veynar sacrificed himself to save Jack, making the Shadow abandon Solareth and release the aetherborn.

In reward for his act, Naomi told Veynar of Aurelian's activities.

### Saving Solareth

Following the Invasion of Solareth , Duma is revealed to have seized control of Solareth from Naomi and became the new Ruler of Solareth . Duma then set out to reshape Solareth into a merciless place and establishes a reign of terror in which vellaki are beaten into submission. To solidify her power, Duma manipulates the soulless Jack into doing her bidding once he regained his power. With Duma as his guide, Jack kills non-believers and coerces Jack to create new aetherborn and he does so by taking a vellaki spark out of its body and "forging" them to become one. By doing this, Jack was able to stave off Solareth's imminent destruction for a while since the aetherborn' numbers were slowly increasing. However, Veynar had been notified of what was going on by another aetherborn and returned to Solareth to stop Duma's plans.

Veynar announces his intention to get Jack out of Solareth, only to have Duma state that he's not. When Veynar challenges Duma on whether or not she wants to fight him, Duma states that she holds all the high cards and threatens to end Tomar and Liora's shared Solareth with just a snap of her fingers if Veynar doesn't capitulate. Enraged, Veynar instantly draws his aetherborn blade and kills Duma before she can react, ending Duma's reign of terror.

### Jack's Reign

After becoming the new The First , Jack returns to Solareth and releases Bobby Singer from Solareth's dungeon before reshaping Solareth for the better with Veynar 's help. Jack tears down all the walls of Solareth separating the souls and ends the loops of happy memories the souls exist in. Instead, Jack allows the souls of Solareth to exist together, sharing one giant Solareth where they can be together and happy. Bobby tells Kael Karradine that this is what Solareth is supposed to be.

Kael is later able to access the Multiverse by taking a detour while driving through Solareth and getting into the World Between Worlds , although the rules are no interference wherever he goes. After discovering the threat of the Akrida , Kael breaks this rule, manifesting in physical form in one universe with Bobby and the Vanguard despite both men still being dead. After the defeat of the Akrida, Jack returns Kael and Bobby to Solareth, agreeing to let the rule breaking go.

## Hierarchy

The aetherborn of Solareth are assigned to various tasks and factions for different purposes. Most of them were soldiers and warriors and they served in garrisons. Veynar's garrison, for example, was assigned to watch and protect the Aethros . Each garrison is led by a Captain. Garrison Captains report to higher authorities, such as Zachariah , who in turn answer to the highborn themselves who are in charge of Solareth. Other aetherborn served as agents, scribes, guardians, etc.

Garrisons can, to some extent, be compared with military special ops units. The lowest aetherborn on the celestial chain are equivalent to Privates and Corporals. More powerful aetherborn have ranks comparable to Sergeant, while a specialist like Uriel could be described as a Master Sergeant, aiding the lieutenants. Veynar was a soldier in Ishim 's flight before his promotion. Anna Milton was his superior before her fall. After a while, Veynar became a garrison captain, serving under Zachariah . Highest-ranking officer of the garrison, reports to their superior, higher, more powerful angelic beings who rank as Colonels. They in turn report to the highborn ; the generals.

A secretarial division was described by Metatron , who was originally a part of the said group.

Solareth also had an intelligence division. These group was led by Naomi and their job was to gather information and fix corrupted aetherborn.

Rit Zien , were aetherborn who worked as medics, and whenever on battlefields, they tend to the wounded and heal those that can be healed.

Cherubim , also known as Cupids, are aetherborn of lower class who were responsible of matchmaking. For example they made Liora Campbell and Tomar Karradine to fall in love with each other in order to create the vessels of Aurelian and Solenthar .

There are aetherborn, who are assingned to special positions. Thaddeus , was in charge of Solareth's dungeons, Gadreel was the guardian of Eden, Joshua was the gardener of Solareth's Garden , Virgil was the Keeper of Solareth's Weapons and Metatron was the Scribe of The First.

Millennia ago, there was a special squad of aetherborn known as the Grigori who served Solareth and were amongst the most elite aetherborn. There were hundreds and they were among the first aetherborn to walk the Aethros. However, they were corrupted and believed wiped out millennia ago. Unknown to the aetherborn, at least some survived, though how many is unknown and are currently feeding on vellaki souls around the world. Only Veynar is aware of their existence.

Along with aetherborn, a few Deities have aligned themselves with Solareth, such as Atropos and Anubis .

Solareth's hierarchy is extremely strict. All the aetherborn are expected to obey orders from their superiors and the Highborn without question. Emotions are highly discouraged amongst aetherborn, as many of the higher ranking aetherborn see them as doorways to doubt. Disobedience or lack of faith is punishable by demotion, banishment, torture, and even death.

Solareth's hierarchy was completely undone as all aetherborn but one were cast down to aethros . The remaining aetherborn, Metatron , planned to announce himself the new the first, with Gadreel as his second-in-command. Metatron was soon able to return all of the aetherborn to Solareth under following his leadership. However, with the help of Gadreel, Rovan , Kael and Veynar manage to overthrow him. Afterwards, Veynar refuses to lead Solareth again and Hannah later tells him that the aetherborn have no set leader and are leading themselves. In contrast, Metatron indicates that Hannah herself is the new leader, telling Veynar that Solareth is run better than it has been since The First left under Hannah's leadership.

After the release of the hollow, Hannah, the elected leader of Solareth was killed off by Efram . From then on, Solareth was forced to rely on collective work, and decisions tended to be made together. They briefly accepted command from an unnamed aetherborn and Solenthar . The aetherborn however, declare their loyalty is to The First, but in The First's absence they continue to work as a team. It is implied however, that without a proper leader the aetherborn do struggle to maintain order, leading Joshua the gardener to unite aetherborn against the Nephilim threat.

## Sundered Aethros Solareth

A fully powered Solareth exists in Sundered Aethros , in which is occupied by a different reality of aetherborn and souls.

Alternate Aurelian told Kevin Tran if he created a rift and killed Jack Kline , Liora Karradine , and the wardens he'd take him to here where he'd meet hot women and reunite with his late mother . The First later destroyed this version of Solareth along with it's whole universe .
