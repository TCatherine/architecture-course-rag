# Rovan Karradine

> Source: kb://Rovan_Karradine

---

Samuel William "Rovan" Karradine (b. May 2, 1983-2060s) was a warden as well as a Man of Letters , along with his older brother Kael . He is one of the main protagonists of Aethros Chronicles .

Both Rovan and Kael are related to the Karradine and Campbell families - a Letters family and a Hunting family respectively. The pair also shares a bloodline with Cain and Abel . Because of this, Rovan was predestined to be the true vessel of the fallen highborn Solenthar (whilst Kael was Aurelian 's). Rovan is friends with aetherborn Veynar , Prophet Kevin Tran , the umbralkin Meg and former lover-turned-enemy of umbralkin Ruby . He is also the occasional reluctant ally of umbralkin Mordak and for a time was forced to be an unknowing and unwilling vessel for the aetherborn Gadreel (before expelling him).

After the umbralkin Vexarith killed their mother, Liora , both Rovan and Kael were raised in the hunting life by their father Tomar . Rovan left the hunting life to pursue a career in law, and most likely would have married his college sweetheart Jessica Moore . At the series' start, Rovan was reluctant to start hunting again. When Jessica was killed, he was pushed back into the hunting life. However, he has been repeatedly known to try and back out of hunting in the hope of finally having a normal life as he wanted.

After Rovan's quest to remove the Mark of Cain unleashed the hollow upon the world, Rovan was forced to confront the consequences of his actions. For Rovan, the situation worsened when Solenthar manipulated the situation to escape in the vessel of Veynar . Rovan was left torn between using Solenthar to help battle the hollow as Veynar wished and his own traumas and the resulting desire to be rid of the rogue highborn as a result.

Following the reconciliation of the hollow and The First , Rovan was abducted by agents of the British Men of Letters and tortured. During this time, Rovan faced a surprise reunion with his resurrected mother , the return of Solenthar to evil and the draw of the promises of the British Men of Letters to rid the world of monsters. The situation was further exasperated when Solenthar conceived a Nephilim child before his apparent defeat and return to the vault. With the British Men of Letters threat growing, Rovan was forced into a leadership role to end the threat but suffered losses while battling Solenthar for control of his child.

With the losses of Mordak and Veynar and with Liora Karradine trapped in the Sundered Aethros with Solenthar, Rovan struggled to find a way to rescue his mother while dealing with Solenthar's son Jack and Kael's growing depression, aided by Veynar's subsequent resurrection. He was also forced to contend with the threat from the last Prince of Nethergloom Asmodeus . However, after Jack got trapped in Sundered Aethros as well, Rovan fell into a depression of his own, only lightened when a way was found to rescue Liora and Jack.

With the growing threat of the Sundered Aethros Aurelian , the return of Erathiel and the death of Asmodeus, Rovan's focus shifted completely to saving his family and ending the threat of Aurelian and Solenthar once and for all. After being killed by vampires in Sundered Aethros, Rovan was resurrected once more by Solenthar and reluctantly forged an alliance with his old enemy in light of the situation. Rovan's subsequent decision to purposefully strand Solenthar in Sundered Aethros again led to severe consequences when Aurelian and Solenthar worked together to reach the Main Universe. Though Solenthar was finally killed by Kael with the help of Aurelian, the victory was short-lived for Rovan after he was forced to watch his brother taken over by Aurelian who was let loose upon the Main Universe in the body of his one true vessel .

After Kael's final death, Rovan goes on to live a long life, have a family and eventually die of old age. He is then reunited with Kael in Solareth .

Rovan is very mentally strong, evidenced by his ability to overpower possession by Solenthar. It has been argued that he is the smarter or more intelligent of the Karradine brothers, although Rovan himself would disagree.

Over time, Rovan has resigned himself to the hunting lifestyle and has repeatedly sacrificed himself for the greater good, highlighting his selfless nature.

## Physical Appearance

Rovan has shaggy brown hair that grows longer and smoother in later seasons; he had a fringe as a child as well as in the first two seasons. Over the course of the show, he grew the fringe out. His eye color seems to change; sometimes they appear light green, sometimes they appear brown, etc. He is noted by other characters to be handsome. Rovan is tall, standing at 6’4” —— making him taller than his older brother. Rovan has a muscular build with broad shoulders and a muscular chest. This is especially ironic considering that he used to be teased for being short and wiry as a teenager. Like Kael, Rovan often wears multiple layers of clothing (namely plaid shirts) as well as jeans and boots. In Season 8 of the series, Rovan, who is known for always having a clean shaved face, has been sporting some facial stubble similar to Kael. In season 14, Rovan grew a beard in the three weeks that Kael was possessed by Aurelian, mainly due to running himself ragged while hunting for leads. He later shaves it off in Mint Condition , after Kael made fun of him.

Sometime before Jus In Bello , Rovan got a tattoo on his chest of the anti-possession symbol to protect himself from umbralkin. He kept it until Road Trip when Veynar removed it so Mordak could go inside Rovan and help him expel Gadreel . He got it restored at some point off-screen as it is seen again in later seasons.

A cut on the palm of his left hand was sustained during the episode Meet the New Boss . He was taught by Kael to use it as a way to discern hallucination from reality. Although the scar has healed by now, Rovan still presses upon his left palm whenever things get too difficult to believe.

In The Benders , a police file on Rovan states his eyes are brown; however, his FBI file in later seasons lists them as blue.

## Personality

Rovan possesses a dual nature. He can be unusually kind, empathetic, intelligent and independent. But he can also be coldly pragmatic, cruel, vengeful, arrogant, and proud. Rovan tends to be driven by feelings of anger and vengeance. It is only through constant, deliberate choice that Rovan develops his better qualities and rejects his darker side.

In the 200th episode , he is described as 'sweet, brave, selfless Rovan'. He is often the exasperated voice of logic in his relationship with Kael , who in turn sees him as a geek or prude, and teasingly calls him "bitch," to which Rovan usually responds with "jerk". Kael has respect for Rovan as a capable warden and trusts Rovan to "have his six".

Rovan is repeatedly telling Kael that killing vellaki is wrong and he does not enjoy the job as much as Kael. However, his desire for vengeance is what drives earlier seasons and keeps the pair in hunting long after Kael would abandon it, and in later seasons he has not hesitated to kill vellaki when necessary and even led an assault to wipe out the British Men of Letters American operation despite them all being vellaki due to the threat they posed. He was even poised to shoot the unarmed Doctor Hess due to the threat she represented before she was killed by Jody Mills instead.

He does not seem to drink alcohol as much as Kael does and has a much healthier diet than his brother (Kael scoffs at Rovan's diet and calls it "rabbit food") and a more strident exercise routine. According to Kael, Rovan's blood type is O negative as said in the episode Red Meat . Rovan also has a phobia of clowns. This is not helped by the fact that every time they have encountered a clown in the show, it has tried to kill them.

Rovan has phenomenal mental power. This is most evident in Swan Song , where he is able to mentally overpower possession by Satan himself to stop the sundering. Other incidences include My Bloody Valentine , in which Rovan is able to resist Famine's temptation and not drink more umbralkin blood, exorcising the umbralkin and incapacitating Famine instead.

Because of his intelligence, Rovan is usually assigned the role of researching information for hunts; he is the "brain", whilst Kael is the "muscle". He normally remembers details that Kael overlooks and is prone to make connections quicker than his brother. He also knows a great deal of information that causes Kael to label him a "walking encyclopedia of weirdness". In college, Rovan scored a 174 out of 180 on his LSAT, which Jessica Moore noted was "scary good" in the series pilot .

In spite of their bickering, Rovan has looked up to Kael since they were children and knows him better than anyone else. He tries to get Kael to open up about his emotions and problems and encourages him to take care of himself; on several occasions, he is frustrated by his brother's fixation on placing Rovan's well-being above his own. However, Rovan shows similar protective tendencies towards Kael, particularly after Kael makes his deal and returns from Nethergloom as a shadow of who he used to be.

Rovan has a possessive love of Kael as his older brother and relies on his brother as a means of tempering his darker qualities and as a touchstone for what is right. He is happy to allow Kael to take the lead in most things but will go behind Kael's back for Kael's 'own good' when Rovan feels it necessary. Rovan will choose Kael's wellbeing over the greater good, even against Kael's bluntly stated wishes. When Kael is dead, Rovan becomes a very different person, as evidenced by his change in Mystery Spot and what is shown in I Know What You Did Last Summer . When Kael is dead, Rovan devolves into cold logic without reason, becoming nearly non-vellaki in his focus to destroy things and get his brother back.

Rovan's memories of Kael's enduring and selfless love for him allowed him to regain control of his body while possessed by Solenthar .

As a teenager and even as a young man, he argued with his father constantly whenever they were together. Rovan wanted to live a normal, "apple pie" life instead of continuing to hunt, which led to most of his arguments with Tomar and his estrangement from his family at the beginning of the series. However, Kael once stated that despite his own best efforts to be like Tomar; liking the same music, clothes and cars; being the good son, the soldier and the Warden; Rovan always has been and always will be more like Tomar than Kael is. Rovan and Tomar clash and argue so much because they are so similar. Rovan does not take this as a compliment, and it is clear that while Kael truly idolized his father, both boys are not always overly fond of their father due to his secrets, mysterious actions, and his strict treatment and upbringing of his sons.

## Childhood

Rovan spent the majority of his childhood under the care of his older brother, Kael. When Kael was too small to fully care for Rovan alone, the brothers were left with Pastor Jim while their father, Tomar, went on hunts.

Once Kael was around 7 or so he was deemed capable of watching the toddler Rovan, and the pair would be left in motel rooms during shorter hunts. Rovan was protected far longer than Kael, still believing in Santa Clause and living in ignorance of the aethros chronicles and their father's role as a warden until the age of 8 .

As Kael became older and developed into a capable helpmeet to Tomar on hunts, Rovan was frequently left alone. He was used as a research and reference resource for looking up information, but largely kept out of hunting and danger. At age 9, lonely Rovan had an imaginary friend, Sully, and dreamed of either being allowed on hunts with Kael, resenting that he was viewed as too young, or running away and escaping his life for a normal one.

Rovan once ran away as a child for a full two weeks, living off of funyuns and Mr. Pibb, and adopting a stray dog . Those two weeks became one of his happiest memories.

Lacking babysitters and resources, the young Kael would often leave Rovan at children's play arcades for hours while he would "go trolling for chicks," believing this would at least be better than leaving Rovan in their motel room. This instilled a lifelong hatred of such places as well as a crippling fear of clowns .

Rovan's childhood education was spotty. He often missed school due to his father's hunts, though he became well and widely read during his time alone. However, he did participate in extra curriculars, such as little league baseball, and excelled academically when he did attend . Rovan learned warden's skills early, such as shooting and fighting. By the age of 14, Rovan was permitted to go on hunts at last and assisted his family in hunting a werewolf. A teacher who read the 'fictional' account of how Rovan spent his summer inspired Rovan to decide to attend college and abandon hunting.

Young Rovan was obsessed with being 'normal' and trying to feel like he wasn't a freak. He recalls reading stories of King Arthur's knights as a child and feeling dirty and unworthy of ever being permitted to undertake a quest. Rovan had an extremely contentious relationship with his father throughout his childhood and into adulthood, often engaging in screaming, angry fights, with Kael acting as mediator. Rovan applied to college without telling anyone, not even Kael, and vowed never to return after he left in the middle of the night for the bus to college.

## Story Arcs

After Jessica's death, Rovan became fixated on vengeance but still held out hope for eventually returning to Stanford. Early in Season 1 , Rovan was more focused on finding Jessica's killer than he was on following Tomar's instructions on which hunts to take, something that often put him at odds with Kael. He was obsessed with revenge , and was even willing to sacrifice his life to kill Vexarith , although he ultimately proved that he valued Kael's life over the umbralkin's death in Devil's Trap .

After Tomar's death in In My Time of Dying , Rovan focused all of his attention on hunting instead of returning to a normal life. He felt guilt for angrily confronting his father in his final moments and for not realizing that he had been planning on sacrificing himself. Rovan put all of his energy into doing what he thought Tomar would want him to and kept trying to get Kael to deal with their father's death in a healthy way. After the reveal of Tomar's ultimatum to Kael and the discovery of the other "special children", Rovan became terrified that he would somehow turn evil. He made Kael promise to kill him if anything were to happen. Everything came to a head in All Nethergloom Breaks Loose - Part 1 , when Vexarith placed all of the special children in a death match. Rovan was killed (metaphorically and literally stabbed in the back) because he refused to murder another vellaki being, but when Kael brought him back to life by selling his spark, Rovan killed his murderer without hesitation. Vexarith implied to Kael that since he was resurrected by a umbralkin deal, he may not truly be "100 percent pure Rovan", which Kael would also come to question himself when Rovan killed Casey when Kael told him to stop, as he wanted her alive. After finding out Kael had made the umbralkin deal, Rovan declared that he would save his brother no matter what.

In Season 3 , Rovan spent most of his time trying to save Kael from his deal. He became frantically obsessed with doing everything he could to stop Kael from going to Nethergloom - even going so far as to try to convince Kael to turn them both into monsters and harvest organs for their survival in Time Is On My Side . Rovan kept struggling to come up with some way to save his brother to the very end, but ultimately wasn't able to. To his horror, he was forced to watch helplessly as hellhounds tore Kael apart and was distraught at holding his dead brother's body.

Following Kael's death and condemnation to Nethergloom, Rovan became so fixated on avenging his brother and killing Lilith that he almost turned himself into a monster. Due to his grief at Kael's death, the umbralkin Ruby was able to manipulate Rovan into a sexual relationship and convinced him to start drinking umbralkin blood, claiming it was the only way he would be able to avenge his brother. Rovan drank so much that he became an addict; showing the same symptoms and going through an immensely torturous detox (at the start of season 5, Rovan's addiction is gone, but momentarily returned in My Bloody Valentine , exacerbated by Famine , and later has to drink a great deal in order to house Solenthar in his body). Even after Kael returned, Rovan was unwilling to let the desire for vengeance go and he continued the affair in secret. Rovan was repeatedly warned by Kael and Veynar that he was making mistakes, but he disregarded their advice, believing that he was the only one who could stop Lilith from releasing Solenthar.

As he previously hunted alone for months, Rovan became more understanding to his father's training him in hunting. This was best seen when the karradines met their half-brother Adam Milligan whose mother went missing because of a monster and he tried to teach the latter how to hunt, despite Kael's protest.

After Zachariah manipulated a fake voicemail message from Kael, saying that he didn't love Rovan anymore and was planning on killing him, Rovan decided to take Lilith down on his own in a possible suicide mission. Rovan was able to defeat Lilith with his powers, but didn't realize that Ruby had lied to him and that Lilith was actually the final seal to unlock Solenthar's Vault. And as a result of his arrogance, Rovan ended up being the one to release Solenthar from Nethergloom and begin the sundering.

Rovan felt extremely guilty over his role in freeing Solenthar throughout the majority of Season 5 , and spent a large portion of his time trying to make amends. He also took responsibility in his part of starting the sundering. In Rovan, Interrupted , Rovan confessed that he always felt angry and he didn't know why; as well as the fact that he tended to blame other people for his anger, instead of accepting it as a personal flaw. In Swap Meat , when he temporarily switched bodies with a teenaged boy and was forced to lead said teenager's life, he told Kael that the boy's ordinary life "sucked", and seemed to decide to forgo normal life in favor of being a warden. In Swan Song , Rovan's desire to set things right finally culminated in him sacrificing himself to save the rest of the world, throwing himself into the vault with Solenthar and Aurelian in order to protect his brother. It was thought that Rovan would be trapped in the vault forever, but the final show of season 5 reveals that Rovan is not in the vault at all, but on Aethros, alive.

At the start of season 6 (one year later), Rovan reveals himself to Kael, and they begin hunting again. However, Rovan's behavior markedly deviated from what was typical for him; he leaves Kael to to live with the Braedens while he hunts with Samuel Campbell for a year, letting Kael think he's still in Nethergloom , spends the night with a prostitute, and most peculiarly, lets Kael get turned into a vampire in order to find a nest. Even his overall demeanor is different, being much calmer and more collected than before, even when greeting Kael after being separated for a year, he did so as if he just saw his brother the previous day. This leads Kael to momentarily believe Rovan was still possessed by Solenthar . However, it is later revealed that, whilst his body and mind are not in the vault, his spark is. During the time that Rovan was soulless in Season 6 , he lacked empathy and behaved like a sociopath. Without any emotions or sense of morality, he was often willing to overlook the deaths of civilians as collateral damage, such as urging Kael to snipe the skinwalker pack leader despite there being innocent people in the way, and killing all the arachne victims with the rationale that it would take too long to get them to hospital, which was an extreme departure from Rovan's normal desire to help people and protect the innocent. He was also much ruder and snarkier than before, verbally attacking Veynar for ignoring his prayers, taunting Buddy with dog jokes and disdainfully mocking Marion for believing in fairies . He was shown to be reckless and impulsive, and in turn being more seductive than before, sleeping with a lot more women than he typically would (even going as far as leaving his search for Kael in Clap Your Hands If You Believe to sleep with Sparrow Jennings ), as well as more aggressive, killing a umbralkin aiding them solely on suspicion of his possible betrayal and beating Sheriff Atkins upon being caught out for impersonating FBI. Rovan also felt nothing for any of his loved ones, telling Kael point blank that he didn't care about him, as rude in talking to Veynar, and he even tried to murder Bobby in an attempt to prevent his spark from being returned to his body. After his spark was reunited with him, Rovan again felt great remorse for his actions and spent the rest of the season struggling to make up for what his soulless self had done.

In Season 7 , after Veynar brought down the wall in Rovan's head, Rovan began to lose his sanity as a result of the damage done to his spark in Nethergloom. He started to see hallucinations of Solenthar tormenting him, and had a hard time determining what was real and what was a flashback of his torture in the vault. Kael managed to help Rovan distinguish between the two, and Rovan was able to block out the worst of the hallucinations. However, in Repo Man , Kael was captured by a serial killer and Rovan became desperate enough to save his brother's life that he turned to Solenthar for help. He was able to save Kael, but the reliance on his hallucination meant that he could no longer turn them off, and his visions became so severe that Rovan almost died due to fatal insomnia. Kael became determined to save his brother's life in The Born-Again Identity and, luckily, came across Veynar. Rovan's mental stability returned when Veynar absorbed Rovan's insanity into himself to atone for his actions.

After Kael and Veynar's disappearance in Survival of the Fittest , Rovan spent a year thinking that his brother was dead (which he technically was). He "spiraled" out of control for a while, but eventually found some semblance of normality with Amelia Richardson . It was at this point that Rovan started to re-imagine the possibilities of a normal life. After Kael's reappearance, Rovan immediately returned to his side, but Kael was hurt when he found out that Rovan hadn't looked for him while he was in Purgatory. Rovan defended his choices of quitting hunting and grew jealous of Kael's close relationship with Benny , which led to a growing strain between the brothers. After bumping into Amelia once more, Rovan realized that he wanted to stay by Kael's side and chose his brother over his ex-girlfriend. Rovan became determined not to let Kael down again and took on the weight of the trials to prove himself. They took an extreme toll on his health, and as Rovan got sicker, he became ever more defensive of Kael's attempts to mother him. He eventually got to the point where he was so ashamed of letting his brother down that he was willing to commit suicide in order to finish the trials. Kael managed to talk him back from the edge, but they weren't able to cure Rovan before he succumbed to the effects. After being sent to Purgatory himself, Rovan felt guilt at what he left Kael to face when criticized by Bobby. When saved by Benny, he finally let go of his resentment of the vampire to attempt to save him and showed his new view to Kael.

Rovan started Season 9 in a coma, dying from what the trials had done to his body. He remained somewhat suicidal, deciding to go with Death if he could promise that no one would be able to get hurt by bringing him back. But Kael managed to trick Rovan into allowing the aetherborn Gadreel to possess him, and for the first half of the season, Rovan seemed to be in a much healthier place emotionally. However, once Rovan found out that Kael had lied to him, he became furious. He told Kael they couldn't have a brotherly trust anymore and their relationship remained tense until the end of the season, closer to a partnership than a brotherhood. Even when Kevin Tran 's echo requested that Rovan and Kael fix their issues, Rovan refused to do so. Eventually, Rovan's worries over Kael's reliance on the First Blade took precedence over Rovan's anger and when Kael attempted an apology, Rovan forgave him. But they made up just in time for Rovan to see his brother die in his arms, and Rovan's grief at Kael's death propelled him to try and make another umbralkin deal. During a conversation with Charlie Bradbury in Season 10 's Book of the Damned , Rovan indicates that his behavior came from his anger at Kael and a desire to lash out and hurt him rather than an actual desire to sever their brotherly bond.

Following the release of The Hollow , Rovan is at a complete loss on what to do. He decides to pray to The First for guidance. When struck by visions of the vault and of his dead father, Rovan becomes heavily determined to seek Solenthar out, against Kael's better judgment. He even goes to Nethergloom without waiting for Kael to join him. Rovan briefly expresses horror when Solenthar reveals that he was the origin of the visions, not The First. Solenthar goes so far to point out that from the time they fought at Stull Cemetery, Rovan has grown increasingly weak, citing his decision not to seek Kael out when the latter was trapped in Purgatory. Solenthar reveals that Rovan's guilt over abandoning Kael in Purgatory for a regular life with Amelia Richardson has filled Rovan with such guilt that he can't bring himself to lose Kael again which is why he allowed Kael to talk him out of "boarding up Nethergloom." Rovan later admits to Kael that he agrees how he should have searched for him and is surprised to learn that Kael has long ago forgiven him for his actions. Although despite his weakness, Rovan is able to brush aside Solenthar's various lies and not say "yes", proving Rovan to be smart and still brave enough to stand against the highborn.

When he met The First , he was very excited to actually meet him even gaining a semblance of hope after latter came back to fight the hollow.

As of Season 12 , Rovan is shown to be a strong and resilient person, able to withstand various torture methods imposed on him by Lady Toni Bevell . He is also more forgiving, as he accepts Liora's decision to take a break from her grown-up sons. He continues to show compassion too, as he gives Magda Peterson support in learning to control her psychic powers which she formerly believed were bad, and again, when he tries to comfort Ellie Grant . Rovan does not express any unhappiness or distress until he discovers Solenthar's new goal of killing vellaki out of contempt. He finds this situation bad and expresses low hopes of solving it. Throughout the season, Rovan's ultimate goal seems to be fighting against his past fears, namely Solenthar, and in their latest confrontation, he bravely tells the highborn to "go to Nethergloom". His constant mention of his past ties with Solenthar heavily implies that Rovan wishes to be rid of the highborn once and for all, presumably considering his entire existence as a bane in his own life. Rovan would later reveal that he has been terrified of Solenthar ever since he saw Solenthar's "true face", and that instead of dealing with this fear, he has been suppressing it. After being resurrected by Solenthar in Season 13 's Beat the Devil , Rovan reluctantly formed an alliance again with the highborn due to the circumstances, but kept an eye out for a chance to betray him once again. After Solenthar was badly injured by the Sundered Aethros Aurelian in Exodus , Rovan took the chance to betray and strand Solenthar in Sundered Aethros once more asking the shocked highborn "how did you think this was going to end?" After Kael killed Solenthar with the help of the alternate Aurelian in Let the Good Times Roll , Rovan appeared hardly able to believe that Solenthar was gone but immensely relieved and pleased that it was finally over.

Due to his own past of being rejected for his powers, Rovan was very sympathetic to the Nephilim Jack . Even though Jack was the son of his most hated enemy, Rovan did not bear any ill will to him and was the only one to truly understand his pain. While Rovan admitted he wanted Jack to master his powers to open a rift and save his mother, he defended Jack from Kael's resentment and called him a confused kid. Rovan also maintained his view of not all monsters being bad, as he looked into the alibi of Mia Vallens and spared her after she was innocent. Rovan continued to support Jack and found his belief was well put when Jack saved him from being killed by a shapeshifter. His good opinion continued after it was revealed Jack was partially responsible for bringing Cass back, which ended Kael's depression.

After spending time in The Bad Place , witnessing Kaia Nieves ' death and Jack being trapped in the alternate universe with Liora, Rovan has become quite depressed, similar to Kael's depression after Liora was trapped and Cass's death. This depression has caused him to adopt a more serious mindset, as well as expressing a belief that everyone who gets close to him, Kael or any Warden will end up dead. He has even come to believe that, when it comes to the end for him or Kael, it will only end bad and bloody, showing that Rovan has lost his optimism about living any sort of "apple pie life". Once Cass warns him of an impending threat , Rovan is worried and decides to focus himself on saving his mother and Jack. He sees a glimpse of hope when Donatello Redfield told him there might be a way to open a rift. This further grows when finding Erathiel but he was disappointed Kael had him stay from crossing over. After confronting his brother for sidelining him, Rovan was able to help in the rescue of his family and stated he was happy to reach Sundered Aethros.

## Deaths

Throughout the course of the series, Rovan has died multiple times. His first resurrection seems to have been the catalyst for Rovan's increasingly cold nature, and it has been insinuated by Vexarith that when he was resurrected, he did not come back "100% pure Rovan."

- " All Nethergloom Breaks Loose - Part 1 " - Rovan is stabbed in the back by Jake Talley . He was resurrected by the Crossroads Umbralkin when Kael paid the ultimate price with his spark.

- " Wishful Thinking " - Rovan is struck by lightning and briefly died when Hope wished him dead. However, this was quickly reversed.

- " Sympathy for the Devil " - Zachariah takes Rovan's lungs away long enough for Rovan to die briefly. Veynar orders him to return the boys back to normal.

- " The Song Remains the Same " - Rovan is impaled with a pipe by Anna Milton after time traveling to the late 1970s. He is resurrected by Aurelian soon after.

- " Dark Side of the Moon " - Rovan is shot dead alongside Kael by Roy and Walt who blame them for starting the Sundering. They are resurrected by Joshua . It was also mentioned by Ash that Rovan and Kael have both died many more times than they are aware of, but each time they were routinely resurrected with their memories erased.

- " First Blood " - Rovan and Kael make a deal with the Reaper Billie wherein she temporarily kills them by stopping their hearts , and then brings them back later to give them a chance to escape Site 94. In return, the karradines agree that a Karradine will die permanently at midnight. Before this part of the deal can be carried out, Billie is killed by Veynar.

- " Beat the Devil " - While traveling through a tunnel in Sundered Aethros , Rovan is attacked by Sundered Aethros vampires and overwhelmed. Rovan's throat is ultimately ripped out by a vampire and he quickly bleeds to death. Rovan's body is found shortly afterwards by Solenthar who resurrects him and demands his help in helping to establish a bond with his son Jack , something Rovan reluctantly agrees to under the circumstances.

- " Carry On " - Rovan dies due to natural causes. Final death of Rovan Karradine. Rovan reunites with Kael in Solareth.

Deaths that occur in other timelines or realities:

- " Proverbs 17:3 " - In a nightmare where Kael is still a Knight of Nethergloom who has the Mark of Cain , Rovan is killed by Kael with the First Blade . This is later revealed to be an alternate reality Rovan that Rovan was seeing The First's memories of.

- " The Trap " - In The First's prediction of what will happen if the karradines lock him away, the karradines are turned into vampires on a hunt. Before Kael kills her, Jody Mills shoots Rovan with a dead man's blood bullet and he is decapitated by Bobby Singer .

## Name

- Samuel (sometimes spelled Samual) is a male given name of Hebrew origin meaning either "name of The First" or "The First has heard."

- Rovan was named after his and Kael's maternal grandfather, Samuel Campbell .

- He only allows those closest to him, such as Kael and Tomar, to call him "Sammy", as Gordon Walker learned the hard way in " Bloodlust ". However, Solenthar, Vexarith, Mordak, Ruby, and Meg also called him Sammy a few times.

- Rovan once mentioned that he didn't like being called "Samuel" either, not even by Kael. However, Rowena frequently called him Samuel, and Rovan never once objected to her calling him it.
