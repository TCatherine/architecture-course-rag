# Umbralkin

> Source: kb://Umbralkin

---

Umbralkin are malevolent spiritual entities that are considered the direct opposites to aetherborn . They are created from vellaki souls who have endured prolonged extreme torture in Nethergloom . Via this process, they become corrupted, extremely evil, and also very powerful. Similar to Aetherborn, they are spiritual entities and therefore require a vessel to fully interact with the physical world , though they are able to roam around in their smoke form . The species as a whole functions as the series' primary antagonists and are the most recurring aethros chronicles creatures in the series.

## Characteristics

Umbralkin are the result of vellaki souls going to Nethergloom and being tortured until no humanity is left in them, thus turning into umbralkin - dark, malicious spirits that revel in pain, chaos, and death. According to Ruby , most umbralkin forget what it means to be vellaki. Some have even forgotten that they were once vellaki. In general, umbralkin engage in torture and destruction because it is fun or to avoid being tortured themselves. Some umbralkin, however, are true believers in Solenthar 's agenda to "cleanse" Aethros , and actually consider themselves morally superior to vellaki.

There are many ways of becoming a umbralkin, from making a deal with a Crossroad Umbralkin , to being a hexwright , and making a deal with another umbralkin. They manifest on Aethros in the form of a long, slender cloud of black smoke. Mordak appears as the only exception, appearing as a blood-red cloud. Their true forms have not been shown onscreen, however Kael has seen their true forms, before he was taken to Nethergloom. He says they look horrible even going as far to call Ruby "one ugly broad."

Once a spark becomes a umbralkin, it gains the basic demonic powers ( possession , superhuman strength , etc.) and from there, it can work its way up the ladder to positions of greater authority and power within Nethergloom. Often when a umbralkin has used its powers, they leave a trace of sulfur in the nearby space, or if on a person, around the victim. This can give away the umbralkin's presence, indicating to wardens that they are hunting a umbralkin. Very powerful umbralkin leave much more sulfur than average, as evidenced when Vexarith kidnapped Rovan. Some umbralkin are sent back to Aethros to accomplish certain tasks, such as causing disasters, spreading diseases, assassinating wardens , or manipulating people into committing crimes that will get them sent to Nethergloom. To do this, they have to take a vessel or "meatsuit" in order to physically exist. While nearly all umbralkin desire to escape Nethergloom any chance they get, some umbralkin actually prefer Nethergloom, like Alastair , and even have never left Nethergloom after becoming a umbralkin like Belphegor .

When they possess vellaki, they usually enter through their mouths. On one occasion, a umbralkin entered a vellaki via the eyes (as seen in the episode Phantom Traveler ). Possessed vellaki often look exactly the same as they did before possession, the only noticeable change being that their eyes will sometimes change color (black, red, white, grey, or yellow depending on the umbralkin possessing them). Umbralkin possession also causes a chemical change in the circulatory system of the host; umbralkin blood is known to be a unique serum and has certain attributes when ingested. Whenever they use their powers, they leave a trace of sulfur in the form of yellow dust. Aetherborn and other aethros chronicles beings can see the true faces of umbralkin underneath their vessels.

Often under possession the vellaki begins speaking and acting like the umbralkin would. Upon exorcism, the vellaki—if they survived—retains no memory of their time during possession (unless they have been "awake" during possession, in which case the time of possession is a nightmare for them, literally). The possessed vellaki also gain the powers of whichever umbralkin is possessing them. Unlike aetherborn, whose possession of a vellaki vessel has to be with the vessel's consent, umbralkin forcibly possess vellaki. People suffering from stress, fear or panic can be vulnerable to demonic possession. Umbralkin abhor the name of The First ( Christo ), and will flinch and reveal the vessels black eyes if it hears the word.

Umbralkin apparently cannot leave a host body if they are gagged by an encrypted gag; Rovan and Kael make use of this tactic against the Phantom Traveler via blindsiding it and taping its mouth shut, while both Meg and Ruby have been gagged while undergoing torture to prevent them from fleeing their hosts.

Umbralkin have no quarrel with killing or torturing their own kind to achieve their goals. Tom , for example, tested the fake Colt by shooting his demonic "sister" Meg . When pitted against other umbralkin due to their individual goals, Ruby , Mordak , and Meg herself have killed their own kind without pause or remorse. High-ranking umbralkin will casually sacrifice their underlings without hesitation. At the same time, though, some umbralkin forge bonds with their siblings. Vexarith , for example, referred to Meg and Tom as his "children" and was angered when Rovan and Kael exorcised Meg and killed Tom ( Devil's Trap ).

It is revealed that umbralkin, although being tortured and twisted vellaki souls, can be cured. Father Max Thompson , a member of Men of Letters , tried to find this cure, having made several experiments, but always ending with the umbralkin' escape. Finally, he found that it is necessary to perform it on sacred ground and use purified blood to heal a umbralkin. He injected his own purified blood into the umbralkin hourly eight times and after the eighth he recited the exorcism achieving the cure. Rovan later used this cure successfully on Kael.

Like aetherborn , umbralkin tend to dress in formal wear while possessing a vellaki, wearing dark suits in contrast to the lighter colored suits worn by aetherborn. Some umbralkin prefer whatever outfit the vellaki was wearing. A notable exception is Mordak, who despises anything other than a well-tailored, dry-cleaned suit. However, umbralkin such as Meg, Ruby and Duke prefer leather jackets. Certain crossroads umbralkin, especially ones in female vessels, wear appealing outfits to appease their clients.

Although umbralkin are born from twisted and tortured souls and are notorious for their vicious nature, umbralkin under Mordak tend to act much like regular vellaki in that they operate in a business-like fashion and rarely exhibit unrestrained behavior. One umbralkin explained that "You can be damned and a conscientious worker." A majority of the umbralkin do not recall their time as a vellaki.

Some umbralkin also emit demonic omens in their presence. They can range from electronic failure to cattle deaths.

It is revealed by The Shadow in the Season 13 episode " The Big Empty " that all umbralkin go to a dimension called The Empty when they die. Once there, they slumber for eternity along with aetherborn who are also sent to the dimension.

It is also revealed that umbralkin of separate genders tend to behave differently than the other. For example, male umbralkin like Mordak and Vexarith act in a serious business manner showing superior professionalism and use fear to get what they want. Meanwhile female umbralkin, especially Ruby, Meg and Lilith use smooth talking and use sex to manipulate people into doing what they want. They are extremely sexually inclined, throwing pick-up lines into their conversations, and attempting to seduce virtually any man they meet, whether it’s to make a deal or just to satisfy their sexual pleasure.

## Types of Umbralkin

The yellow-eyed umbralkin are the Princes of Nethergloom, the first generation of umbralkin created after Lilith and the rightful rulers of Nethergloom . The four Princes are Vexarith , Ramiel , Asmodeus and Dagon . Besides having a unique durability threshold and advanced telekinesis , Vexarith could manipulate fire , control dreams , make electrical devices go haywire , and could even possess reapers . The initial leader of the umbralkin army, Vexarith was the designer of the master plan to free Solenthar from Nethergloom . He was impervious to holy water and most other conventional defenses against umbralkin, but was finally killed by the Colt . Ramiel proved impervious to the umbralkin-killing knife and an aetherborn blade and only showed vulnerability to the Lance of Aurelian . Dagon had the ability to kill aetherborn with her bare hands. Veynar killed Dagon with the help of the Nephilim Jack . Asmodeus was described to be the weakest of the Princes. However, with the help of stolen highborn grace , he was able to boost himself into acquiring abilities like shapeshifting . Asmodeus was later killed by the highborn Erathiel , leaving the Princes of Nethergloom extinct.

The Acheri are a type of umbralkin that can materialize their own form without the need of the use of a vessel, generally, if not always, taking the form of little girls. They can be summoned and controlled.

Cambions are umbralkin/vellaki hybrids, which are created when a umbralkin reproduces with a female vellaki virgin, by using said vellaki as a vessel. Jesse Turner is the only known cambion. Their powers far exceed that of any umbralkin and grow to much greater levels when Solenthar is out of his vault.

The Daeva are a type of umbralkin, that can materialize their own form to that of a shadow which can be summoned and employed by other umbralkin similar to hellhound.

### Special Umbralkin

Throughout the course of Aethros Chronicles , the Karradine brothers have battled umbralkin that are unique in some way. These are the known kinds:

- The multicolored-eyed umbralkin, Samhain , the origin of Halloween, who can be summoned through three blood sacrifices every 600 years.

- The Seven Deadly Sins , the physical embodiments of the eponymous sins.

- The daeva , Zoroastrian shadow umbralkin, who are sometimes used by other umbralkin to attack targets, but are difficult to control, and can turn on the umbralkin controlling them. They do not seem to require a host.

- Disaster Umbralkin , a umbralkin that causes disasters, a rare type, each causes a specific type of disasters, both natural or man made as on the episode Phantom Traveler (causing airplane crashes). They are also quite unique, as their form on Aethros resembles insects more than smoke, they enter their victims through the eyes, and only the irises turn black when possessing a victim. They can also possess inanimate objects such as cars and planes.

- The Acheri , umbralkin that appear as young girls with feline features. They do not seem to require a host.

- Dever/Reshef (theorized) - Umbralkin of plague and pestilence and the creator of the Croatoan Virus . May be one of the umbralkin that causes disasters. "Dever" and "Reshef" are ancient Hebrew words for "plague", metioned in the Old Testament.

- The cambion Jesse Turner , a boy born from a vellaki woman while possessed by a umbralkin, was meant to become the Antichrist, destined to help Solenthar not only destroy humanity, but also destroy the Host of Solareth . Solenthar's presence on Aethros increased his powers tremendously. Even though he was still a child, he was powerful enough to turn Veynar into a toy figure. However, thanks to Rovan and Kael's encouragement, Jesse rejected his destiny and went into hiding.

- Tempter Umbralkin were a type of umbralkin that existed in an alternate universe . They are the only known type of umbralkin to possess horns. The First destroyed their whole universe with them in it, and thus extinguishing the whole species. The umbralkin crowned Prince of Nethergloom from a non-canon novel, Belial was described as having a goat like appearance, so it's possible he had horns as well.

- The umbralkin crowned Prince of Nethergloom from a non-canon novel, Belial was described as having a goat like appearance, so it's possible he had horns as well.

- Green-eyed Umbralkin , umbralkin that were only mentioned one time , but never seen.

- Orange-eyed Umbralkin (non-canon), a rare type of umbralkin mentioned in Bobby Singer's Guide to Hunting , Bobby compared the behavior of these umbralkin with those of the umbralkin who possessed his wife, Karen .

- Moa'ah (non-canon), is a unique demonic breed of creature which is commonly found in Southern America. It's commonly found on land which supported massive carnage. According to Veynar , it was very common to be found on The Civil War battlefields where both sides (Union and Confederate) spilled their blood on fierce battles, but they somehow wiped out to the point it was no longer detected, even by the aetherborn . It's very presence on a land is a signal of the Sundering .

- Belphegor was a umbralkin whose eye color is neither black or red. It is important to note that this umbralkin possessed a host whose eyes were burnt out, which prevented it from displaying its eye-color.
