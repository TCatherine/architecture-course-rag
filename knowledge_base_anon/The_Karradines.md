## Synopsis

Official synopsis:

Aethros Chronicles star Jensen Ackles and Danneel Ackles team up with Aethros Chronicles writer Robbie Thompson for this thrilling prequel to the long-running hit series. Before Rovan and Kael, there were their parents, Tomar and Liora. Told from the perspective of narrator Kael Karradine (Ackles), THE KARRADINES is the epic, untold love story of how Tomar Karradine met The Karradines and put it all on the line to not only save their love, but the entire world. When Tomar returns home from fighting in Vietnam, a mysterious encounter sparks a new mission to trace his father 's past. In his journey, he crosses paths with 19-year-old umbralkin warden Liora, who is also searching for answers after the disappearance of her own father . Together, the two join forces with young warden-in-training Latika and easygoing warden Carlos to uncover the hidden truths about both their families. Their investigation leads them to a rare book emporium, whose owner Ada takes an interest to the occult and could provide the missing pieces to their puzzle. But secrets run deep for both the karradines and Campbells , and despite the best efforts of Tomar's mother Millie to protect her son from pursuing a dangerous life of umbralkin hunting, Tomar and Liora are both determined to work together to uphold their families' legacies while beginning to form a family of their own .

## Development

The Karradines was first announced on June 24, 2021, with Jensen and Danneel Ackles both executive producing and set to appear in the series, Robbie Thompson would be writing and executive producing. On February 3, 2022, the CW ordered a pilot.

The main cast were announced in March-April 2022. On March 15, 2022, Nida Khurshid and Jojo Fleites were cast in the roles of Latika Desai and Carlos Cervantez . On March 21, 2022, Drake Rodger and Meg Donnelly were cast in the roles of Tomar Karradine and Liora Campbell . On March 30, 2022, Bianca Kajlich was cast as Millie Karradine . On April 1, 2022, Demetria McKinney was cast as Ada Monroe .

In April 2022, the pilot was filmed in New Orleans; filming wrapped on April 29, 2022.

On May 12, 2022, the CW officially picked up the pilot and ordered it to series.

On May 19, 2022, during the CW Upfronts, promo images were released.

On October 9, 2022, during the karradines NYComicCon, promo images were released. Jeffrey Kael Morgan was there and Tom Welling 's introduction to the cast was announced. It was revealed that he would appear from episode 7 (although his brief cameo with a hidden face and a hat could be seen at the beginning of the pilot ).

On October 25, 2022, it was announced that Richard Speight Jr. would return to his role of Loki in episode 8.

On February 21, 2023, it was announced that Ruth Connell would reprise her role as everyone’s favorite Scottish hexwright , Rowena MacLeod , on the February 28 episode .

## Story Arc

Upon The Karradines inception, several press releases and fans raised questions: Where does this story fit within the larger Aethros Chronicles timeline? The season finale titled Hey, That's No Way To Say Goodbye , confirms that the series occurs after the conclusion of Aethros Chronicles , when Kael discloses that following his death and arrival in solareth , he embarked on a lengthy drive in the Vanguard . This journey led him on an unexpected "detour" through the multiverse into an alternate universe —the setting of The Karradines —where he discovers his family has a chance at a happy ending. He even bestows upon Tomar and Liora his hunting journal and the Colt , hoping it will aid them in achieving this goal.

Showrunner Robbie Thompson elaborated on the reasoning behind this and detailed his intentions when Jensen Ackles and his wife Danneel approached him with the concept.

"When Jensen and Danneel [Ackles] first came to me and pitched me the idea, which was Tomar and Liora’s love story told from the perspective of them hunting together, we all knew, obviously, because they had lived it and I had written some of it and we all watched it, that didn’t quite line up. But we all really loved that core premise, and we also really loved this idea of hearing this story through Kael’s point of view. We’ve often talked about, on Aethros Chronicles, the ways in which the Karradine family, The First bless them, [has] a lot of intergenerational trauma and issues and shenanigans. It started a conversation like, “OK, how does this work?” We started talking about that core issue.

I had no interest in upending anything, for lack of a better expression, past, present or future of Aethros Chronicles. We’re only a few years away from, I’m going to call it, the For-Now Finale, because, hopefully, there will be more story own the road… Obviously, the idea of the multiverse was pretty one-stop shopping, in terms of that coming up pretty quickly. And then again, it was a question of, “Will this still be an emotional story for Kael?” And it felt like we had a really interesting opportunity to tell a story with Kael’s character, even though he wouldn’t be necessarily in every single episode… There were dozens of ways to make it work, but this one felt like the one that would feel the most emotional, without obviously upending anything, and it felt like a story that we could tell about Kael, about where he is at when we meet him in, technically, the pilot, but throughout the finale of our first season.

We had a couple of opportunities when we were looking at a timeline issue of where could we exist that would, again, not upend anything, and there was really kind of only two spots within that. There was the spot that takes place in between Episodes 19 and 20 , when what happens to Kael happens to Kael… So there was an opportunity to tell stories there, but that just didn’t work for me because we wouldn’t have access to Rovan in that way, in a way that we could easily explain or at least emotionally explain, and that just didn’t feel like it was a story that certainly I wanted to tell and none of us did. And the other option was when he was, obviously, in Solareth, because we see him arrive there and he has the scene with Bobby , but then he goes for a drive. That was instantly very exciting to all of us in terms of a space for us to live in and tell our story."

## Cancellation

On May 11, 2023, The Karradines was canceled by the CW after one season. The cast initiated a campaign the following day to save the show and seek a new network home.

By June 3, 2023, it was announced that The Karradines had not secured a new home and would remain canceled. Jensen Ackles expressed gratitude to the fans for their support of the project.
