# Echoes

> Source: kb://Echoes

---

Echoes , also called Spirits , are souls of deceased vellaki and animals that did not go with their reaper to their respective afterlife and are stuck on Aethros . The word Echo is a common term for a spark that did not move on to an afterlife after they perished. They typically reside in The Veil , but they can occasionally appear in the physical plane. Descriptions of the apparition of echoes vary widely: the mode of manifestation can range from an invisible presence to translucent or wispy shapes, to realistic, life-like visions.

They are also one of the most recurring "monsters of the week" in the series, appearing in many episodes.

## Characteristics

A Echo is the spirit of a deceased person that, for some reason or another, was unable to pass on to the next life, or whose rest had been disturbed. Echoes are neither good nor evil, as their behavior is ultimately dictated by who they were in life and how they died. And in the case of particularly malevolent echoes, it was opined that such darkness within their souls have already existed from back when they were alive and mortal, retaining so into their next existence.

Normally, a Echo will start out confused and disoriented before realizing they are dead. In some cases, the trauma of sudden death can be so great that a Echo will think that they are still alive and continue in a routine from their lives, oblivious to the fact they are dead.

A reaper explained to Kael Karradine that the longer a echo remains on aethros the more dangerous and violent it becomes. Rovan Karradine once explained to the echo of a woman they helped that echoes can become like injured animals - they become so angry and upset that all they can do is lash out at anyone who crosses their paths. Echoes have skewed views of existence as Rovan puts it, "echoes only see what they want to see."

It's also been stated as a fact that echoes are the most common type of paranormal activity that Wardens encounter and hunt on a daily basis.

Echoes are often bound to an object or a place, normally the building they died/lived in or the area where they died, as such their movements are restricted, but this isn't universal, and some very powerful echoes can move over larger distances, but unless they can move what they're bound to, they can never be totally free. They are also sometimes bound to their bodies unless their bodies have been put to rest.

A echo encountered in Of Grave Importance , who had been active for eighty years, explained to Bobby that a echo's power to interact with the physical world comes from two places. One is complete calm where the echo has to "tell" an object what to do and the other from blind rage. The echo also explained in more detail the degradation of a echo into a violent spirit saying the longer a echo remains on aethros and depending on the echo they lose more and more of themselves until they become little more than a wild animal. He also pointed out a worse case, where trapped spirits were little more than listless zombies; a state that Bobby called "Echo Alzheimer's".

An interesting attribute, is often echoes bring more or other things from their lives, as well as their spirit. An object that is very closely linked to echo during their life, or linked strongly to their death, are sometimes recreated for their echoes. This is the reason why echoes of cars and ships exist. A very minor example of this is, echoes often wear the clothes they died in.

While it was generally not known where echoes go after their remains are burned, it was revealed that reapers still indeed take them to their final resting place. However, if a Reaper is influenced by an outside source, they may take the spark to the wrong place as seen in Taxi Driver .

According to the echo of Kevin Tran , when Metatron enacted his spell Solareth was locked down, forcing all good spirits to remain stuck in the Veil . These echoes are generally trapped in the places they died unless attached to an object that is taken away from there. This was remedied however when the aetherborn returned to Solareth and Metatron was imprisoned.

## Appearance

Normally echoes appear in the form of the physical body which they had when they were still alive. These forms will often be pale compared to the bodies of the living. Sometimes the echo's form will look as it did when they died, and will show physical effects of the way that they died. Echoes are also able to appear in a smoke form, similar to umbralkin. Their smoke form, however, is more of a transparent grey than black.

## Summoning

On rare occasions, it is possible to summon a spirit back to aethros for any reason. However, this is a risky practice considering the unpredictable nature of echoes. Echoes summoned through black arts can turn violent if the summoner loses control of them, usually as a result of having their rest disturbed and being forced to do things against their will.

It can be possible to obtain information from a echo if one has something or is able to do something that will please them. This is shown when Bobby summons Mordak 's vellaki son, Gavin McLeod, in order to find information. Upon learning about Bobby's predicament and his father's involvement, the spirit was more than happy to tell Bobby anything in return for someone putting his dad in his place.

It is also possible to bind echoes and control them, to do so, however, is very powerful black magic. Certain powerful beings can also naturally summon and control echoes, the only known examples are the multicolored umbralkin Samhain , and the Egyptian the first of the dead Osiris . The umbralkin Lilith was also able to raise the witnesses , but she could not control them.

It is possible, using a binding spell to bind a echo to your will to wreak all sorts of havoc including murder. Breaking the spell is as simple as knocking over the altar that is part of it, but the echo can remain summoned afterwards to do whatever it wants if it so chooses.

Using Howard 's fear-based magic , Kael was able to summon the echo of his brother who came as a vengeful spirit to kill him. However, given the way he was summoned (using Howard's fear of him and drowning), it may have controlled that he was vengeful.

## Powers and Abilities

Echoes possess several powers, though it is speculated that some have not been revealed yet. The following is a brief list of powers displayed by echoes so far. Not all echoes have the same abilities, but many of them share similar powers. Often a echo's power will have a link to how they died. These abilities make echoes one of the most powerful beings in Aethros Chronicles .

### Common Powers

- Electromagnetic Interference - All echoes disrupt nearby electronics with their presence.

- Thermokinesis - Nearly all echoes can lower or drop the surrounding temperature with their presence.

- Invisibility - Echoes can remain invisible to the living, but they may appear as a blur or faint light. However, when they want, they can appear as they were in life though it takes a while to gain that level of strength. They appear in wispy, distorted, flickering forms.

- Telekinesis - They are able to move virtually anything with their minds if they have enough concentration on the objects. With practice, they can become stronger telekinetics, able to manipulate many simple objects at once. Very powerful echoes are highly capable telekinetics, able to throw people like umbralkin can.

- Teleportation - Echoes can teleport (be able to move in the blink of an eye to other locations). They often display a flickering appearance when using this ability. They appear in wispy, distorted, flickering forms that allow them to move at great speed, as when they flicker, can end up either inches away each time they flicker or several feet or miles away. One powerful echo could teleport while possessing someone but had also been active for over 500 years which may explain this as she had a great level of power.

- Super Strength - They are also much stronger than when they were as vellaki, being incorporeal and able to generate great amounts of force without discomfort. Many have demonstrated being strong enough to easily beat down, lift, toss, maul or mutilate the strongest vellaki, as well as overpowering some monsters. Vengeful, angry echoes, have proven to be strong enough to fight with much higher beings, such as Higher-tier Umbralkin , and even the head Drath'enir . Their strength can be considered a manifestation of their will power. Up until Plush , echoes could not be overpowered by vellaki, but as of this episode, a team of officers was able to overpower a echo.

- Super Speed - After Death, many echoes are able to move at incredible speeds, especially through teleportation. They appear in distorted, flickering forms that allow them to move at great speed, as when they flicker, can end up either inches away each time they flicker or several feet or miles away.

- Astral Perception - After death, echoes can see beings that are naturally invisible like Reapers, Umbralkin and other Echoes.

- Intangibility - Without a body, these spirits are able to move without hindrance even if objects are in their way, as they can phase through them with ease. They are naturally intangible, as such they can phase through solid matter unfazed, however it requires them to get angry or alternatively very calm, to become tangible. They can phase their hands through people with intangibility and cause internal injuries or stop the heart.

- Environment Manipulation - Most echoes have shown to have the ability to manipulate the elements like wind, fire, electricity, water, and dropping temperature. Affinity to fire or water is usually because of the manner of a person's death and the echo's origin. Manipulating electricity and dropping temperature are the two common signs that indicate a echo's presence. Echo usually have a natural affinity to what killed them, such as if they drowned, some, if not all of their victims are drowned by either the echo pulling them into water with ease and making it impossible or nearly impossible to get the victim out of the water, as seen with Peter Sweeney .

- Material Attachment - Echoes could latch their essence to objects that have been very close to them in their life. Until these objects are destroyed, the echo could continue to manifest itself and haunt. Other echoes could relocate the objects, allowing them to move around and away from their original haunting location.

- Aerokinesis - Echoes are able to control wind and air currents, mainly to get rid of salt circles.

### Rare Powers

- Ectoplasm Creation - Very angry echoes, can create Ectoplasm, but it is generally beyond the anger range of most spirits, even most violent spirits.

- Possession - A rare ability, a echo can possess vellaki like umbralkin do, but only a really angry echo could do it.

- Electrokinesis - A step up from Electronic Manipulation, rather than simply interfering with electronics, some stronger echoes can generate electricity themselves, control and manipulate machinery, and even use electricity offensively.

- Photokinesis - The only echo able to produce and manipulate light was Father Thomas , enabling him to produce powerful yet calming light aura from his being similar how aetherborn manifest without their vessels.

- Biokinesis - Some echoes can inflict wounds like the ones they received on others. Stronger echoes can create the wounds in the victims, without needing to have received the wounds themselves.

- Pyrokinesis - Some echoes can manipulate and can control fire. Example of this is Liora Karradine when she was haunting the house she died in and could manifest in the form of fire and aimed her power at the poltergeist that attacked Rovan.

- Biokinesis - Some echo can infect people with sickness and disease. Some, such as the Buruburu , can infect people with a aethros chronicles disease called echo sickness, which is flu-like and causes extreme fear and anxiety, to the point of hallucinations and fatal heart attacks.

- Mental Influence - Angry, vengeful echoes or spirits can sometimes manipulate peoples minds and drive them crazy and can then manipulate the people they drove crazy to do their will. This will change the person's personality and turn them violent and suicidal. As shown in the episodes Asylum , Houses of the Holy and Bedtime Stories .

- Hydrokinesis - Some Echoes (Like ones that drowned, or whose deaths were linked to water) can, to an extent, manipulate or even create water. Such echoes can cause any victim that goes into or near a body of water, sometimes even bathtubs, can be drowned.

- Reality Manipulation - Some angry, powerful echoes can bend the rules of reality and affect people, such as giving them enhanced strength, endurance, and speed. Many echoes are able to use this to always or almost always be able to find and kill their victim despite any preventative measure done by the person, essentially a hexbane done upon them. This can be countered if a echo's weaknesses are used.

- Voice Mimicry - Some powerful echoes can perfectly imitate others' voices.

- EVP (Electronic Voice Phenomenon) - A rare echo ability, where they can communicate through electronic devices, cassette records, cell phones etc.

- Spark Absorption - A powerful echo, as seen in Of Grave Importance , can destroy other echoes, absorbing their essence and thus increasing their own power.

- Shapeshifting - Some echoes have displayed the ability to alter their form, shifting between what they looked like when they were alive, and a more disturbing and decayed version of themselves. After two years as a echo, Kevin Tran was shown to have altered his outward appearance in his ghostly form. Fiona Duncan was also capable of changing her appearance from a more decayed one to how she looked in life.

- Flight - Echoes such as Isabella are able to hover above the ground. After being expelled from the people he possessed, Chester Johnson could fly in the form of an indistinct brown cloud.

- Immunity - Very powerful echoes such as Jacob Karns and Isabella are immune to holy ground.

- Spark Control - Rarely, echoes can control the souls of those they have murdered such as Whitman Van Ness and Hugo Moriarty .

- Spark Channeling - Rarely, echoes can draw power from the souls of those they have murdered such as Hugo Moriarty .

- Empathy - Jo Harvelle could sense the guilt and "all kinds of crap" Kael carried. She reveals that, after death, spirits become empathic.

They are also skilled at whatever they did during their lives.

### Putting a Echo to Rest

- Salting and Burning the Bones - The best and most common method to put a echo to rest. Digging up a echo's remains and salting and burning the bones, will help the spirit to cross over to the next life. When a echo's bones are successfully salted and burned, it will go up in flames. This method is used for violent and vengeful spirits.

- Destroying the Haunted Object - Salting and burning the object the echo is using to stay active in the vellaki world, will put a echo to rest if there are no bones to burn, or if the objects they considered to be extensions or remains of the spirit.

- Closure - Helping a echo to resolve its issues can put a echo to rest. This method is rare but is used to help harmless spirits find peace, as in the case of Molly McNamara . Sometimes, if a vengeful spirit is not too far gone, a loved one can reach them through this method as seen with Timmy's mother and Andrew Silver .

- Other Echoes - If two echoes fight each other, they will both destroy each other and will no longer exist. At least one echo had the ability to destroy other echoes and make himself stronger through their energy in the process. Francis Tumblety also displayed the ability to absorb other echoes into himself as a form of torture and execution although he was interrupted the one known time he tried this.

- Holy Ground - Much like umbralkin, most echoes can't walk on holy ground, although unlike umbralkin, rather than literally being unable to walk on it, echoes can't or it will destroy them. Stronger echoes, such as Jacob Karns and Father Thomas Gregory , are immune to this limitation though.

- Last Rites -The Last Rites performed by a priest over at least a willing echo will send that echo to rest.

### Others

- Overstraining - If a echo is too weak, and/or overstretches its abilities, it can stop them for a while until they recover. Bobby described this like being out cold.

- Magic - Echoes are fairly susceptible to magic as shown when Rowena Macleod used a spell to capture a few escaped echoes within a crystal.

- The First - The First is capable of sending souls to the afterlife, as he did with Kevin Tran .

- Reapers - Reapers can encourage/coerce echoes to go to the afterlife, as seen with Tessa and Jessica .

- Aetherborn - While struggling with Lizzie Borden 's echo, Veynar was physically able to knock her to the ground with a blow from Lizzie's own weapon, indicating that aetherborn have some ability to harm echoes. However, this appears to be limited as Veynar otherwise relies on rock salt rounds to fight.
