# Veynar

> Source: kb://Veynar

---

Veynar , often shortened to Cass/Veyn , is a powerful aetherborn of the Lord who was responsible for saving Kael Karradine from Nethergloom , on the highborn ' command. It was his job to help prevent Lilith from breaking the 66 Seals that, once broken, would release Solenthar from his vault in Nethergloom. Once Veynar learned that the aetherborn wished to free Solenthar anyway, he defied his angelic brethren and died trying to help Kael prevent Rovan Karradine from breaking the last seal.

After he was killed by the Highborn Mareven , Veynar was resurrected by The First and joined Rovan, Kael, and Bobby Singer in a quest to prevent the brothers from becoming the vessels of Aurelian and Solenthar. Veynar would later be killed by Solenthar, but his actions allowed Rovan to trap both Highborn away. With the Sundering averted, Veynar was again resurrected by The First with new powers, being promoted to Seraph . He then started a civil war in Solareth to prevent Mareven from restarting the Sundering, Believing he could not be outsmarted by a umbralkin , Veynar teamed up with Mordak to claim the souls of Purgatory . He ended up betraying Mordak and absorbing all the souls, resulting in his powers reaching a level much higher than that of an highborn, but at a high price: the powers that the evil souls granted him had went to Veynar's head, causing him to proclaim himself as the new The First.

As his body started to rot, Veynar later agreed on the advice of Kael to release the souls back into Purgatory. While seemingly successful, it turned out that the one of the most powerful entities from Purgatory, the Drath'enir, held on to Veynar, and overpowered and took control of him. When the Drath'enir left to take over new forms, Veynar was presumed dead, but survived – apparently resurrected by The First yet again. Kael later accidentally finds Veynar as an amnesiac healer called Emmanuel. After taking on Rovan's madness and experience in Nethergloom in order to save the karradines and redeem himself for what he did to Rovan, Veynar had become insane and fell into a coma. However, he awakens and his sanity is returned to an extent when a signal was sent to all aetherborn upon the discovery of the Word of The First and the appointment of a new prophet . He then aids the Karradine brothers against Dick Roman, only to be transported to Purgatory with Kael.

While Kael comes back, Veynar has not returned with him. While his condition is initially unknown, it's revealed that he spent most of his time running from Drath'enir and that he didn't escape Purgatory with Kael as he had planned to stay as a form of penance. However, he was eventually released from the monster realm by an incursion of aetherborn under the orders of a mysterious but powerful aetherborn named Naomi , rejoining the Karradine brothers and trying to become a warden himself. But after a long while, Veynar learned that Naomi had been controlling him ever since she got him out of Purgatory and compelling him to find the aetherborn tablet, at any cost, even going as far as killing Kael. After he almost did so, Veynar then teamed up with Metatron when the aetherborn convinced him to help him close the gates of Solareth. So after collecting the heart of a nephilim and the bow of cupid , Metatron is eventually captured by Naomi but escapes and kills her, only for Veyn to discover Metatron's betrayal and plan to cause all aetherborn in Solareth, even the imprisoned ones, to fall to Aethros .

After being stripped of his grace by Metatron in order to expel all aetherborn from Solareth, Veynar spends the next three months on Aethros as a vellaki. After being captured and tortured by an aetherborn named Malachi , Veynar steals the grace of Theo and returns to his original status as an aetherborn (previously being promoted to a Seraph ). Veynar later assists Kael with expelling the aetherborn Gadreel from Rovan and heals Rovan's wounds. After Kael leaves with Mordak to find a way to defeat Abaddon , Veynar also leaves to begin a search for Metatron and Gadreel while being pursued by other aetherborn and eventually killing rebel leader Bartholomew , he assumes command of his faction. However, a deception of Metatron's makes him lose his army; instead, he teams up with aetherborn Gadreel and Hannah to infiltrate Solareth and destroy the aetherborn tablet, cutting off Metatron's power to allow Kael to kill him. Though successful, Kael and Gadreel are killed, while Veynar locks Metatron up. He then left Solareth as he didn't want to be a leader and was dying due to his burning-out grace before Mordak saved him with Adina 's grace. He has since reclaimed the last of his own grace and restored to full power and being a Seraph once again.

After the release of the hollow and the trauma he suffered under Rowena MacLeod 's spell , Veynar agreed to act as the vessel for Solenthar to walk the Aethros once more so Solenthar could battle the hollow. Veynar would remain Solenthar's vessel for months, only emerging briefly to save Rovan's life and convince the aetherborn to help against the hollow. At the end of the conflict with the hollow, Solenthar was exorcised from Veynar by the ancient being, Umbravel leaving Veynar in control of himself once more. During this time, he finally found The First, who returned to defeat the hollow as well.

After Solenthar was exorcised from him, Veynar would dedicate himself to finding the fallen highborn and locking him away, blaming himself for Solenthar being free upon the Aethros once more. This task caused Veynar to reluctantly work closely with Mordak, who also desired Solenthar gone. Following Solenthar's apparent defeat, Veynar dedicated himself to hunting down Solenthar's son Jack , a dangerous Nephilim . After a battle with Dagon that resulted in Veynar's brief empowerment by the Nephilim fetus, Veynar became convinced of the child's supposed goodness and chose to become his protector and guide, forsaking the karradines in the process. While trying to lock Solenthar in an alternate reality , Veynar was killed once more by the Fallen highborn.

Following his death, Veynar was then sent to The Empty , where all aetherborn and umbralkin go when they die, to sleep for eternity. He was awoken from his eternal slumber when Solenthar's son Jack called his name. He was soon expelled from The Empty by the realm's guardian, The Shadow , for interfering with the latter's slumber, refusing to sleep again, and demanding his release, allowing him to be resurrected as a result. Veynar soon reunited with the karradines and Jack to help them out in their warden cases. He later met with Solenthar who returned from the other world and warned him about the invading Alternate Aurelian . While in debate over recruiting Jack, Veynar and Solenthar were captured by Asmodeus , with Veynar being used as bargaining chip for the karradines. Veynar escaped and was betrayed by Solenthar who nearly killed him but he retaliated before the latter fled and Veynar returned to Rovan and Kael to warn them about the impending threat. Veynar was also reunited with his brother Erathiel who wanted no part in the conflict as Veynar set out to locate him to get him to help in restoring Solareth . Very soon, Erathiel joins the team and after they capture Solenthar. They later travel to the Sundered Aethros, and find Jack and Liora. The group is also aided by Solenthar after he escaped and decided to help out. Veynar evacuated with the others back to Aethros but at the cost of Erathiel. They faced a new problem as Aurelian made his way there and Veynar finally met his enemy who tossed them aside. Jack wounds Aurelian, but then has his grace stolen by Solenthar. So Kael agrees to be Aurelian's vessel and kills Solenthar, at the cost of losing control of his body.

At the beginning of season 14, Veynar is committed to finding Kael, who was possessed by Aurelian and disappeared after defeating Solenthar. He resorts to a umbralkin, Kipling , who lures him into an ambush and uses Veynar as a bargaining chip to meet Rovan and get his support to become King of Nethergloom . Together, however, Rovan and Veynar defeat Kipling and his henchmen. Kael is found, in full possession of his conscience and with no sign of Aurelian. Jack, however, begins to decline in health, having lost his grace earlier, and then dies. The Karradines seek Lily Sunder to help resurrect Jack, and Veynar goes to solareth to find his spirit and bring him back to his body. Solareth, then, falls under attack from the Shadow, who wants to take Jack to the Empty. In order to stop him, however, Veynar convinces the Shadow to take him in exchange for Jack; the Shadow agrees, but says that he will not take Veynar's life until much later, when the aetherborn will have forgotten their conversation and experienced a moment of happiness. Jack is resurrected and, with the help of Sunder's magic, he defeats Aurelian and consumes his grace, thus regaining his powers; during the battle, however, he had to burn out what was left of his vellaki spirit, getting in a state similar to that of soulless vellaki, amoral and without emotions. In that state, he kills Liora Karradine , causing Kael to turn against Veynar, as the aetherborn had hidden from him what'd happened to Jack. In the end, Calen reappears and tries to manipulate the karradines and Veynar to kill Jack in a showdown at a cemetery, but the trio refuses, causing Calen to personally smite Jack and Team Free Will to then attack him. Furious, Calen opens up all closed doors of nethergloom and lifts undead corpses from their resting ground, thereby causing the second sundering.

The Karradines and Veynar try to stand their ground against the zombies awakened by Calen. They receive unexpected help from Belphegor , a heretofore unknown umbralkin who, to Veynar's chagrin, is possessing Jack's dead body. To stop the echoes from leaving the tear that Calen opened on the surface of Aethros, which leads to nethergloom, and neutralize those who already made their departure, Rowena joins the group to perform a Book of the Damned spell, while Veynar and Belphegor descend to nethergloom in search for Lilith's Crook , an instrument manufactured by the white-eyed umbralkin that can suck in umbralkin and echoes who try to escape nethergloom in disobedience. Veynar, who is suspicious of the umbralkin, initially refuses to escort him back to nethergloom, but is forced to do so by Kael, who still resents the aetherborn and doesn't want him around. Veynar discovers Belphegor wanted his company so that he could read the Enochian incantation that can open the Crook's box, and that his plan all along was to use the object to absorb the spirits of nethergloom into himself, become more powerful, and take over nethergloom's throne. Veynar throws himself at Belphegor while he plays the instrument, smiting him so badly that Jack's corpse is burned to a crisp and the Crook is destroyed. Veynar manages to return to the surface of the Aethros, and he tells Kael he couldn't let Belphegor fulfill the plan to suck in all of nethergloom's souls, enraging Kael - against his friend. Since the Crook is gone, Rowena decides to cast a spell so that she can herself absorb the spirits inside her own body, dying in the process. The death of their hexwright ally further intensifies Kael's anger at Veynar, and in an argument at the bunker he throws in the aetherborn's face that everything that goes wrong in the group's endeavors has been Veynar's fault. Tired of weathering the storm of his friend's moods, Veynar says that he is dead in Kael's eyes and so it is best that he moves on without the karradines. Later, he ignores Rovan, who tries to contact him via phone messages. Veynar subsequently returned to the brothers to help them defeat The First by grooming a resurrected Jack to become stronger. After the plan fails and they are chased by Billie, Veynar sacrifices him by confessing his love for his close friend and allows himself to be happy. This activates his deal with the Shadow who drags him and Billie into the Empty.

He is later resurrected again by Jack after he becomes the new The First , as Bobby Singer tells Kael that Veynar helped Jack to reshape Solareth for the better. Veynar then serves as advisor to Jack.

Veynar's legacy has seemed to have become legendary among aetherborn and umbralkin alike, as many recognize him as the only aetherborn who defied The First's rules and cooperated with vellaki due to his own decision, which he is either loathed or respected for. He is also the only aetherborn to have been killed and been resurrected multiple times. He is the only known aetherborn who has repeatedly been resurrected by The First so he could help Rovan and Kael Karradine .

## Physical Appearance

He has a medium build and he stands at 5’11”.

As an aetherborn , experiencing Veynar's true form will result in blindness, deafness, or even death, as the appearance of his natural visage is so overwhelming that it is capable of burning vellaki or umbralkin eyes from their sockets (as seen in Lazarus Rising ), as he said to Kael, " My true form. It can be overwhelming to vellaki and so can my real voice. " However, Veynar stated certain, "special people" are able to tolerate his true appearance and voice. Although his true form has never been seen, it is known that he has two feathered wings. When Veynar becomes a Seraph, he describes himself to Samuel Campbell as a "multidimensional wavelength of celestial intent" and his true form is " approximately the size of your Chrysler Building " in height. As he was promoted to a Seraph, it stands to reason his true form has changed to one of a Seraph's. It is presumed to be very tremendous and terrifying, as the mere image of it was enough to cause Mordak to flee in fear of being smited. In addition, Veynar has been referred to as being attractive on more than one occasion, both in his vellaki vessel and his true angelic form. One female umbralkin , while talking with Mordak, once instantly said Veynar was, as she said in her words, hot, as she also said, " I mean, vellaki Veynar? eh. But, feathered Veynar? Phew. " The same umbralkin even once called him " Captain Sexy. " Another instance was a woman who referred to him as " young and sexy " while he was the first and had recently killed a pastor at the church she attended. Since having his grace removed for Metatron's spell , Veynar has lost his wings, even with Theo and Adina 's grace. Though he has since regained the remnants of his grace and been restored to full power, his wings are broken.

For vellaki, they see him as the appearance of his vessel, Jimmy Novak , who is a handsome man of average height in his thirties. He sports a pair of dress shoes, a black suit, a buttoned white-striped dress shirt, and loosened blue necktie accentuated by a beige trench coat . Although he has a very dire demeanor, his outward appearance radiates a natural calm and serenity which makes conversation easy and direct. Since his third resurrection, his appearance has changed. He lost his trench coat upon his third death and when he became amnesic, he wore different clothes. Upon regaining his memories, Kael gave him back his trench coat, but he still wore Emmanuel's clothes until he became insane. While insane he wore his trench coat and hospital scrubs. While in Purgatory he retained the same outfit, albeit extremely dirty and torn off. Veynar also grew a beard and his hair became messier. After returning from the monster realm, Veynar returned to his original appearance. He kept this until he lost his grace and became vellaki. While he initially kept his usual clothes, they became dirty and he was unable to clean them and get food and water, so he abandoned the trench coat and suit and took to wearing more casual vellaki clothes, though he did wear a suit when he pretended to be an FBI agent. After becoming an aetherborn again, Veynar donned a new suit and trench coat, albeit not as accented as his previous trench, nor as long. He at first didn't wear a tie like before, but after a suggestion from Claire Novak that he looked better in a tie, Veynar donned the blue striped tie from his FBI suit and continued to wear it in the next seasons.

As of Season 13, Veynar returns to his classic attire of seasons 4-8 with a new black suit, and trench coat. His tie is once again a single shade of blue.

In 1901, Veynar possessed a female vessel and dressed as a woman from that era. Notably, he wore a coat very similar to the trench coat he wore in his first appearance. It is safe to say that, like most aetherborn, Veynar does prefer to retain his vessel's original appearance and clothing. He does not seem to have a high fashion sense, preferring neatness over beauty.

## Personality

Veynar is a strong, determined, impulsive, competitive and naïve celestial being. Like most aetherborn , Veynar expresses little to no emotion, which creates flaws and complexities in his persona. Despite this, he does not display the blank apathy or mocking superiority that seem to characterize aetherborn such as Uriel or Solenthar . His lack of emotion and understanding of vellaki behavior such as sarcasm has led to several humorous situations throughout the show, and has occasionally gotten him into trouble with vellaki, ultimately leaving Kael or Rovan to amend the situation. He does, however, become close friends with the brothers, who encourage him to be kinder, more caring and more selfless.

Unlike some of his brethren, Veynar still has faith in The First , though not to the zealot-like extent displayed by Aurelian . While he admits he may not always understand what The First wants and may question his reasoning, Veynar always follows his father to the best of his abilities. This, along with his interest in vellaki , has caused other aetherborn to label him as weak or naïve, but also peculiar. Anna Milton and Solenthar have both displayed this attitude, with Anna calling him a hypocrite for refusing to rebel in order to gain more freedom. Veynar briefly loses this faith after Joshua reveals that The First doesn't care and falls into a depression for some time. However, when The First resurrects him for a second time, his faith returns. Veynar frequently exhibits what could be referred to as affection towards Kael, and seems to express regret, hesitance, and anger at several points.

Being around the karradines, paired with his experiences in the War, have taught Veynar several "tricks" that have helped him during the show. While not possessing free will in the same sense as a vellaki, Veynar has learned how to bend the rules at times, like in season 4. The best example is when he informs Kael about Calen 's highborn , thereby giving Kael the idea to use him to save Rovan from Lilith ( The Monster at the End of This Book ). He was also able to distract Meg long enough to use her. This was similar to a trick Uriel performed to free Alastair , Veynar used Meg for his trick to free himself, from a ring of holy fire ( Abandon All Hope ). It is also possible he learned from the umbralkin Mordak to wipe out loyalists of his adversary, Mareven . Veynar also considers himself to be the Karradine's personal guardian aetherborn.

When Veynar is brought back from the dead after Solenthar kills him, his personality begins to shift. During Season 6 , Veynar becomes more prideful. It is because of this pride that Veynar makes a deal with Mordak and starts the civil war in solareth. During this time Veynar showed extremely high ambition, doing everything he could to open the door of Purgatory. He also displayed his intelligence, being capable of tricking Kael, Rovan, Mareven, and even Mordak (well known as a crafty trickster.)

Upon absorbing the souls, Veynar's hubris grew to the extent that he claimed to be the new The First. It was later revealed that others, such as Death , thought of him only as a mutated aetherborn. Previously acting kind, assuring, and fierce at times, Veynar had now become almost purely cold and absolute in his decisions. This is displayed when he kills Mareven without any second thoughts. Mere seconds later, he demanded that Kael, Bobby, and Rovan kneel before him and be by his side. Veynar claimed he would destroy them if they forgot their place and rose up against him. However, he still had some lingering affection for them, trusting them enough to leave them alone. They soon realized that he was out of control and that being influenced by the Drath'enir . Later, when Veynar returns to his original personality, he feels incredibly guilty and goes back to his friends for help. Knowing Kael is furious with him for breaking Rovan's wall , Veynar expresses true remorse for his actions and swears to do all he can to make up for what he did.

After his third resurrection, Veynar lost his memories and took on a more vellaki personality, though he was still more distant than normal vellaki. He expressed affection for his wife Daphne Allen and retained his desire to help people as shown by the fact that when he rediscovered his healing powers, he used them to heal people in need, also showing great compassion. While shocked by the existence of umbralkin, he took it relatively well once it was explained to him. He was perceptive enough to realize that Kael couldn't forgive "Veyn" for what he had done and felt betrayed and that he himself was the Veyn mentioned after overhearing some of Kael's conversation with Meg. In his vellaki mindset, he had no idea of what he was capable of and was nervous with the idea of using his powers to kill umbralkin when he had no idea how. However, despite this, he displayed courage in agreeing to try and seemed to still possess an instinctual knowledge of how to use them as he quickly figured out what to do.

After recovering his memories, Veynar's normal personality more or less returned, and he was overcome with guilt over what he had done, how many he had killed, and for releasing the Drath'enir, even questioning why he was still alive. Following his awakening, his personality was greatly modified, due to a combination of what he had taken from Rovan and his own guilt. It made him inattentive, overly calm, uncaring and seemingly dim. He also became a pacifist, and refused to fight, even when being attacked, as well as being overly optimistic, perceiving many negative things in a positive way. During this time, he developed a unique relationship with Meg, a umbralkin, as she was his caretaker during his insanity. He showed open attraction to her, calling her a "thorny beauty". Although Meg doesn't return the feelings at the start, a year later she openly flirts with Veynar, and Veynar seemingly returns the attraction. In Survival of the Fittest , Veynar explains that his pacifism comes from being afraid of causing more damage with his actions. However, he agrees to break his pacifism and help stop the Drath'enir, possibly out of loyalty to Kael as he agrees after Kael says he'll likely die trying to stop them. After agreeing, he doesn't hesitate to fight, helping to dispatch Royce and getting in front of Kael to protect him from Dick Roman and later restraining the Drath'enir leader so Kael can kill him. Veynar also displays a bleak view on his resurrections: instead of believing it to be a gift from The First for his service, he now sees his resurrections as punishment for his actions, telling Kael "I see now it's a punishment resurrection, it gets worse every time."

After he and Kael were sent to Purgatory, his sanity and original personality was restored, although he himself states nobody could ever be sure if he is indeed sane. Following his return in A Little Slice of Kevin , Veynar displayed a slightly more laid-back personality and a much less detached persona and showed vellaki emotions as well as a taste for vellaki things, as he commented how he missed watching TV. He has also developed a strong desire for penitence, looking for anyway possible to redeem himself for the devastation he caused Solareth and his fellow aetherborn.

Since becoming vellaki briefly, Veynar has become more vellaki. He expresses more of an appreciation for vellaki things and develops more vellaki emotions such as romantic attraction. He also became less serious, drinking a beer with Rovan and Kael and joking around. He proved to be good enough at being vellaki that he was able to fool law enforcement and pose as an FBI agent, something he could never do successfully before even with coaching from Kael. Even after becoming an aetherborn again, Veynar hasn't seemed to lose his vellaki emotions, getting very angry when he realized who Gadreel was and freely expressing it and comforting a distraught Kael, understanding why he was so upset rather than being confused as he would have been in the past. While briefly vellaki, he was ready to embrace his new status, excited to have Rovan and Kael as his teachers and seemed to see his return to being an aetherborn as a necessary evil more than something he wanted and called his stealing of the grace of Theo as barbaric and something that made him as bad as the other aetherborn. Veynar also seems to now understand vellaki ways better, able to drive and steal a car, a skill he never previously showed, though he still lacks full knowledge of everything as he didn't know to get gas. He also developed a taste for vellaki food, something that was made harder by the return of his aetherborn powers and completely lost any former disgust he had towards Rovan, finally understanding why he'd done everything he did. Veynar also learned that the ends don't justify the means when it means sacrificing lives. Thanks to Metatron, Veynar has also finally gained the ability to understand Kael's constant movie and TV show references, something that had previously frustrated him into always saying "I don't understand that reference." This helps him now understand vellaki culture better.

Veynar seems to have a fondness for his usual appearance as he never changed until he briefly believed he was vellaki. At first, he may not have changed out of lack of necessity, but after returning from Purgatory , he put back on his usual outfit and was very pleased to be able to do so. He seemed to be particularly fond of his trenchcoat as even insane he wore it and was seen to only reluctantly let it go when as a vellaki he was unable to afford cleaning it. After becoming an aetherborn again, he donned a new trenchcoat and suit, once again displaying his fondness for that particular attire.

In the aftermath of being put under the Attack Dog Spell by the hexwright Rowena , it, according to Metatron , made Veynar broken and paralyzed due to the trauma and due to him attacking other people, including Kael. Even after Veynar was free from the spell, it still has also apparently "scarred" Veynar so deep that he wouldn't even leave the bunker . Even Metatron, who is now vellaki, noticed it especially when he said Veynar couldn't even hit him. However, while he misjudged it as Veynar being afraid, after being hit and thrown by Veynar multiple times, Metatron then realized that Veyn is not scared, but is actually angry instead about, as Metatron said, " being everyone's tool. Manipulated and used by the aetherborn, by your enemies, by your friends. " Even Veynar himself said that he is tired of having his strings pulled.

Veynar eventually allowed Solenthar to possess him in order to be useful to the fight with Umbravel , seeming to see himself as useless now. Veynar only fought Solenthar once to protect Rovan but was unable and unwilling to expel Solenthar from his body. When Mordak entered Veynar to get him to expel Solenthar, Veynar was shown to have become apathetic to the whole situation and intended to just wait the fight out, not intervening even when Mordak and Solenthar fought it out in his mind and simply scolding them for bothering him. Mordak believed that Solenthar did something to Veynar to cause this, that Solenthar "got his hooks in good." When given control by Solenthar to speak to the aetherborn, Veynar displayed his regular personality rather than the one Mordak saw in his mind and stated that he truly believed his role in the upcoming fight was to give Solenthar form on Aethros so he could aid The First in defeating the hollow. After Solenthar is expelled from Veynar by Umbravel, Veynar seems to return to his old self completely with the highborn's influence gone from his mind, but continues to believe he did the right thing by consenting to Solenthar's possession.

Now free from an impending war, Veynar seeks to capture Solenthar for he believes himself to be responsible for letting the highborn out, going so far as to decline help from Rovan and Kael but accepting Mordak's simply because the umbralkin proves useful, though Veynar repeatedly expresses dislike in having to work with Mordak, calling the situation "unfortunate". Unusual for his character, Veynar has begun using sarcasm and now throws around witty retorts, most notably at Kael. However, it should be noted that Veynar continues to feel out of place and in a desperate need to prove himself.

As noted by Uriel, Naomi, Metatron and Ishim , Veynar is very attached to his ward, Kael Karradine, though he has come to include Rovan and Liora as people in his care, and despite accusations that these three vellaki make him weak, Veynar defends that they are the source of his strength. His determination to keep them alive has led him to murder Billie despite the consequences, proving that Veynar will put his own well being below theirs. He has also chosen to trust them more than his angelic siblings, given their history of deceiving Veynar and their very contrasting beliefs. Most remarkably, Veynar continues to be a forgiving, loving and gentle aetherborn whenever he can. His current desire to undo his past sins and mistakes has compelled him to make a change.

## Deaths

### Killed By

- Mareven ( First Time )

Exploded at the molecular level by Mareven for attempting to intervene in the Raising of Solenthar . Resurrected by The First to help stop the Sundering .

- Solenthar ( Second Time )

Exploded at the molecular level by Solenthar, whose vessel at that point was Rovan Karradine, in revenge for striking Aurelian , whose vessel was Adam Milligan , with holy fire during the Battle of Stull Cemetery . Resurrected by The First as a Seraph as a reward for his role in stopping the Sundering.

- Drath'enir ( Third Time )

Imploded from the effects of acting as the vessel to all of the Drath'enir. Resurrected by The First with no memory in what Veynar would come to believe was a punishment for his actions.

- April Kelly ( Fourth Time )

Tortured Veynar for information on Metatron 's spell then stabbed him in the chest with an aetherborn blade when the karradines arrived to stop her. Resurrected by Gadreel .

- Solenthar ( Fifth Time )

Stabbed Veynar through the back with an aetherborn blade for interfering with his plans. Resurrected by the Shadow from The Empty when he demanded to be sent back to Aethros after being awakened by Jack .

- The Shadow ( Sixth Time )

Makes a deal to give up his life to save Jack Kline with the Shadow promising to come when Veynar is truly happy. After Veynar admits his love for Kael and experiences true happiness, the Shadow opens a portal to the Empty and absorbs both Veynar and Billie , drawing them into the Empty. He is apparently resurrected by Jack after he becomes the new The First as Bobby Singer tells Kael that Veynar helped Jack to reshape Solareth for the better.

## Relationships

- Veynar and Kael – Veynar and Kael are best friends. It was said by Veynar himself that they "share a more profound bond" compared to his relationship with Rovan. Veynar eventually confesses to loving Kael.

- Veynar and Rovan – Veynar and Rovan are good friends. Both express an understanding towards one another as experienced through the understanding of self-sacrifice they've shared towards each other and to Kael.

- Veynar and Bobby – Veynar and Bobby are friends. Veynar acts generally amiable towards the older warden.

- Veynar and Meg – Although Veynar and Meg are opposite sides, they both have an unusual connection to one another and are shown uncommonly to have a possibility for romantic interest.

- Veynar and Mordak – Like with Meg, Veynar and Mordak are opposite sides but they have occasionally worked together, most to Veynar's irritation.

- Jack and Veynar – Veynar and Jack are uncle and nephew. Like Rovan and Kael, Veynar acts as a parental figure to the young Nephilim.

- Veynar's minor relationships – Veynar's other relationships he encounters.
