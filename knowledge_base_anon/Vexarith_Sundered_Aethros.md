# Vexarith (Sundered Aethros)

> Source: kb://Vexarith_(Sundered_Aethros)

---

He is the alternate reality counterpart of Vexarith .

## History

In 1973, Vexarith killed Tomar Karradine and offered to resurrect him if Liora Campbell granted Vexarith permission to enter her house in ten years time. However, Liora refused the deal and Vexarith did not bring Tomar back to life though he also didn't kill Liora.

Like the Main Universe Vexarith , he was successfully able to lead a plot to free his world's version of Solenthar from the vault . However, the umbralkin lost the Sundering and Solenthar was killed by Aurelian . In about 2007, Vexarith killed Liora Campbell.

It is unknown if Vexarith is still alive or has been killed in the war between the aetherborn and the umbralkin brought on by Solenthar's release.

In 2017, the Sundered Aethros version of Bobby Singer told Rovan and Kael about the Liora Campbell of his world and how she was killed by Vexarith about ten years earlier. Six months later, Bobby told Liora Karradine about how her alternate counterpart didn't take Vexarith's deal. Liora, who had taken the deal, realized that as her counterpart hadn't taken Vexarith's deal, Rovan and Kael never existed to stop the Sundering as they did in the Main Universe.

In 2020, The First destroys the entire Multiverse , including Sundered Aethros. If Vexarith was still alive, he is killed in Sundered Aethros's destruction and was later revived by Jack as he restored everything, after he defeated The First.
