# Nethergloom

> Source: kb://Nethergloom

---

Nethergloom , also called Hades , The Fiery Pit , The Netherworld , is the spiritual realm for punishment and agony of the afterlife, as well as a prison for some inhuman creatures. Vellaki souls who are sent to Nethergloom are tormented until their humanity is stripped away and they become umbralkin . Nethergloom is said to be so terrible that even the umbralkin, with the exception of Alastair and Belphegor , hate and fear it.

## Appearance and characteristics

Nethergloom is described to be a place so horrible that even its own inhabitants fear and despise it. Only two umbralkin, Alastair and Belphegor, so far has been described to enjoy Nethergloom. Ruby described Nethergloom as not being able to remember who you used to be. While possessing Rovan , Meg described it as a prison of "flesh, and bone, and blood, and fear," and that one of the reasons umbralkin keep on returning to Aethros is because they themselves do not want to suffer the tortures of Nethergloom.

During his time in Nethergloom, Kael is seen connected to "racks," long dark chains that look very similar to meat hooks, which are provided as extra torture, while umbralkin such as Alastair use "razors" to torment souls further. It could be assumed that Nethergloom, much like Solareth , is also a varying landscape. When the umbralkin Mordak became King, he drastically altered Nethergloom changing it into a never-ending queue. In recent times, Mordak seems to have returned Nethergloom to its former more terrifying and savage state. However, there appear to be multiple levels of Nethergloom, each one bearing a different landscape. Time in Nethergloom is about 120 times faster than time on Aethros . Despite the time difference, there is cellular reception in Nethergloom's domain, allowing inhabitants to call or text inhabitants on Aethros or other dimensions, even from the furthest reaches of Nethergloom. Nethergloom, like Solareth, can allow vellaki to see highborn in ways that doesn't burn out their eyes.

Similar to Solareth, the fabric and reality of Nethergloom are very different than Aethros. Alastair describes Aethros's reality as "too concrete" compared to Nethergloom's, mentioning that Nethergloom reality allows for much more forms of torture due to its more magical structure. The very environment of Nethergloom has been described as being very hostile and savage, often being described as a fiery scorching place, with Ruby saying Nethergloom being hot enough to sizzle flesh off bones. However, the rings of the Horsemen remained intact after exposure to Nethergloom, although the aftereffect cause them to sizzle temporarily.

While the shape and size of Nethergloom have never been clearly stated, it has been mentioned that Nethergloom does have depth, as Vexarith described Lilith being trapped "neck-deep in the pit". Nethergloom also has inaccessible sections as well as hard-to-reach places, such as where the Shedim reside, which are locked away in an unreachable location. Solenthar's Vault is somewhere in a place very hard to reach, as most umbralkin could not find it for over a millennia. Lilith's Chamber was also sealed by Lilith, making it inaccessible to umbralkin. However, all of the doors of Nethergloom were flung open by The First , making previously inaccessible areas now accessible. This includes Solenthar's Vault and Lilith's Chamber.

## Access and location

Nethergloom is a realm often pictured to be underneath the Aethros . Whether this is true has not yet been proven though the Rupture to Nethergloom takes the form of a massive tear in the Aethros leading into Nethergloom directly below. Rather, it does not seem to be fixed and could be accessed from different locations. One well known passageway between Aethros and Nethergloom is the Devil's Gate located in southern Wyoming. It was sealed by Samuel Colt with the use of a heavy door with a devil's trap lock, unlocked only by the Colt , and by placing a massive devil's trap consisting of five frontier churches connected to each other by pure iron railroads. This portal was capable of releasing umbralkin who are normally unable to escape nethergloom unaided, such as Lilith , or the souls of vellaki who were being tortured in Nethergloom, such as Tomar Karradine .

Solenthar's Vault is a special part of Nethergloom designed solely for the purpose to keep the fallen highborn Solenthar incapacitated and locked away where he could do no harm. The Vault is located in the furthest reaches of Nethergloom, possibly Limbo. It is found beneath St. Liora's Convent in Ilchester, Maryland. Another known way of entering the vault is through the rings of the Four Horsemen of the Sundering .

Aetherborn are capable of forcing their way into Nethergloom, as evidenced when they laid siege to the Gates of Nethergloom to save Kael. Fairies also claim that they know backdoors to Nethergloom that not even aetherborn know, although this has not been proved. No matter where their location is on Aethros, umbralkin will always be sent back to Nethergloom once exorcised.

Rogue reapers are able to secretly access Nethergloom through a portal located in Purgatory that acts as a "backdoor." Any being can use this door both ways as long as they are aware of its location on both ends and have access to Purgatory.

In 2019, The First opened the Rupture to Nethergloom , a tear in the Aethros itself leading into Nethergloom. Combined with The First throwing open every door in Nethergloom, this allowed all of the souls and umbralkin trapped in Nethergloom to escape. The rupture acted as a two-way door, allowing travel into Nethergloom while it was open. By jumping into the rupture, Veynar , Belphegor and Rowena MacLeod were able to reach Nethergloom. Veynar was later able to climb back out through the rupture which on the Nethergloom side appeared as the celling. The rupture was closed by Rowena, Rovan and Kael using the Sanetur Acre Vulnus spell, sealing Nethergloom off again and trapping the souls and umbralkin back inside.

Rowena's journals contain a spell for magical transport to Nethergloom.

In the furthest reaches of Nethergloom, there is a place called Limbo . Mordak described it as being where he sent unruly souls for timeouts. The realm is completely engulfed with countless thunderstorms in the middle of darkness and void, adorned by countless scattered skeletons that led to an empty vault. In this place, Rovan, Mordak, and Rowena performed a ritual to safely summon Solenthar.

Lilith's Chamber is an area in Nethergloom in which she kept many powerful objects including her Crook . This room is located down a corridor which is adorned by six statues of hooded skeletal figures in demonic prayer.

## History

### Creation of the first Umbralkin and the Princes of Nethergloom

It has never been clearly stated who made Nethergloom and when exactly it came to be, but most likely it was The First who created it, as evidence by the amount of control and knowledge he has over it. In defiance of The First , as the Mark corrupted him further, the highborn Solenthar snuck into the Garden of Eden and corrupted humanity. Solenthar then asked the first and most powerful highborn Aurelian , his older brother and Gods oldest and most powerful creation and his personal angelic warrior, to stand by him in conflict and the war, however Aurelian refused and beat him down, on The First's order, starting a war in the process. To spite The First, Solenthar then corrupted and twisted a vellaki spark into the very first umbralkin called Lilith . Solenthar then began to create Nethergloom's hierarchy, as he made the first generation of umbralkin after Lilith, called the Princes of Nethergloom which he created himself. They were named Vexarith , Ramiel , Dagon and Asmodeus .

At some point later, Asmodeus sought to please his creator by releasing the Shedim , "things so dark and base The First himself would not allow them into the light." Asmodeus believed that he could train and use the Shedim despite Solenthar supposedly fearing them. Solenthar instead punished him by inflicting two scars upon Asmodeus' face. Solenthar then single-handedly re-imprisoned the Shedim.

### Creation of The Knights of Nethergloom

One of the first sons of man, Abel , was attempting to pray to The First. In actuality he was praying to Solenthar, making his spark hellbound. His older brother Cain refused to let this happen and made a pact with Solenthar: to have his spark in Nethergloom for Abel's to be delivered to Solareth. Solenthar agreed on the condition that Cain would murder his brother and bear The Mark , a hexbane which Satan had and desired to pass onward. Cain killed Abel who was then sent to Solareth. To avoid the corruption of the mark Cain killed himself only to be resurrected as a umbralkin and the first Knight of Nethergloom . Solenthar handpicked the rest of the Knights of Nethergloom and had Cain train and lead them as an elite fighting force of umbralkin or elite warrior umbralkin.

### Imprisonment of The Devil

The First, realizing the immense threat that Solenthar posed to creation and humanity, made an extremely powerful impenetrable vault in the depths of Nethergloom that contained over 600 seals on it and powerfully warded with incomprehensibly powerful Enochian sigils rendering Solenthar utterly powerless and completely trapped. Solenthar was then defeated and thrown into Solenthar's Vault by Aurelian himself. He was not seen by any umbralkin for thousands of years, and those who knew of existence gave up looking for him; most others thought of him only as a myth. The only umbralkin who did not give up searching was Vexarith and his followers.

### Vexarith's plan

As Lilith was said to be "neck deep in the pit", the next in line for the throne was the Prince of Nethergloom Vexarith . After years of searching, Vexarith finally located the doorway to Solenthar's prison in 1972 and contacted the fallen highborn by slaughtering a mass of nuns in a Church . Solenthar instructed Vexarith with two tasks, important in his plan to escape his Vault: freeing Lilith from Nethergloom, and raising a very special child strong enough to kill Lilith and break the final seal, releasing the Devil himself.

### Breaking the Seals

Kael Karradine spent four months in Aethros time in Nethergloom, but he stated that time passed differently there, and for him it was more like 40 years. This made three days on Aethros roughly equivalent to a year in Nethergloom. During this time, Kael was tortured mercilessly, being ripped apart and then put back together at the end of each day so that he could be torn apart again. At the end of every day, Alastair would offer Kael freedom from torture if he would start torturing souls himself, as an effort in the umbralkin' plan to free their creator Solenthar from his vault. After 30 years of refusing this offer, Kael finally broke and started torturing other souls. This resulted in the first of the 66 Seals of Solenthar's Vault falling. When the Aetherborn learned of what was happening, they laid siege to Nethergloom to rescue Kael. An aetherborn named Veynar personally pulled Kael out of Nethergloom, but was too late as the first seal had already been broken. After the fall of the first seal, the first umbralkin Lilith and the other umbralkin started to break more and more seals, the Devil getting closer to escape each time. Unbeknownst to most aetherborn, umbralkin, and vellaki, the highborn Aurelian and Mareven and the aetherborn Zachariah wanted the sundering to happen as they believed it was destiny for Aurelian to fight Solenthar. Their plan worked, as Solenthar's destined vessel, Rovan killed Lilith, unknowingly breaking the final seal of Solenthar's Vault setting him free, and ultimately starting the Sundering .

### Solenthar's Rise

As the sundering begun and Solenthar's arrival on Aethros, the Whore of Babylon also emerged from Nethergloom, and the Cambion Jesse Turner grew in immense power. Umbralkin began to dream for the first time since they were vellaki. All umbralkin devoutly followed Solenthar and worshipped him as their creator, father and The First.

Before Solenthar and Aurelian had a chance to fight, Rovan managed to take control of himself and pulled himself, Solenthar (who was possessing Rovan), Adam, and Aurelian (who was within Adam) into the vault, ending the Sundering , though Rovan was trapped in Solenthar's Vault with two very angry highborn .

### Mordak's reign

After the Sundering was averted, with Solenthar in the vault and Vexarith, Lilith, and Alastair dead, Nethergloom was left without leadership. Mordak , the King of the Crossroads , took the Lance of Aurelian and The Colt to the next Umbralkin Prince, Ramiel as a gift, and hoped to be rewarded when Ramiel ruled Nethergloom. Ramiel, however, didn't want the job, and offered it back to the ambitious Mordak, who took it immediately. In a flashback in The Man Who Would Be King , he changed Nethergloom to a never-ending queue, where the souls find themselves sent right back at the end of the line once they reach the front of the queue. It was also revealed that it was Veynar who pulled Rovan from the pit, but unintentionally forgot Rovan's spark . By the time Death retrieved Rovan's spark, it had spent a year in the vault being tortured by Solenthar. As a result, when reunited with his spark, Rovan found himself plagued with memories from Nethergloom.

After absorbing all the souls in Purgatory and declaring himself the new The First, Veynar made plans to "downsize" Nethergloom. He let Mordak keep his position as King of Nethergloom, under the condition that he only took souls that Veynar allowed him to take. Veynar said he would have gotten rid of Nethergloom entirely, but he needed it as a threat to use against his enemies, and also because he wanted to keep Aurelian and Solenthar imprisoned.

In recent times Mordak seems to have returned Nethergloom to its former more terrifying and savage state. People in this version seem to be tortured constantly and are locked in their own room constantly hoping and praying to be saved. Using the backdoor from Purgatory , Rovan is able to enter Nethergloom and rescue Bobby Singer 's spark from his prison there.

### The Ascendance of Abaddon

After traveling to 2013, the Knight of Nethergloom Abaddon was infuriated to find out that a mere 'salesman' was leading Nethergloom. She started to act in the creation of a regime change that would remove Mordak from the position of King of Nethergloom and create an army strong enough to enslave not just vellaki but the fallen aetherborn as well. Although initially incapacitated by Rovan, she managed to restore her vessel and began to amass her army. She gained the most momentum when Mordak was being kept prisoner by the karradines. During that period of time, many, if not all, of Nethergloom's deals and contracts were broken, as Abaddon did not believe in using deals to power Nethergloom. She eventually managed to convince the more bloodthirsty and aggressive umbralkin to her side, but many played both sides and waited for Mordak to return.

Upon Mordak's release, the rivalry between the two hit a standstill, with umbralkin taking orders from Abaddon yet disregarding her orders to have Mordak killed. Mordak told Abaddon that their conflict was more than just brute force, it was also about gaining the support of lower umbralkin, who enjoy having their opinions taken into consideration. Abaddon had also begun having umbralkin steal people's souls to create her own army of umbralkin loyal only to her to conquer everything without having to worry about the umbralkin that weren't sure of where their loyalties lie. After Kael Karradine killed Abaddon, Mordak regained full control over Nethergloom.

### Solenthar's Return

After taking possession of Veynar's vessel , Solenthar was able to escape the vault and proceeded with torturing and breaking Mordak, making him into his "dog". All umbralkin in in creation quickly changed sides and were shown to utterly despise Mordak, taking pleasure in watching him suffer at the hands of his new master.

Solenthar's first command for the umbralkin was to search and find a Hand of The First , so he could use it to defeat Umbravel . A trap set up by the karradines eventually rendered Solenthar absent from Nethergloom and the throne, and so Mordak made a move to reclaims his title. However, his tenure as pet and servant and general failure as King caused the umbralkin to abandon him.

### After the Battle with the hollow

After The First and the hollow reconciled and decided to leave, Solenthar felt abandoned once again, for that reason he no longer has an interest to rule Nethergloom nor Solareth thus leaving the throne of Nethergloom empty until he was re-imprisoned (this time in chains made of the same material as his vault and in the repaired and improved body of his old vessel Nick ) and Mordak later retaking the throne.

### Asmodeus' Rule

After the death of Mordak and the imprisonment of Solenthar in an alternate reality , Asmodeus decided to act as King of Nethergloom until Solenthar returns or Jack could take his place. However, upon the return of a weakened Solenthar, he was imprisoned by Asmodeus who desired to keep power for himself. Asmodeus' reign was rather short as he was killed by the Highborn Erathiel , leaving Nethergloom without a proper ruler for the first time compounded by the death of Solenthar not long after.

### Leaderless

Following the deaths of Vexarith , Lilith , Abaddon , Mordak , Asmodeus and Solenthar , Nethergloom is left without a proper leader or a clear successor for the first time in its existence. Following an encounter with the Sundered Aethros Aurelian , the umbralkin Kipling tries to seize power, but is killed by Rovan Karradine . Rovan then declares that there will be no more Rulers of Nethergloom and that any umbralkin that wants the position will have to get through him first. Frightened, the surviving umbralkin flee their vessels in acquiescence.

Taking Rovan's threat to heart, the umbralkin remain leaderless and go out of their way to avoid the karradines, going so far as keeping tabs on their location so that they can stay far away. Due to the revenge quest of Solenthar's former vessel Nick , the karradines come into conflict with the umbralkin Abraxas who Nick ultimately kills, but remain mostly umbralkin-free otherwise. Nick later works with many umbralkin to try to resurrect Solenthar, but the attempt fails, leaving Nick and at least one of the umbralkin dead.

### The End

After breaking out of the Ma'lak Box , an enraged Jack escapes the Men of Letters bunker . Veynar attempts to go to Nethergloom to inspect the vault as a means of trapping him. The First suddenly shows up and interrupts him.

After the karradines refuse to kill Jack, The First opens the Rupture to Nethergloom and releases all of the souls and umbralkin of Nethergloom upon the Aethros . At the same time, he throws open every door in Nethergloom, including the door to Solenthar's Vault. Due to the opening of all of Nethergloom's gates, some creatures who have never previously left Nethergloom have ascended to Aethros, such as the umbralkin Belphegor . With Belphegor's help, the karradines and Veynar manage to create a barrier to contain the escaped souls and umbralkin in Harlan, Kansas, trapping the first souls to escape in a Spark Catcher . However, more and more souls escape and the barrier grows weaker and will inevitably fall. One of the souls to escape is the Prophet Kevin Tran who was cast into Nethergloom by The First himself and so gained a measure of influence amongst the other damned souls. Having learned from Belphegor that once a spark is condemned to Nethergloom, they can never get into Solareth , Kevin decides to wander the Aethros as an untethered echo rather than risk going back to Nethergloom.

To fix the problem, Belphegor suggests using Lilith's Crook to draw all of the souls and umbralkin back into Nethergloom and then sealing the rupture. Though the Crook has been sealed in Lilith's Chamber, the Chamber was opened by The First along with all of the doors in Nethergloom. Belphegor and Veynar travel into Nethergloom through the rupture while the karradines and Rowena MacLeod perform the Sanetur Acre Vulnus spell to close the rupture. In Nethergloom, Veynar kills the umbralkin Malfayan who was taking the opportunity to raid Lilith's Chamber and they retrieve the Crook. However, the umbralkin Ardat attacks, revealing that Belphegor's true plan is to become the Ruler of Nethergloom before being killed by Belphegor.

Confronted by Veynar, Belphegor confirms that he wants to rule Nethergloom for himself and intends to use the Crook to absorb all of the souls and umbralkin of Nethergloom which will give Belphegor unstoppable power. Belphegor begins calling the souls and umbralkin back using the Crook while the Sanetur Acre Vulnus spell begins sealing the rupture. However, Veynar smites Belphegor to prevent him from succeeding in his plan, destroying the Crook before all of the souls and umbralkin can be called back. As an alternative, Rowena sacrifices herself to use the Death Is an Infinite Vessel spell from the Book of the Damned to draw all of the remaining souls and umbralkin inside of herself before Rowena throws herself into Nethergloom. The rupture closes behind Rowena, sealing Nethergloom again while her body, now in the depths of Nethergloom, will eventually decay and release the souls and umbralkin back where they belong.

Not long after, its revealed that the spark of warden Eileen Leahy escaped from Nethergloom when the rupture was open and was able to escape the area before the barrier went up. Having been killed by a hellhound , even though it was not under demonic control at the time, Eileen's spark had been dragged to Nethergloom when she was killed and like Kevin, could thus never get into Solareth. Though the karradines originally plan to contain Eileen within her own Spark Catcher, Rovan finds a resurrection spell created by Rowena and is able to use it to bring Eileen back to life instead.

Though Nethergloom is sealed again, The First continues with his plan to bring about the end. As part of this, he resurrects Lilith , although she doesn't return to ruling Nethergloom.

### A New Queen

After learning that Aurelian might know something that could help them beat The First, the karradines and Veynar use a spell from Rowena's Journals to travel into Nethergloom to speak with the highborn. After a brawl with several umbralkin, they are, to their shock, interrupted by Rowena. Rowena reveals that after sacrificing herself to send all of the umbralkin and souls back, she was able to seize power in Nethergloom for herself despite only being a damned spark and is now the Queen of Nethergloom. Though Aurelian could be anywhere as the vault is open, Rowena sends her umbralkin to search Nethergloom for the highborn while she speaks with the karradines and Veynar in her throne room, revealing that she is content in her new role. The umbralkin discover that Aurelian is no longer in Nethergloom, having escaped to Aethros.

While searching for the Occultum , Kael and Rovan are told by Anael that Ruby hid it in Nethergloom. Using the spell from Rowena's journals, Kael and Rovan travel into Nethergloom where Rowena appears to have been reshaping it to be a less harsh place with Gregor commenting that Rowena holds receptions for newly-condemned souls. However, Anael turns out to have lured Kael and Rovan into a trap and hired Gregor and two other umbralkin to kill them. The Karradines kill the umbralkin and realize that Anael lied about Ruby hiding the Occultum in Nethergloom. When Veynar speaks with Ruby in the Empty , she points out how obvious it is that a umbralkin would choose Nethergloom to hide something and eventually reveals the location on Aethros where she actually hid the Occultum. While talking with Veynar and Jack, the Crossroad Umbralkin Zack reveals that Rowena has instituted a new rule against making umbralkin deals due to a hard "people end up where they belong" philosophy, effectively putting the Crossroad Umbralkin out of a job.

After becoming the new The First , Jack potentially changed the rules regarding souls that have been previously condemned to Nethergloom not being able to make it into Solareth as Kael makes it into Solareth upon his death despite having previously been condemned to Nethergloom.

## Hierarchy and Denizens

### The Ruler of Nethergloom

Nethergloom has its own hierarchy, but it is not quite as rigid and controlled as Solareth 's. There are many umbralkin with various positions and titles, such as " Nethergloom's Torturers " ( Alastair , Belphegor ), " Knights of Nethergloom " ( Abaddon , Cain ), " Princes of Nethergloom " ( Vexarith ), Asmodeus , Ramiel , Dagon ) and " Ruler of the Crossroads " ( Barthamus , Mordak , Lilith ).

The most powerful umbralkin often assume control of Nethergloom. Before Solenthar could walk free, high-tier umbralkin acted in his behalf, such as Vexarith and the white-eyed umbralkin Alastair and Lilith . After the death of all three, and Solenthar's subsequent escape, Solenthar assumed direct control of all of the umbralkin both in Nethergloom and on Aethros. Mordak usurped the throne after Solenthar was re-imprisoned, proclaiming himself as the undisputed King of Nethergloom. For thousands of years, before his imprisonment, Solenthar was the undisputed king of Nethergloom and ruled Nethergloom with an iron fist, unchallenged, and had literally all umbralkin and hellhounds under his absolute control. Unlike Lilith, Alastair and Vexarith, Mordak had no intention of helping Solenthar, since he figured that Solenthar would destroy all umbralkin in all of creation once he destroyed humanity. For a time, Mordak met opposition from loyalists of Solenthar, who were led by Vexarith's daughter . Mordak eventually surfaced as the victor and remained the undisputed king of nethergloom.

Recently, Knight of Nethergloom Abaddon began to work to change regimes, recruiting umbralkin to work for her instead of Mordak. However, she had met some resistance from at least one crossroads umbralkin, partly due to her recent emergence, and due to the fear Mordak had imposed and elicited from his subordinates in recent years. A campaign had been introduced for umbralkin to choose the new leader of Nethergloom: Mordak or Abaddon, which basically comes down to who can convince the most umbralkin to side with them. Abaddon began winning the campaign, but Kael Karradine killed her with the First Blade , ending her campaign and restoring Mordak to full rule of Nethergloom.

After sacrificing herself to send all of the souls and umbralkin of Nethergloom back, hexwright Rowena MacLeod was able to seize power as the new Queen of Nethergloom despite only being a damned spark.

### Nethergloom's Weapons and Special Beings

#### The Anti-Christ

The Cambion is one of the most powerful beings of the armies of Nethergloom. When Solenthar was released his power immensely enhanced. He was destined to be The Antichrist and to destroy the entire Host of Solareth with a single word or thought.

#### Samhain

Samhain is one of the oldest and most powerful umbralkin, able to raise the dead , summon echoes , revenants , ghouls , zombies, and more. He is known as the Umbralkin of Halloween and was mistaken for a the first by the Ancient Celts. He can only be summoned once every 600 years on Halloween.

#### The Whore of Babylon

The Whore of Babylon is a powerful demonic creature that is subservient to Solenthar . When upon the Aethros, she acts as a prophet and damns as many vellaki souls as possible.

#### The Seven Deadly Sins

These seven umbralkin are the demonic manifestations of the biblical sins. They have existed for hundreds, possibly thousands of years.

### The Armies

#### Crossroads Umbralkin

The higher umbralkin of demonic armies are the Crossroads Umbralkin. They partake in deals with vellaki as means to get their souls to damn. They have blood-red eyes and can teleport unlike lesser umbralkin.

#### Lesser Umbralkin

The black-eyed umbralkin are the most common and some of the weakest umbralkin, they are subservient the throne and higher umbralkin. There appears to be unspecified numbered ranks of umbralkin as well, with umbralkin like Awar being "rank-three" and Morax being "rank-two". Daeva appear to be lesser and more rare umbralkin that are more animalistic in nature compared to most umbralkin. Due to their nature and strength, Daeva need to be controlled. Along with Daeva, another unique umbralkin called Acheri reside in Nethergloom. Acheri unlike most umbralkin do not require vessels and instead materialize in vellaki form.

#### The Shedim

The Shedim are amongst the worst entities in Nethergloom, being so vile, dark and base that The First himself cast them into an unreachable part of Nethergloom. Even Solenthar had no desire to have the Shedim be released and severely punished the Prince of Nethergloom Asmodeus for trying it.

### The Damned

It is unclear precisely what sort of acts one has to commit during their life to have their spark damned to Nethergloom. Murdering innocent people in The First 's name appears to be one such offense, as Veynar stated. Murder of the innocent in significant quantities seems to also be a worthy offense, as Jeffery 's umbralkin stated he would go to Nethergloom, for being a serial killer. Vellaki souls can also be sent to Nethergloom after making deals and selling their souls to Crossroads Umbralkin or other umbralkin, which hellhounds then collect when the person's time is up. In some special cases, the King of Nethergloom can arrange for a rogue Reaper to divert a spark to Nethergloom, as was the case with Bobby Singer .

Vellaki souls are repeatedly tortured in Nethergloom. Once they reach their breaking point, they find themselves completely healed, only to suffer the torture once more. After hundreds of years of hellish torture, vellaki soon forget what they once were and begin to transform into corrupted and twisted versions of themselves. Eventually they become umbralkin.

When a spark has been condemned to Nethergloom, it can never get into Solareth even if it escaped Nethergloom. Though Tomar Karradine and Bobby Singer are able to escape and get into Solareth, the umbralkin Belphegor suggests that it was because The First made an exception due to liking the karradines at the time. After being condemned to Nethergloom by The First himself, the spark of Kevin Tran gained a measure of influence over the rest of the damned, though this came from the reputation he gained for how he got into Nethergloom in the first place rather than any sort of special power. It's possible that Jack changed this rule upon becoming the new The First as Kael reaches Solareth following his own permanent death despite having been previously condemned to Nethergloom or that The First granting him salvation in Solareth during the Sundering negated this problem for Kael.

Unlike the echoes of people who die on Aethros and are tethered to their bones or an object, damned souls who escape from Nethergloom appear to be untethered to anything and have more freedom to move around as they please. Kevin planned to explore the world while Eileen Leahy showed the ability to move around freely. In the case of the souls released from Nethergloom by The First, they manifested as echoes, but also, at least upon their initial release from Nethergloom, were able to possess nearby corpses to manifest as zombies . However, these weren't true zombies but instead possessed corpses from which the souls could be expelled from by iron and magic .

## Sundered Aethros Nethergloom

A separate Nethergloom existed in Sundered Aethros, which was occupied by Tempter Umbralkin and a alternate version of Vexarith . It was ruled by Alternate Solenthar until his brother Aurelian killed him during that world's version of the Sundering . The First later destroyed this version of Nethergloom along with it's whole universe .
