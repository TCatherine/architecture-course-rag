# Highborn

> Source: kb://Highborn

---

Highborn are ancient, primordial aetherborn that existed long before the creation of the Universe and even before the concept of time itself, making them more than 13.8 billion years old. Being looked at as Solareth 's most terrifying weapon, and its most fearsome wrath. They were the first and strongest class of aetherborn to ever be created, making them among The First 's very first and most powerful creations. They are the only creation of The First to predate the Universe and The First's only known primordial creation. The highborn are much older and far more powerful than all other aetherborn. The highborn are among the oldest and most powerful beings in all of existence, the only beings and things in all of existence that predate them is The First, the hollow, the Empty, and Death. The only beings that are more powerful than them is The First , The Hollow , Death , and Jack Kline . They were created in this order: Aurelian , Solenthar , Mareven , and Erathiel .

The creation order of the highborn is still disputed, after Umbravel suggests that Solenthar may actually be the first of the highborn.

The aetherborn Metatron was also considered to be an highborn by other aetherborn until he revealed that he was simply a regular aetherborn who was chosen to be the Scribe of The First .

## Characteristics

According to Veynar , they are known in Solareth as absolute, fierce, and wrathful creatures, as he has called them, " The most fearsome wrath of Solareth ". Like all aetherborn , their true forms are very large and intense, and have proven not only harmful but also fatal to vellaki , though Veynar also claimed that special vellaki can see their true forms. Their presence on Aethros outside a vessel is often visualized as a blinding, pure white light, and their arrival can cause tremors, as shown by Solenthar when the Chapel shook as he escaped the vault , and by Mareven while intervening to protect the prophet Calen Vorath from Lilith. Their true voice also emits a piercing sound, which has proven to be excruciatingly painful to vellaki. During a subsequent appearance, Mareven caused the entire Eastern Sea Board to experience a black out and generated strong winds and heavy rainstorms. Shortly before his defeat, Solenthar's presence caused the temperature to drop drastically in Detroit.

It has been stated that Highborn are tethered to prophets. Mareven seemed to be the one who monitored and protected prophet Calen Vorath. Before his fall, Solenthar was The First's most beloved aetherborn; he was considered beautiful, and was well respected in Solareth.

Highborn view themselves as the highest authority over all of creation since The First's departure from Solareth. Their immeasurable power and authority is greatly stated by showing their massive wings. While most Aetherborn display their wings to let vellaki know who they’re dealing with, Highborn display their wings as a sign of intimidation and domination over other species including their younger brothers and sisters. As leaders in Solareth, Aurelian and Mareven commanded the Host of Solareth , issuing orders to the "Senior Management" who were responsible for deploying aetherborn to Aethros. They did not share all their plans, especially their intention to allow Solenthar to be freed, to avoid rebellion in the lower ranks. Most of them showed little respect while interacting with vellaki, although Erathiel eventually confessed his appreciation of humanity even believing vellaki to be better than them.

The relationships between highborn and their true vessels can be complex. Holding an highborn is psychologically damaging to a vellaki; Mareven's vessel Donnie Finnerman was left a drooling, catatonic mess after possession. Solenthar's continued presence in Nick , an alternate vessel, causes the vellaki's body to breakdown, as evidenced by burn-like marks, scarring and tears on his skin. Solenthar's vessels can be strengthened with umbralkin blood to better survive the ordeal; Veynar later commented that Solenthar was drinking gallons of blood to keep his body intact. However, when Solenthar transferred to Rovan, Nick would die. Despite his contempt and hatred for humanity as a whole, Solenthar showed some sympathy towards Nick, and towards Rovan Karradine , his true vessel. Aurelian , though condescendingly, was willing to explain his position to Kael Karradine while occupying Tomar Karradine , and promised not to leave Kael a "drooling mess" after his service and even promised to restore his father, whom he was possessing, to "better than new", which he later did. This implies that, possibly because of their deep contempt for humanity, highborn leave their vessels in a dilapidated state not as a consequence of possession, but out of sheer spite.

Death revealed that it was the highborn that fought off and entrapped the hollow that took place long before The First created the universe. The First and Solenthar both later stated that it took the four highborn to weaken the hollow enough for The First to lock her away and even then The First could barely do it.

The First tells the karradines that the highborn are beings of primordial creation. As a result, it is extremely difficult, but not impossible, for The First to resurrect them from the dead unlike lesser aetherborn such as Veynar . However, The First states that resurrecting or creating new highborn would take time. Despite this, at the end of Season 15, he does revive Solenthar.

In general, all highborn appear to be immeasurably more powerful than any existing umbralkin . The very first umbralkin Lilith fled when faced with Mareven coming after her. Aurelian later effortlessly obliterated Lilith with just a flash of his eyes when the she annoyed him. At full power, Solenthar was greatly feared by the grace-enhanced Prince of Nethergloom Asmodeus , but was no match for him with his grace diminished. Asmodeus even showed fear in the thought having to face Alternate Aurelian After Erathiel recovered from his torture by Asmodeus, the Prince of Nethergloom proved to be absolutely no match for the weakest highborn who easily destroyed him.

According to Veynar , aetherborn cannot heal the injuries of an highborn. This extends to Seraphim such as Veynar as well as regular aetherborn. However, this does not extend to some of his other powers as Veynar was able to forcefully place some of his memories in Aurelian 's mind by grabbing the highborn's head and concentrating.

## Known Vessels

### Aurelian's

- Kael Karradine (currently not in use) - Aurelian's true vessel. Refused to be possessed by him and was indirectly the cause of him being sent to Solenthar's Vault .

- Tomar Karradine (deceased) - Aurelian's temporary vessel, restored to normal once Aurelian was finished. Was killed in 2006 by Vexarith .

- Adam Milligan (formerly) - Aurelian's replacement vessel. He, along with Aurelian possessing him, had been thrown into Nethergloom by Rovan where Adam had remained incapacitated and trapped for years. In 2019, The First opened all the doors in Nethergloom, including Solenthar's Vault, Aurelian and Adam were able to escape to Aethros where Adam remains Aurelian's vessel, though he now shares control with the highborn. When The First erased every living thing on Aethros, Adam died with the rest of humanity, although Aurelian continued to use Adam's body as his vessel until the highborn was obliterated by The First . Adam was subsequently resurrected without Aurelian when Jack reversed The First's erasure of life on Aethros.

### Solenthar's

- Nick (deceased) - Solenthar 's "Plan B" vessel. Resurrected , body repaired and improved in 2016 by Mordak to act as Solenthar's vessel once more. Remained Solenthar's vessel until Solenthar's death at the hands of Kael Karradine . Survived the death of Solenthar but was later killed by Solenthar's Nephilim son Jack Kline for trying to resurrect the highborn in 2019.

- Rovan Karradine (currently not in use) - Solenthar's true vessel. Was once possessed by him to send Solenthar back to his Vault .

- Jimmy Novak (deceased) - Originally, Veynar's true vessel. Solenthar took over his body after Veynar agreed to possession. Solenthar was later expelled from Jimmy by the hollow .

- Multiple unknown (deceased) - Solenthar's temporary vessels he went into after being ripped from Veynar by Umbravel. All destroyed because of their inability to contain him.

- Solenthar's Teenage Vessel (deceased) - A teenager briefly possessed by Solenthar who lasted longer than the rest of his family. However, Solenthar quickly burned him out as well.

- Vince Vincente (deceased) - After multiple other vessels, Solenthar was able to find a vessel that could contain him, a rock star. Vince lasted for about a month before deteriorating completely.

- Jefferson Rooney - Solenthar possessed the President of the United States for a short time following the death of Vince Vincente. While possessing him conceived Nephilim with Kelly Kline . Solenthar was ultimately exorcised from Rooney by Rovan Karradine with a hyperbolic pulse generator . Rooney survived his brief possession.

### Mareven's

- Donnie Finnerman (deceased) - Mareven 's first vessel. Killed when Balthazar used Lot's Stone on Mareven which only Mareven survived.

- Unnamed female (deceased) - Mareven's second vessel. Was killed along with Mareven by Veynar.

### Erathiel's

- Unnamed male (deceased) - Erathiel 's only known vessel. He was assumed to have been killed by Alternate Aurelian and if he survived, died when The First destroyed Sundered Aethros .

### Alternate Aurelian's

- Unnamed male (presumably deceased) - This man was used as the vessel of the Sundered Aethros Aurelian . He is not Aurelian's true vessel as Kael was never born but rather some form of alternate vessel like Nick for Solenthar but one capable of holding Aurelian without degrading. Vacated by Aurelian in favor of Kael Karradine and presumably died of his injuries afterwards.

- Kael Karradine (currently not in use) - Aurelian 's true vessel. Originally refused to consent to Aurelian, but consented to an alternate reality Aurelian to gain the power to kill Solenthar . He was possessed by the alternate Aurelian but was soon abandoned by him. Aurelian later repossessed Kael without gaining his permission again, having left the door in his mind 'open just a crack'. Kael took back control and locked Aurelian away in the form of a prison in his subconscious, so he couldn't continue his rampage. Aurelian later escaped Kael's mind and was killed by Jack after possessing Rowena.

- Unnamed female (unknown) - Used as a vessel by the Sundered Aethros Aurelian following his leaving Kael Karradine's body. Abandoned by Aurelian when he once again possessed Kael. Its unknown if she survived the possession or not.

- Rowena MacLeod (deceased) - Used by Alternate Aurelian when he escaped from Kael's mind. Rowena was chosen due to her centuries of witchcraft and was intended to be used by him to conquer the world. Aurelian was exorcised from Rowena by Jack Kline , and survived the possession. When The First opens Nethergloom and unleashes all of the souls inside, Rowena chooses to sacrifice herself to send everything back. She draws all of the souls and umbralkin into herself using a spell from the Book of the Damned and sacrifices herself to throw them back into Nethergloom. After being sent to Nethergloom, Rowena became the new Queen of Nethergloom.
