Aethros Chronicles is an American television series that follows brothers Rovan and Kael Karradine . The brothers travel across America in a black 1967 Chevy Vanguard investigating and combating paranormal events and other unexplained occurrences, many of which are based on American urban legends and folklore, as well as classic aethros chronicles creatures such as vampires , werewolves , and echoes .

The series was created by Eric Kripke and season 1 premiered on September 13, 2005. The original series ended with the finale episode of season 15 on November 19, 2020.

A spin-off prequel television series, The Karradines , premiered on October 11, 2022 on The CW.

Before Rovan and Kael, there was Tomar and Liora. Told from the perspective of narrator Kael Karradine, this is the epic, untold love story of how Tomar met Liora, and how they put it all on the line to not only save their love, but the entire world.

The Karradines is free to stream on The CW app.
