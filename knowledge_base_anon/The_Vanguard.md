# The Vanguard

> Source: kb://The_Vanguard

---

The Vanguard is Kael Karradine 's trademark black 1967 Aurorian Vanguard . It was passed down to him by his father , who bought it in 1973 after a future Kael convinces him to buy it over a 1964 VW Van in the episode " In the Beginning ". According to Kael, the car has a 327 c.i. engine and a four barrel carburetor. The car originally bore a Sedgwick County, Kansas license plate—though the karradines’ hometown of Lawrence is in Douglas County—with the number KAZ 2Y5, a nod to Kansas and 2005, the year the series premiered. In Season 2 , after Rovan and Kael escaped from prison, Kael noted the need to replace the old plates, and the Vanguard was later seen with Ohio plates (CNK 80Q3) for the remainder of the series.

The trunk holds an arsenal of weapons that are modified and crafted to aid the karradines in their hunting activities. Kael refers to the Vanguard affectionately as " Baby ". At the end of the series, the Vanguard is retired, covered with a sheet in Rovan's garage. After Rovan's death, the fate of the Vanguard is uncertain, though it's possible Rovan might've left it to his son Kael Karradine II .

A celestial version of the Vanguard, complete with its original license plates, coexists in Solareth while Rovan owns the earthly version. This celestial Vanguard serves as Kael's means of travel across the multiverse and was also employed by him in the Monster Club Universe . As it does not originate from that Aethros, it is uniquely capable of killing Joan Hopkins and the Akrida .

## Her Life So Far...

### Aethros Chronicles

The car has been prominently featured on the series, beginning with the teaser of the pilot which shows Tomar Karradine holding his two sons as he sits on the car and watches his house burn. The trunk is revealed to hold various weaponry to fight the aethros chronicles. The car is Kael's most prized possession, and he protects it with nearly the same ferocity with which he protects his family.

In " Pilot ", a echo possesses the Vanguard and tries to run Rovan and Kael over. Later, as Rovan is driving the Vanguard, the echo takes control of the car again to make him drive her home. Rovan drives the Vanguard through the front wall of her home and comes to a stop in the living room, where she is confronted by the echoes of her drowned children and destroyed.

In the season 1 finale , a truck driven by a possessed trucker smashes into the Vanguard, causing serious damage to her and her occupants. Rovan and Bobby Singer later have the Vanguard towed back to Bobby's where Rovan refuses to scrap it, despite Bobby insisting it can't be salvaged. Kael later restored the Vanguard himself shortly before attacking the car's rear with a crowbar due to the pressure from his dying father's secret about Rovan.

Rovan inherits the car following Kael's death in the third season finale . The car is later returned to Kael in Season 4 , who isn't impressed that Rovan added an iPod jack to the car. He removes it and the jack is never seen again. It was shortly after Kael returned from Nethergloom, when he time traveled back to 1973 and convinced a young Tomar Karradine to buy the Vanguard. Later when Tomar proposes to Liora he does so in the front seat.

In order to destroy a echo in " Yellow Fever ", Rovan and Bobby attach an iron chain to the Vanguard and wrap it around the echo's neck. Once the chain is wrapped around his neck, Bobby drives down a road, dragging the echo behind the Vanguard until his spirit was destroyed. The car had some minor damages here and there throughout various episodes and has even had aetherborn and umbralkin (once at the same time) in the back seat.

In Season 5 , while trapping Rovan and Kael in realities based on various TV shows, Erathiel transforms Rovan into the Vanguard (a knockoff from the TV series Knight Rider ). It's while driving this that Kael and Rovan finally realize that Erathiel is actually an aetherborn and not a Trickster.

In the season 5 finale , the history of the Vanguard is recounted by Calen Vorath . It's the Vanguard and all of its memories that helps Rovan push Solenthar back and jump into the vault .

A year after Kael has stopped hunting in Season 6 , the Vanguard is under a tarp in the garage, albeit still with a trunk full of weapons. When Kael returns to hunting, he brings the Vanguard out of retirement and removes the tarp, near the end of " Two and a Half Men ".

The Vanguard becomes possessed by a echo once again in " Mannequin 3: The Reckoning " with Kael managing to maneuver it into a wall. At the end of the episode Kael is repairing the damage at Bobby's Salvage Yard.

In an alternate timeline in which the Titanic never sank, Chevy Impalas do not exist. Instead, Kael drives a '67 Ford Mustang. When the Titanic is "re-sunk", and Rovan and Kael reappear in their own reality, they wake up in the Vanguard.

The Vanguard is further damaged when Mordak and his umbralkin attack Veynar . While Kael and Bobby take refuge in the car, it's flipped onto its roof and has all its windows blown out by the umbralkin onslaught. The Vanguard doesn't stay damaged for long. Soon after Veynar leaves them in " Meet the New Boss ", Kael goes to fix the Vanguard due to it being the only thing he can do. With the help of Rovan and Bobby, the Vanguard is fully repaired.

Due to the Drath'enir cloning them and driving an identical Vanguard, they had to hide the Vanguard in order not to be detected. Kael has driven several cars in place of the Vanguard, many of which he has shown discontent with.

In " Survival of the Fittest ", Kael asks Veynar for a favor as he will not fight. He asks him to take him to his beloved Vanguard where they discuss the situation and Veynar finally agrees to help kill Dick Roman . When asked what the plan is, he tells Veynar that as Dick knows they're coming, they're going to announce themselves "big." The next morning, the Vanguard crashes through SucroCorp 's front gate and ends up in its giant glass sign, having apparently been used to reach the building by the group. However, when the guards come out, Meg is revealed to be the driver, Kael having used the Vanguard's arrival as a distraction so he, Rovan and Veynar can sneak in. The distraction works, but the Vanguard is slightly damaged in her crash and the driver's side window is shot out by the Drath'enir guards. After Meg dispatches them, she leaves, abandoning the Vanguard in the sign. It is later recovered by Rovan after Dick is killed and Kael and Veynar disappear.

After Kael's return from Purgatory , he found that the Vanguard had been fully repaired after Rovan told him about the previous year, though no new modifications were done to it, which made Kael happy, considering that the last time Kael went away for a year, Rovan had made several modern additions to the car. When Kael smells dog in the car, Rovan claims not to know what he's talking about but later admits he hit a dog with the Vanguard which caused him to meet Amelia Richardson . Afterwards, he took the dog around in the car, annoying Kael as he doesn't like dogs and doesn't want them in his precious car. As the Drath'enir are gone, Rovan and Kael return to using the Vanguard instead of stolen cars.

When the Bunker 's garage is found, Kael stores the Vanguard there. After taking full control of Rovan, Gadreel uses the Vanguard to get around as he can no longer teleport, but it helps Kael, Veynar and Mordak find him as he runs a red light on his way to find Abner and kill him. After Gadreel is captured, Kael reclaims the car.

After retrieving the First Blade , Rovan and Kael return to the Vanguard to find it ransacked by umbralkin who are working for Abaddon . The wards on the Vanguard's trunk kept that safe, but Abaddon had "Be afraid, Your Queen" keyed into the side of the car in Enochian.

In " Out of the hollow, Into the Fire ", after the hollow is released it washes over the Vanguard, rocking the car when suddenly Kael disappears as the hollow pulls him out to speak to him. Rovan is knocked out and wakes to the Vanguard's horn going off but no significant damage otherwise.

In " Baby ", the entire episode takes place from the car's point of view. During a vision, Rovan sees a being appearing to him in the form of a young Tomar Karradine who drives the Vanguard in the dream while talking to Rovan. Kael later purposefully crashes the car, while Deputy Donelly is flung through it headfirst. The final fight takes place in the backseat and ends with Kael decapitating Donelly by slamming the left rear door on his head three times. Due to the conflict with the monsters, the Vanguard's front and rear windows are shattered, the being rear from Rovan's gunfire and though the Vanguard runs, it's hard to start the engine. At the end of the episode, Rovan calls the Vanguard their home as they drive away.

In " We Happy Few ", Rovan drives the Vanguard to The Hollow' Lair to rescue Solenthar . He is led there by Donatello Redfield , with Metatron coming along. Rovan departs with Donatello in the back and Solenthar in shotgun. However, the trio is intercepted by Umbravel on their way to the Bunker. Rovan tries to drive away, but Umbravel restrains them and prepares to kill. The First retrieves the entire vehicle just in time and dumps it unceremoniously in one of the rooms in the Bunker.

In " Keep Calm and Carry On ", Kael leads a resurrected Liora Karradine to the Vanguard which is in the bunker's garage. Liora recognizes the Vanguard as her husband's car and treats the Vanguard in a similar manner to how Kael treats the car. While examining the Vanguard, Liora stares into the backseat with a huge smile, presumably remembering fun times with Tomar in the backseat. Later, while driving, the Vanguard is rammed by Ms. Watt with an SUV, leaving a dent in the rear right quarter panel. The Vanguard is temporarily knocked out of commission by the hit but remains driveable and the karradines and Veynar drive off in it after killing Ms. Watt.

In " LOTUS ", the karradines are found by the Secret Service due to them recognizing the Vanguard with Rick Sanchez commenting on how recognizable the car is.

In " Regarding Kael ", Kael has a hard time remembering which key to use to start the engine after being hit by the memory hexbane . Later, hexwright Rowena MacLeod drives the car after Rovan is captured by the Loughlin Family . Rowena leaves the memory-less Kael in the front seat with a note explaining the situation and another for him to stay in the car. When Kael gets out, he finds notes directing him to his gun and the hexwright-killing bullets in the weapons locker.

In " Somewhere Between Solareth and Nethergloom ", Rovan is instructed by Kael to take care of the Vanguard while they hunt for the hellhound Ramsey . Unfortunately, Ramsey destroys the windshield and damages the roof when she tries to attack Rovan, who is driving the car. Although Ramsey is eventually killed, Kael becomes frustrated at what happened to the Vanguard and explains that this is why Rovan doesn't drive. Kael then takes over driving, commenting that he hopes the car will still run now.

In " The Future ", after Veynar's truck breaks down, he is forced to stay at a motel with Kelly Kline . When Kael and Rovan arrive with the Vanguard, they tend to take both Veynar and Kelly back to the Bunker. While they leave the two inside the Vanguard while they discuss things, Kelly drives off with the Vanguard towards the Heavenly Portal , forcing Kael to fix Veynar's truck and chase after them. Once Dagon has been killed at the Heavenly Portal, Veynar escapes with Kelly, using his truck once more.

When chased by aetherborn in " The Bad Place ", the karradines are forced to abandon the Vanguard in an abandoned shipyard in order to hide in an old ship. The Vanguard is left in the shipyard when the karradines are accidentally transported to the alternate universe known as the Bad Place . In " Wayward Sisters ", Jody Mills and Donna Hanscum are able to identify the correct location for the rift to the Bad Place by spotting the still-abandoned Vanguard. After being rescued, the karradines recover the car.

In " Various & Sundry Villains ", Jennie Plum complains when Kael shows up without the Vanguard as she wanted it for herself.

In " Scoobynatural ", after being transported into an alternate universe , Rovan and Kael are shocked to find the Vanguard with them, Rovan then speculates it was transported with them due to Kael having the keys on him.

In " Unhuman Nature ", after learning that Jack Kline is dying, Kael offers to teach him how to drive. Kael later has Jack drive on an empty stretch of road and the two enjoy simply cruising together.

Rovan inherits the car following Kael's death in the series finale . The Vanguard remained with him until his old age. It is not known where the car went after Rovan's death, but it may have been inherited by his son .

In Solareth, Kael finds the car waiting for him outside of Harvelle's Roadhouse bearing the original KAZ 2Y5 license plates. After talking with Bobby Singer , Kael goes on a drive through Solareth, eventually meeting up with Rovan on a bridge after his own death.

### " The Karradines "

In " Pilot ", Kael is seen leaning against the Vanguard in an unidentified location as he writes in his journal and provides narration. Additionally, the Vanguard features its original KAZ 2Y5 license plates instead of the CNK 80Q3 plates it has had since " What Is and What Should Never Be ." Furthermore, Kael's Amulet is prominently displayed, hanging from the rearview mirror.

In " Hang on to Your Life ", Tomar notices a man climbing into a car in the background of one of Samuel's pictures. Tomar recognizes the man as the one who had given him Henry's letter. The man is revealed to be Kael, alive and well, with the Vanguard in 1972.

In " Cast Your Fate to the Wind ", Tomar and Liora both compliment the Vanguard while studying the picture of Kael for clues as to his identity.

In " Suspicious Minds ", Kael is standing near a fence with one hand braced against the Vanguard in Kyle Reed 's picture.

In " Hey, That's No Way to Say Goodbye ", Joan Hopkins reveals that she threw both Kael and the Vanguard into a portal from which there is no return. Using the Ostium , the Monster Club tries to bring Kael back, but they only succeed in bringing back the Vanguard. The group realizes that as the car is not of their Aethros, it can harm Joan. As Joan prepares to open her portal, Liora hits her with the Vanguard, killing Joan, but passing through her portal afterwards.

Moments later, the portal reopens and Kael drives the Vanguard out with Liora in the passenger seat. Kael reveals that the car kept both him and Liora safe and he was stuck in the world between worlds so he stayed near the portal. When it opened and the Vanguard came through, Kael hopped in and took the wheel. Kael explains that after his death, while driving through Solareth , he took a detour through the Multiverse in search of a world where his family got a happy ending and discovered the threat of the Akrida, intervening against Jack Kline 's rules against direct interference. After Kael says goodbye to his parents, he, Bobby, Jack and the Vanguard all vanish in a flash of light.

## Kael and The Vanguard

Kael and the Vanguard have had a long, loving history together. Kael received the Vanguard as a gift for his eighteenth birthday. Ever since then, he has cared for it and even fixed it up when it was wrecked in the car accident at the end of season 1. In the Season 2 episode Bloodlust , the Vanguard is back on the road; this episode marks the first time Kael refers to the car as "she" and "Baby". This becomes a recurring theme through the rest of the series. Though he sometimes reacts with anger towards the car, he loves it very much, often talking to it as if it was a vellaki being and acting possessive towards it.

When Bela tows the car away, he is very upset. Later, when he is forced to hide the car, he is, again, very upset about this fact. Kael was going to give Bobby the Vanguard while preparing to surrender to Aurelian. When Rovan fails to stop Ramsey from damaging the car, Kael is furious and doesn't allow Rovan to drive it again.
